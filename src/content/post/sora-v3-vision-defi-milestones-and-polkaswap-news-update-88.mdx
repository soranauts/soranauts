## 2 · SORA v2 – polishing the on-ramp 💳

### 2.1 Kusama parachain & bridge  
Scarce core-time left a handful of KSM transfers in limbo. Engineers are patch-testing the runtime for **[JAM compatibility](https://wiki.polkadot.network/learn/learn-jam-chain/)** and hoarding core-time before auctions reopen on **21 July**. Expect normal bridge flow shortly after.

### 2.2 Wallet & SORA Card
- **Partner wallets** – white-label builds for gaming guilds and remittance corridors are already in closed QA.  
- **Card features** – swap BTC, ETH, USDC straight to EUR (and back) **inside the app**; SEPA IBANs + Apple/Google Pay support included.  
- **Road-map tease** – direct XOR ↔ fiat swaps are pencilled in for later this year once compliance reviews wrap.

<a id="why-this-matters"></a>
## 5 · Why this matters

Legacy rails leave millions stranded in inflation or conflict zones. SORA’s thesis is simple:

1. **Non-debt, supranational currency (XOR/KUSD)**  
2. **Predictable fees + fast finality**  
3. **Bridges to real-world CBDCs and community micro-economies**

If it works, a farmer in PNG or a freelancer in Lagos can transact globally—no gatekeepers, no 15 % remittance fee.
