### Revolutionary Tokenomics

The new dual token model with XOR and vXOR is turning heads. By stopping [TBCD token](https://www.soranauts.com/soras-token-bonding-curve-dollar-tbcd-explained) minting, XOR becomes deflationary. Builders get tokens as pay and can use them as collateral for Kensetsu XOR.

This setup gives DeFi a boost and makes the SORA network more stable.

The model follows the ΔMV=ΔPY rule for economic growth. It's in line with the General Quantity Theory of Money, helping sustainable expansion. vXOR, created through referendums, is priced lower than XOR.

Users can snag cheaper vXOR, which acts as collateral in the Kensetsu platform. This clever move opens doors for more people in finance and beefs up the SORA ecosystem.

### Improved Liquidity Management

SORA V3 takes liquidity management to new heights. Producers get tokens they can swap for Kensetsu XOR in Polkaswap liquidity pools, starting with XOR-ETH. This fix tackles the liquidity crunch seen in SORA V2, which struggled with a flexible XOR supply managed by a token bonding curve.

Chameleon pool liquidity providers earn KARMA tokens, kicking off with the XOR-ETH pool. The SORA Ecosystem Integrated Plan is now live, bringing Polkaswap as a Service to the table. This service mixes in decentralized exchange features, enabling cross-chain liquidity.
