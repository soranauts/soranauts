## 1. 100 × Token Repackaging — Why It Matters <a id="1-100-token-repackaging--why-it-matters"></a>  
The community-approved referendum re-denominates every **100 XOR → 1 XOR** and **100 TBC → 1 TBCD**. The change only affects assets *inside* the SORA network; legacy tokens on Ethereum or other chains convert automatically when bridged in.  

**Quick Start**  
1. Bridge legacy XOR into SORA.  
2. Watch your balance shrink (digit-wise) while value stays the same.  
3. Enjoy fewer fat-finger mistakes.  

For a deeper dive, see *“Understanding the TBCD and Its Role in the SORA Ecosystem”* on Medium.

## 3. Fujiwara Testnet — Hands-On With the Future <a id="3-fujiwara-testnet--hands-on-with-the-future"></a>  
The public **Fujiwara** testnet rebuilds weekly. Spinning up a one-node validator takes about ten minutes:

1. `git clone https://github.com/sora-xor/fujiwara`  
2. `cd fujiwara && ./bootstrap.sh`  
3. `sora --validator --name YOUR_NODE`  

**Validator Checklist**  
* 4 vCPUs, 8 GB RAM, 200 GB SSD  
* Static IP with P2P ports open  
* Daily log snapshots to spot consensus hiccups  

Why bother? The hub-chain will let anyone — even central banks — spin up assets on a supranational platform while preserving privacy in their own sub-nets.

## 4. Polkaswap 2.0 — Multi-Sig + Expiry <a id="4-polkaswap-20--multi-sig--expiry"></a>  
[Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io) now lets you bundle **multi-signature orders** (up to 15 signers) and add an expiry block so stale trades self-cancel. Perfect for DAO treasuries, family funds, or any shared vault.  

**Quick Start**  
1. **Settings → Security → Enable Multi-Sig**.  
2. Add co-signers’ SS58 addresses.  
3. Tick **Auto-cancel after** and choose a block number.

## FAQ — Read This First <a id="faq—read-this-first"></a>  

**Q1. Will my old XOR on Ethereum auto-convert?**  
Yes. When you bridge legacy XOR into the SORA network, the bridge automatically converts it to the new 1 : 100 denomination — no manual steps required.  

**Q2. Does the hub-chain mean SORA is abandoning Polkadot?**  
No. SORA V3 runs *alongside* the existing Polkadot-based SORA V2. A bridge will connect both, and the hub-chain simply adds a neutral settlement layer for sovereign players.  

**Q3. How can I test Fujiwara today?**  
Follow the validator tutorial in the [SORA Wiki](https://wiki.sora.org). A 4 vCPU VPS and open P2P ports are enough to join.  

**Q4. Is SORA Card available outside Europe yet?**  
Not yet. The physical and virtual cards are currently limited to residents of the European Economic Area. Global expansion is planned; check the [restricted-countries list](https://soracard.com/blacklist) for updates.
