## FAQs

### What is SORA blockchain?

SORA is a blockchain platform with its own on-chain governance system that helps make decisions about funding and creating new goods and services.

### How does the SORA token bonding curve work?

The [token bonding curve](https://www.soranauts.com/soras-token-bonding-curve-dollar-tbcd-explained) in the SORA network manages how new tokens are made and priced, helping match supply with demand to prevent big ups and downs in value.

### Can SORA help stop boom-bust cycles in the economy?

Yes, SORA aims to create an elastic supply of capital using a decentralized central bank idea to help avoid boom-bust valuations common in other cryptocurrencies.

### What makes SORA different from regular economic systems?

SORA is built on the idea of a supranational world economic system without one central bank controlling everything, which can lead to fairer ways to use money and resources.

### Can anyone be involved in making choices on the SORA network?

Yes, if you hold XOR tokens on the network, you can join others in voting for how to best use funds for projects that make good products and services.

### Is the Sora Blockchain connected to other blockchains like Polkadot or Kusama?

Yes! As a parachain, it runs alongside both Kusama and Polkadot ecosystems - this means it works well with different blockchains for more flexibility.
