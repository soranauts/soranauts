## SORA: A Revolutionary Economic Model

The [SORA network](https://sora.org) reimagines economic systems with three key innovations:

**1. Elastic Supply Model**: Unlike Bitcoin's fixed supply or fiat's arbitrary printing, SORA uses an elastic supply mechanism that automatically adjusts based on economic conditions to maintain stability.

**2. Decentralized Governance**: Community-driven decision making through SORA Parliament and referendum voting with transparent proposal and funding systems.

**3. Cross-Chain Integration**: Built for interoperability with multiple blockchains, enabling seamless asset transfers and future-proof architecture.

### SORA v3: The Next Evolution

SORA v3 builds on Hyperledger Iroha 2 as a Hub Chain for CBDCs and government integration. The **<a class="glossary-term" href="/glossary#fujiwara-testnet" data-term="fujiwara-testnet" aria-describedby="tip-fujiwara-testnet">Fujiwara Testnet</a>** is now live, testing DeFi functionality, SORA Parliament governance, and network stability on enterprise-grade infrastructure.

<span role="tooltip" id="tip-fujiwara-testnet" class="glossary-tip" hidden>

</span>

## Polkaswap: The Cross-Chain Trading Revolution

[Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io) is a **liquidity aggregation platform** that enables seamless cross-chain trading across multiple blockchains.

**Key Benefits:**
- **Borderless Trading**: Trade assets from different blockchains without bridges
- **Reduced Slippage**: Better prices through liquidity aggregation
- **Trustless Trading**: Smart contract-based settlement with full user control
- **Community Governance**: Aligned with SORA's economic model and transparent fee structures
