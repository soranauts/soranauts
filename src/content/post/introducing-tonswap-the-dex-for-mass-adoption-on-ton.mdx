## Why TON?

The **[Open Network (TON)](https://ton.org)** is a sharded Layer-1 originally conceived by Telegram.
With native wallet integration, sub-second finality, and negligible fees, TON lowers the barrier for the **800 million+ Telegram users** who will power the next wave of DeFi adoption.

## Core Features

-   **Mobile-native UX** — a Telegram-style interface makes swapping as simple as sending a sticker.
-   **TON-native liquidity pools** — earn yield on $TON and popular jettons without leaving the ecosystem.
-   **Blazing performance** — sharding + TVM deliver near-zero gas costs on every trade.
-   **Permissionless listing** — anyone can create a pool, bootstrap liquidity, and launch community tokens.

## Bridging to SORA v3

While TONSWAP is **built on TON**, it will connect to the upcoming **[SORA v3 Hub Chain](https://soranauts.com/sora-roadmap-token-repackaging-hub-chain-defi-upgrades)** via a trust-minimized bridge, unlocking:

-   Access to **SORA-based assets** such as $KUSD stablecoins.
-   Deep liquidity shared with **[Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io)** and other SORA-aligned DEXs.
-   Cross-chain DeFi strategies without leaving the TONSWAP UI.

> “TONSWAP will be built on TON (The Open Network) and will have a bridge to the upcoming SORA v3 Hub Chain network.” — _[Introducing TONSWAP](https://tonswap-org.medium.com/introducing-tonswap-260536a78c6b)_

## Getting Started

1.  Install the **TON Wallet** browser extension or Telegram mini-app.
2.  Acquire $TON via **Telegram’s @wallet bot** or a CEX such as MEXC.
3.  Head to **TONSWAP** (testnet link coming soon) and connect your wallet.
4.  Provide liquidity or swap instantly—no KYC, no hurdles.
