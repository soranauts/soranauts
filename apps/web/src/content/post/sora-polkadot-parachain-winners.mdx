## Understanding Parachains and Polkadot Auctions

At the heart of Polkadot's innovative design lies the concept of parachains—unique blockchains that operate in parallel to supercharge functionality and interoperability. The fiercely competitive parachain auctions are where projects vie for these coveted slots, a process both thrilling and critical, as securing a place on this relay chain can be transformative for any blockchain venture.

### What are parachains?

Parachains are like unique lanes on a super-fast highway. Each one is its own special blockchain that fits into the larger Polkadot network. They work side by side without slowing each other down, kind of like trains on parallel tracks.

This setup lets them handle more transactions and do different tasks all at once.

They're not just any blockchains; to join the Polkadot network as a parachain, you have to win a spot through auctions. It's tough because lots of projects fight for these slots—it's like the Olympics for blockchain tech! Winning means getting connected to Polkadot’s central chain, which can make your project stronger and ready to grow big.

### How do Polkadot auctions work?

Polkadot auctions are exciting events. They determine who gets to join the Polkadot network as a parachain. Here's how these auctions work:

- Projects place bids using DOT, Polkadot's cryptocurrency, to compete for a limited number of parachain slots.
- Each bid is a promise to lock up a certain amount of DOT for as long as the parachain slot is leased.
- Slots are leased in periods that can last up to two years, providing stability for projects.
- These auctions follow an unpermissioned candle auction format. This means no one knows exactly when the auction will end.
- The candle auction's ending is retroactively determined, which helps prevent last - minute sniping bids.
- If a project wins, they must lock up the DOT they bid until their lease period ends.
- Other users can support their favorite projects by contributing DOT to crowdloans. In return, they often receive rewards from the project.
- Crowdloan contributions add to a project’s total bid. This can increase its chances of winning a slot.
- Once an auction closes, the winning project secures its slot and can connect its blockchain to Polkadot's relay chain for enhanced security and interoperability.
- The project then gets busy integrating with Polkadot and preparing its technology to go live on the network.

## FAQs

### What is a Polkadot parachain auction?

A [Polkadot parachain](https://soranauts.com/understanding-parachains-kusama-polkadot-guide) auction is a competitive event where projects bid to secure one of the limited slots on the Polkadot network to run their blockchain.

### How did SORA win a slot in the second round of Polkadot parachains?

SORA managed to gather enough DOT — the cryptocurrency of Polkadot — through crowdloan contributions and won its position by bidding in the ninth parachain auction.

### Does winning a parachain spot mean SORA will be part of Kusama too?

Not directly; while Kusama is closely related to Polkadot, securing a slot on one doesn't grant automatic inclusion on the other. Each operates its own set of auctions for parachains.

### Why are these slots important for projects like SORA within the Polkadot ecosystem?

Parachain slots are crucial because they allow blockchains like SORA to connect with Polkadot's main relay chain, benefiting from enhanced security and interoperability with other networks.

### When did Parachains start operating on Polkadot?

The first batch of successful projects began operating as official parachains on the network after auctions concluded at the end of 2021.

### Can any project join these auctions or do they need something special?

Projects need resources—often large sums of DOT—to compete effectively in an auction and if successful, must maintain robust technology and community support to thrive within this sharded proof-of-stake blockchain ecosystem.
