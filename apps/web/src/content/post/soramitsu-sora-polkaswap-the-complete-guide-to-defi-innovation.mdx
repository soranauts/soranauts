## Introduction
The [SORA ecosystem](https://sora.org), developed by [SORAMITSU](https://soramitsu.co.jp), represents one of the boldest attempts to rethink global finance. Combining the **SORA blockchain**, the **XOR token**, and **Polkaswap**, this ecosystem isn't just another DeFi project — it's a blueprint for a decentralized, interoperable, and community-governed economy.

In this guide, we'll explore how SORA works, what makes it unique, and how its components fit together to form a financial system built for the future.

## The Vision Behind SORA
At its core, SORA is more than a blockchain: it's an **economic system**. Guided by economic theories such as those of [Professor Richard Werner](/richard-werner-exposes-central-banks-and-the-sora-alternative), SORA aims to create a more **equitable and sustainable global economy**. The idea is simple yet powerful — enable communities to issue credit, manage resources, and build without relying on centralized banks.

## Polkaswap: SORA's Decentralized Exchange
Polkaswap is the decentralized exchange (DEX) built on SORA. Unlike typical DEXs, Polkaswap is **interoperable**, allowing assets from different blockchains to trade in a trustless way.

Key features include:
- **Cross-chain trading** via bridges to Ethereum, Polkadot, Kusama, and TON, with upcoming JAM compatibility.  
- **Liquidity aggregation** to optimize swaps.  
- **Fee structure**: 0.3% trading fees in XOR are used to buy back and burn PSWAP tokens, with burned PSWAP partially reminted as rewards for liquidity providers (starting at 90%, decreasing to 35% after 5 years).
- **Integration with the SORA Card** for real-world spending.

For detailed documentation, see [Polkaswap Docs](https://wiki.sora.org/polkaswap.html).

## TONSWAP: Expanding the SORA Ecosystem to TON
**TONSWAP** is a next-generation decentralized exchange built on The Open Network (TON) blockchain, featuring concentrated liquidity technology and seamless Telegram integration for mobile-first DeFi access.

A key innovation connects TONSWAP directly to the SORA economy: **10% of all TONSWAP trading fees are automatically allocated to buy back and burn XOR tokens**. This mechanism:
- Creates sustained on-chain demand for XOR from TON ecosystem activity.
- Reduces XOR circulating supply through recurring burns.
- Connects two major blockchain ecosystems (TON and SORA) through economic alignment.

This cross-ecosystem buyback mechanism demonstrates how SORA's interoperable design enables value capture from multiple blockchain networks.

### How the Bonding Curve Powers the SORA Economy
SORA's **bonding curve** governs how new XOR enters circulation. Instead of mining or fixed issuance, the system expands or contracts XOR supply depending on market demand. This ensures:
- Predictable liquidity for ecosystem growth.  
- A self-balancing system resistant to manipulation.  
- Transparent monetary policy coded into the protocol.  

The bonding curve also maintains **TBCD (Token Bonding Curve Dollar)**, an algorithmic stablecoin pegged to approximately $1 USD. TBCD can only be created and allocated by on-chain governance, meaning XOR token holders decide the supply. It serves as a reserve asset and can be allocated for funding the creation of new goods and services within the ecosystem.

## Kensetsu: SORA's Over-Collateralized Stablecoin Platform
The **Kensetsu Platform** is SORA's version of MakerDAO, enabling users to create over-collateralized stablecoins through a decentralized borrowing system. Users can:
- Create vaults and deposit collateral (XOR, VAL, PSWAP, TBCD, ETH, or DAI).
- Borrow **KUSD** (Kensetsu USD) against their collateral while maintaining the $1 peg through algorithmic governance.
- Benefit from stability mechanisms where 19.5% of all SORA network transaction fees are allocated for KUSD buyback and burning.

In **SORA v3 tokenomics**, KUSD is used to pay builders instead of XOR, helping maintain the KUSD peg while funding ecosystem development. This creates a sustainable model for growth while keeping the stablecoin value stable.

## Governance in SORA
SORA currently uses **Polkadot v1 Governance** (also called **Governance V1**), a decentralized governance model where token holders propose and vote on key decisions through council-based proposals and referenda. Unlike traditional systems where power concentrates at the top, SORA governance aims to distribute influence fairly among participants.

Holders of both **XOR** and **VAL** can participate, making governance not only decentralized but also **incentive-aligned** with network security.

Looking ahead, **SORA v3** will introduce the **SORA Parliament**, a next-generation governance system using multi-body sortition with random citizen selection (not token voting). Citizens will post XOR bonds to participate, and the Parliament will allocate newly minted XOR to productive projects through a hybrid DAO framework with Iroha Special Instructions (ISIs).

## The SORA Card: Bridging Crypto and Everyday Spending
The SORA Card extends the ecosystem into the real world. With it, users can:
- Spend crypto like cash at merchants worldwide.  
- Fund their card directly from Polkaswap with support for swaps between BTC, ETH, USDC, and EUR directly in-app.  
- Retain privacy and control while staying connected to traditional financial rails with SEPA IBAN support.  

This makes SORA one of the few ecosystems with a **direct bridge from DeFi to daily life**.

### Why SORA Chose Hyperledger Iroha 3
**SORA v2** operates on Substrate (a legacy Polkadot-compatible chain). Early **Hub Chain prototypes (Phases 1–2)** were implemented on Hyperledger Iroha 2. The upcoming **SORA v3 — codenamed Nexus —** migrates fully to [Hyperledger Iroha 3](https://docs.iroha.tech/), establishing an independent Hub Chain architecture.

This transition provides:
- **Higher performance** with BFT consensus. Iroha 2’s Sumeragi consensus demonstrated the *theoretical possibility* of up to 20,000 TPS under lab conditions. Iroha 3 builds upon this foundation with a redesigned consensus layer for deterministic, enterprise-grade performance.  
- **Enhanced modularity** with layered runtime for domain-specific modules and Iroha Special Instructions (ISIs).  
- **Enterprise-level stability** proven in CBDC deployments, with improved security primitives and validator orchestration.  
- **Hub Chain architecture** enabling seamless collaboration between permissioned and decentralized systems across multiple blockchains (TON, Polkadot, Ethereum).

The **Fujiwara testnet** serves as the development playground for SORA v3 features, providing valuable insights for this innovative public blockchain infrastructure.

This move reflects SORAMITSU's track record as the developer of national-level CBDCs, such as Cambodia's [Bakong system](https://bakong.nbc.gov.kh/en/).
