### Brief history of major crypto hacks

Hacks have targeted various blockchains, causing significant losses. These events have shaken the trust of investors and users in the security of cryptocurrencies.

1.  [In 2014, Mt. Gox](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/m/mt-gox.asp), one of the biggest bitcoin exchanges, lost $460 million to hackers. This event marked one of the earliest and largest breaches in cryptocurrency history.
2.  The DAO, a decentralized autonomous organization on Ethereum, faced a $50 million theft in 2016 due to a smart-contract vulnerability.
3.  Bitfinex, another large cryptocurrency exchange, was hacked in 2016, resulting in the loss of $72 million worth of bitcoin.
4.  Coincheck suffered a massive breach in 2018 where hackers stole $534 million worth of NEM tokens.
5.  In 2019, Binance, a leading crypto exchange, reported a hack that led to the theft of $40 million worth of bitcoin.
6.  Poly Network experienced one of the most [significant hacks](https://research.kudelskisecurity.com/2021/08/12/the-poly-network-hack-explained/) in DeFi history in 2021 with over $600 million stolen across multiple blockchains.
7.  KuCoin also fell victim to hackers in 2020 with about $281 million stolen from its wallets.
