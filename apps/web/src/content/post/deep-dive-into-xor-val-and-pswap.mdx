**Updated for 2025** — The SORA ecosystem runs on three foundational tokens: **XOR**, **VAL**, and **PSWAP**.  
Each serves a distinct purpose — from economic coordination and governance to liquidity provisioning — working together to sustain a **self-regulating, decentralized economy**.

## Understanding SORA's Token Framework

The SORA Network is more than just a blockchain — it's an **economic system** built to manage resources, decision-making, and liquidity in a decentralized way.  
Instead of relying on central banks or intermediaries, SORA uses algorithmic and governance-based mechanisms powered by its native tokens.

| Token | Primary Role | Core Function | Layer of the Ecosystem |
|--------|---------------|----------------|------------------------|
| **XOR** | Economic Base | Acts as SORA’s main currency for transactions, liquidity, and minting assets | Economic layer |
| **VAL** | Governance | Allows voting, decision-making, and treasury management | Governance layer |
| **PSWAP** | Liquidity & Incentives | Rewards liquidity providers and supports decentralized trading | Market layer |

Together, these tokens enable a **self-balancing system** that supports trading, decision-making, and sustainable tokenomics across the SORA and Polkaswap networks.

## 1. XOR — The Economic Engine of SORA

**XOR (SORA Token)** is the foundation of SORA’s digital economy.

### Key Functions
- **Currency and Settlement Unit:** Used to pay transaction fees and for cross-chain swaps on Polkaswap.  
- **Liquidity Reserve:** Acts as the base asset for minting and redeeming stablecoins such as **KUSD (Kensetsu USD)**.  
- **Dynamic Supply Control:** Its supply adjusts algorithmically through the **token bonding curve**, expanding or contracting based on market activity. The [Bancor Protocol](https://bancor.network/) pioneered this concept for algorithmic market makers.  
- **Decentralized Monetary Policy:** Removes the need for centralized control over money issuance, creating a self-regulating financial system. This approach is similar to [MakerDAO's DAI stability mechanisms](https://makerdao.com/en/whitepaper/).

| Feature | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Token Type | Native currency of the SORA network |
| Supply Model | Algorithmically adjusted via bonding curve |
| Use Cases | Payments, liquidity, asset creation, and network fees |
| Interoperability | Cross-chain swaps via Polkaswap and bridges |

**In essence:** XOR represents **economic energy** — the fuel for transactions and the anchor for stability across SORA’s ecosystem.

## 2. VAL — Governance and Network Coordination

**VAL (SORA Validator Token)** empowers the community to **govern and evolve** the SORA network.

### Key Functions
- **Voting Power:** VAL holders propose and vote on governance decisions like validator policies, treasury allocations, and network parameters.  
- **Validator Incentives:** Supports the consensus layer by rewarding network validators.  
- **Ecosystem Development:** VAL is used to fund projects that strengthen the ecosystem, such as community grants or infrastructure upgrades.

| Governance Function | Example |
|----------------------|----------|
| Proposal Voting | Adjusting transaction fees or staking parameters |
| Treasury Allocation | Funding ecosystem tools, education, or DeFi apps |
| Validator Staking | Securing network operations |
| Community Incentives | Rewarding participation in governance or development |

**In essence:** VAL transforms SORA into a **community-driven economy**, where users control the rules, priorities, and future direction.

## 3. PSWAP — Liquidity and Market Efficiency

**PSWAP (Polkaswap Token)** incentivizes and sustains liquidity across Polkaswap, SORA’s decentralized exchange.

### Key Functions
- **Liquidity Provider Rewards:** Distributed to users who contribute liquidity to trading pools.  
- **Swap Incentives:** Encourages consistent participation and market depth.  
- **Deflationary Mechanism:** A percentage of PSWAP is burned with each swap, gradually reducing total supply.  
- **Cross-Chain Utility:** Helps balance liquidity between XOR-based and bridged assets like ETH, DAI, and DOT.

| Feature | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Token Type | DEX reward and incentive token |
| Emission Model | Decreasing over time with burning mechanism |
| Utility | Liquidity rewards, market incentives, and governance integration |
| Network | Polkaswap on the SORA Network |

**In essence:** PSWAP keeps Polkaswap **efficient, liquid, and community-driven**, aligning incentives for traders, liquidity providers, and developers alike.

## 6. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q1: What gives XOR its value if it’s algorithmically adjusted?**  
XOR’s value comes from its **utility and demand** — it’s required for transactions, liquidity, and stable asset creation. Its algorithmic model maintains balance between supply and ecosystem activity.

**Q2: How is VAL different from PSWAP?**  
VAL governs the network and treasury, while PSWAP incentivizes liquidity on Polkaswap. VAL directs long-term strategy; PSWAP supports short-term market efficiency.

**Q3: Can I use PSWAP for governance?**  
Not directly. Governance is primarily handled via **VAL**, though future integrations may connect PSWAP's role to liquidity governance mechanisms.

**Q4: What is the connection between XOR and KUSD?**  
XOR is used as collateral and pricing reference for minting **KUSD (Kensetsu USD)**, maintaining a stable, interoperable unit of value within the ecosystem.

**Q5: How does SORA prevent token inflation?**  
Through mechanisms like **PSWAP burning**, **VAL governance**, and **XOR's bonding curve**, SORA continuously rebalances supply and demand without relying on fixed inflation models.
