## Why DEXs Matter Today

Traders increasingly prioritize:  
- **Self-custody** (no custodial risk),  
- **Transparency** (open [smart contracts](/glossary/smart-contract)),  
- **Global access**, and  
- **On-chain innovation** like [yield farming](/glossary/yield-farming) and [liquidity pools](/glossary/liquidity-pool).

For **SORA**, DEXs act as core economic infrastructure enabling concepts like [elastic supply](/glossary/elastic-supply), the [token bonding curve](https://wiki.sora.org/tbc.html), and seamless [cross-chain](/glossary/cross-chain) activity.

## Spotlight: TONSWAP (on TON)

**[TONSWAP](/glossary/tonswap)** is a next-generation DEX and launchpad built on The Open Network (TON) blockchain, designed for mobile-first trading with seamless Telegram integration.

**Why it stands out**
- **Mobile-first design** with native Telegram wallet integration  
- **Ultra-low fees** with near-zero transaction costs on TON  
- **Concentrated liquidity** (CLMM) for optimized trading efficiency  
- **Launchpad functionality** for new TON projects and token launches  
- **Mass adoption potential** with access to 1B+ Telegram users

TONSWAP represents the future of mobile DeFi, making decentralized trading as simple as sending a message in Telegram while maintaining the security and transparency of on-chain protocols. Its native token **TS** powers platform governance, liquidity incentives, and transaction fee models.

**Unique Economic Model**: TONSWAP implements a deflationary mechanism where 10% of all trading fees are used to buy back and burn [XOR](/glossary/xor) tokens, creating a direct economic bridge between TON and SORA ecosystems while reducing XOR's circulating supply.

## Choosing the Right DEX

Consider:  
- **Liquidity depth** (slippage and spreads)  
- **Asset support** (does it list what you need?)  
- **Cross-chain access** (Polkaswap excels here)  
- **Fees and rewards** (trading costs vs LP incentives)  
- **Governance** (token-holder influence over upgrades)

## FAQs

### What makes DEXs different from CEXs?
DEXs rely on on-chain [smart contracts](/glossary/smart-contract) for peer-to-peer trading, while CEXs are custodial and order-book based.

### Is Polkaswap beginner-friendly?
Yes. It preserves self-custody and offers straightforward swaps, with deeper features for advanced users.

### What makes TONSWAP unique?
[TONSWAP](/glossary/tonswap) is the first DEX designed specifically for mobile users through Telegram integration. It offers ultra-low fees on the TON blockchain, concentrated liquidity technology, and a built-in launchpad for new projects. The seamless Telegram experience makes it as easy as sending a message.

### How does TONSWAP connect to SORA?
TONSWAP is built on TON (The Open Network) and will feature a bridge to the upcoming [SORA v3 Hub Chain](/glossary/sora-v3) network. This bridge will enable seamless cross-chain liquidity and trading between TON and SORA ecosystems, allowing users to access both networks’ assets and features through a single interface.

### Which DEX should I start with?
- **Mobile users**: [TONSWAP](/glossary/tonswap) for Telegram integration and ultra-low fees  
- **Cross-chain trading**: **Polkaswap** for SORA ecosystem access  
- **Ethereum simplicity**: **Uniswap** or **PancakeSwap** for basic trading
