## Why DEXs Matter Today
Traders increasingly prioritize:  
- **Self-custody** (no custodial risk)

- **On-chain innovation** like yield farming and liquidity pools  

For **SORA**, DEXs are core economic infrastructure enabling elastic supply, the [token bonding curve](https://wiki.sora.org/tbc.html), and seamless cross-chain activity.

## Interpreting TVL vs Innovation

A DEX’s **Total Value Locked (TVL)** measures how much liquidity users have deposited — it reflects **trust and capital depth**, not necessarily *technological innovation*.  

In November 2025, data from DeFiLlama shows Uniswap and PancakeSwap leading with multi-billion-dollar TVL across dozens of chains, while new entrants like **HumidiFi**, **Fluid**, and **Meteora** are climbing rapidly.  

However, Soranauts rankings focus on **innovation and ecosystem impact** rather than just TVL.  

| Perspective | What It Measures | Example |
|--------------|------------------|----------|
| **TVL Rank** | Total liquidity across chains | Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Curve |
| **Innovation Rank** | Design, interoperability, real-world integration | Polkaswap, TONSWAP, dYdX |

High TVL often means market maturity, but smaller or emerging DEXs can still lead in **cross-chain interoperability, governance design, or mobile adoption**. In SORA’s case, **Polkaswap** and **TONSWAP** emphasize *future-focused architecture* — not just liquidity, but *connectivity* between networks.

*Source: [DeFiLlama DEX Rankings](https://defillama.com/dexs), November 2025.*

## Spotlight: TONSWAP (on TON)
**TONSWAP** is a next-generation DEX and launchpad built on The Open Network (TON), designed for mobile-first trading with seamless Telegram integration.

**Why it stands out**
- **Mobile-first design** with native Telegram wallet integration  
- **Ultra-low fees** on TON (near-zero gas)  
- **Concentrated liquidity (CLMM)** for efficient swaps  
- **Launchpad functionality** for new TON projects  
- **Mass adoption potential** through Telegram’s 1 B+ user base  

**Economic Model** — TONSWAP uses a deflationary mechanism where fees buy back and burn TS tokens. Additionally, *implemented or planned* allocation of 10 % of all trading fees to buy back and burn XOR tokens creates a direct economic bridge between TON and SORA, reducing XOR’s circulating supply.

## Choosing the Right DEX
When selecting a DEX, consider:  
- **Liquidity depth** (slippage + spreads)  
- **Asset support** (listed pairs)  
- **Cross-chain access** (Polkaswap excels)  
- **Fees & rewards** (trading vs LP yield)  
- **Governance** (token-holder control over upgrades)
