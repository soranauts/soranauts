## TL;DR
Decentralized exchanges (DEXs) form a core layer of modern crypto trading, letting users swap assets while retaining self-custody.  
This article compares leading DEXs across ecosystems, focusing on **Polkaswap** on SORA and **TONSWAP** on TON, alongside Ethereum-based platforms like **Uniswap** and **Curve**.  
It highlights how innovation metrics—such as cross-chain connectivity, mobile-first design, and on-chain derivatives from platforms like **dYdX** and **GMX**—complement traditional TVL rankings when evaluating DEXs.  
Overall, DEXs offer transparency, control, and global access, but differ significantly in liquidity depth, user experience, and ecosystem design.

## Why DEXs Matter Today
This guide is for traders and DeFi users who want a neutral comparison of major DEXs in 2025, with extra attention on how Polkaswap and TONSWAP fit into the broader landscape.

## Interpreting TVL vs Innovation
A DEX’s **Total Value Locked (TVL)** measures how much liquidity users have deposited — it reflects **trust and capital depth**, not necessarily *technological innovation*.  

As of November 2025, data from DeFiLlama shows Uniswap and PancakeSwap leading with multi-billion-dollar TVL across dozens of chains, while new entrants like **HumidiFi**, **Fluid**, and **Meteora** are climbing rapidly.  

However, Soranauts rankings focus on **innovation and ecosystem impact** rather than just TVL.  

| Perspective | What It Measures | Example |
|--------------|------------------|----------|
| **TVL Rank** | Total liquidity across chains | Uniswap, PancakeSwap, Curve |
| **Innovation Rank** | Design, interoperability, real-world asset integration | Polkaswap, TONSWAP, dYdX |

High TVL often indicates maturity, but smaller or emerging DEXs can lead in **cross-chain interoperability, governance design, or mobile adoption**.  
In SORA’s case, **Polkaswap** and **TONSWAP** emphasize *future-focused architecture* — not just liquidity, but *connectivity* between networks.

*Source: <a href="https://defillama.com/dexs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">DeFiLlama DEX Rankings</a>, November 2025.*

## Spotlight: TONSWAP (on TON)
**TONSWAP** is a next-generation DEX and launchpad built on The Open Network (TON), designed for mobile-first trading with seamless Telegram integration.

**Why it stands out**
- **Mobile-first design** with native Telegram wallet integration  
- **Ultra-low fees** on TON (near-zero gas)  
- **Concentrated liquidity (CLMM)** for efficient swaps  
- **Launchpad functionality** for new TON projects  
- **Mass adoption potential** through Telegram’s 1B+ user base  

**Economic Model** — TONSWAP uses a deflationary mechanism where a portion of fees buys back and burns TS tokens.  
There is also a planned economic bridge in which part of trading fees would buy back and burn XOR tokens, creating a direct connection between TON and SORA and helping to reduce XOR’s circulating supply once fully implemented.

## Choosing the Right DEX
When selecting a DEX, consider:  
- **Liquidity depth** (slippage + spreads)  
- **Asset support** (listed pairs)  
- **Cross-chain access** (Polkaswap excels)  
- **Fees & rewards** (trading vs LP yield)  
- **Governance** (token-holder control over upgrades)
