Telegram games exploded in 2024. Notcoin, Hamster Kombat, and others proved that simple tap-to-earn mechanics could onboard millions of crypto-curious users. The formula is straightforward: make it easy, make it addictive, add token incentives.

SORATOPIA is SORA's entry into this space—a samurai-themed game where players choose between the path of Bushido (honor) or Degeneracy (chaos), complete quests, and accumulate points for a future airdrop.

But unlike most Telegram games that exist in isolation, SORATOPIA connects to an actual DeFi ecosystem: Polkaswap, the SORA network, and the emerging TONSWAP platform. The game isn't just entertainment—it's an onboarding funnel.

This guide covers how SORATOPIA works, what's changed since launch, and what players should know about the upcoming airdrop.

## How to Play SORATOPIA

Getting started takes less than a minute:

### Step 1: Open the Game on Telegram

Direct link: <a href="https://t.me/soratopia_bot/app" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">t.me/soratopia_bot/app</a>

No download required—runs directly in Telegram on mobile or desktop.

### Step 2: Choose Your Path

When you first enter SORATOPIA, you'll choose between:

- **Bushido** — The honorable samurai path, focused on discipline and long-term strategy
- **Degeneracy** — The chaotic degen path, embracing risk and short-term gains

Your choice affects the game's flavor and narrative but both paths earn points. Pick whichever resonates with your play style.

### Step 3: Daily Check-Ins

Tap to earn points—up to 6 times per day. Consistency matters. Daily streaks may boost rewards, so try not to skip days.

### Step 4: Complete Quests

New quests are added every few days, ranging from simple social tasks (follow SORA on X, join Telegram groups) to ecosystem engagement (exploring Polkaswap, learning about TONSWAP). Each completed quest earns bonus points.

### Step 5: Invite Allies

Share your referral link with friends. Each referral earns you **10% of their points**—a significant multiplier if your network is active.

### Step 6: Participate in Seasonal Challenges

SORATOPIA runs 14-day seasonal cycles with daily tasks (00:00–23:59 UTC). Completing seasonal objectives likely factors into airdrop eligibility, so stay engaged throughout each cycle.
