## 1. 100× Token Repackaging — Why It Matters
The community-approved referendum redenominated every **100 XOR → 1 XOR** and **100 TBCD → 1 TBCD**.  
The change only affected assets *inside* the SORA network; legacy tokens on Ethereum or other chains convert automatically when bridged in.  

**Quick Start**
1. Bridge legacy XOR into SORA.  
2. Watch your displayed balance shrink (digit-wise) while value stays the same.  
3. Enjoy fewer decimal headaches when trading or sending.  

For a deeper dive, see [Understanding the TBCD and Its Role in the SORA Ecosystem](/soras-token-bonding-curve-dollar-tbcd-explained).

## 3. Fujiwara Testnet — Hands-On With the Future
The **Fujiwara Testnet** serves as the proving ground for SORA v3's Hub-Chain and Hyperledger Iroha integration.  
It allows developers and early validators to test new transaction logic, governance modules, and interoperability bridges in a sandboxed environment.  

Fujiwara is **not rebuilt weekly** and **no longer uses manual bootstrap scripts**.  
Participation is coordinated through the official <a href="https://wiki.sora.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SORA Wiki</a> and community governance channels, where public validator access will be announced. For underlying ledger details, review the <a href="https://iroha.readthedocs.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Hyperledger Iroha documentation</a>. SORA v2 runs on Substrate. The Fujiwara testnet uses Iroha 2, while SORA v3 (Nexus) will run on Iroha 3.

**Key Goals**
- Validate Hub-Chain message-passing and subnet configuration.  
- Test Iroha-based accounting precision and multi-asset handling.  
- Prepare the migration path from SORA v2 (Substrate-based) to SORA v3 (Nexus, built on Iroha 3).  

Follow Fujiwara developments via SORA’s Discord and governance threads — public participation will expand as the network matures.

## 4. Polkaswap 2.0 — Multi-Sig + Expiry
<a href="https://polkaswap.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Polkaswap</a> now lets users create **multi-signature orders** (up to 15 signers) with a built-in expiry block that automatically cancels stale trades.  
This feature strengthens DAO treasuries, shared vaults, and multi-party swaps.  

Learn more in the <a href="https://wiki.sora.org/polkaswap" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Polkaswap Docs</a>.

**Quick Start**
1. Go to **Settings → Security → Enable Multi-Sig**.  
2. Add co-signers’ SS58 addresses.  
3. Tick **Auto-cancel after** and select a block number.

## 5. SORA Card — Fiat ↔ Crypto Rollout
**SORA Card** is rolling out EUR-based fiat ↔ crypto functionality, with European users prioritized and additional assets integrating progressively. Support for major crypto assets (including BTC, ETH, stablecoins, and SORA ecosystem tokens like XOR) is part of the ongoing roadmap.  

### Why It Matters
* **Integrated KYC**: Fiat services are provided via an IBAN account with standard verification, as described in the <a href="https://wiki.sora.org/sora-card.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SORA Wiki</a>.
* **SEPA connectivity**: EUR transfers during banking hours.
* **Unified experience**: Fiat and crypto activity visible in one interface.  

**Availability:** SORA Card currently serves residents of the European Economic Area, with broader geographic support planned. See the <a href="https://soracard.com/blacklist" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">restricted countries list</a> for current limitations.

<FaqSection>
  <details>
    <summary>Will my old XOR on Ethereum auto-convert?</summary>
    Yes. When you bridge legacy XOR into the SORA network, the bridge automatically converts it to the new denomination — no manual steps required.
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Does the Hub-Chain mean SORA is abandoning Polkadot?</summary>
    No. SORA v3 runs alongside the existing Polkadot-based SORA v2. A bridge will connect both, while the Hub-Chain adds a neutral settlement layer for sovereign participants.
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>How can I test Fujiwara today?</summary>
    Validator guides and tooling are documented in the SORA Wiki. Public validator access will be announced through official governance channels.
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>How do Polkaswap multi-sig orders work?</summary>
    Multi-sig orders require a threshold of co-signers (up to 15) to approve before execution. You can enable this in Polkaswap under Settings → Security. See the Polkaswap Docs for details.
  </details>
  <details>
    <summary>Is SORA Card available outside Europe yet?</summary>
    Not yet. Physical and virtual cards are currently limited to residents of the European Economic Area. A global rollout is planned; see the <a href="https://soracard.com/blacklist" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">restricted countries list</a> for updates.
  </details>
</FaqSection>
