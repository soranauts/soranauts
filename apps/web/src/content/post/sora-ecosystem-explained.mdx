## Introduction

The [SORA](/glossary/sora) ecosystem is a decentralized economic network that coordinates multiple components — [XOR](/glossary/xor) as the monetary base, [Polkaswap](/glossary/polkaswap) for liquidity, [Kensetsu](/glossary/kensetsu) for stablecoins, and [TONSWAP](/glossary/tonswap) for Telegram-native DeFi. Rather than a single blockchain, SORA operates as a coordinated economy where public DeFi rails connect with permissioned partners through shared rules.

This guide explains how those building blocks work today and how **SORA Nexus (v3)** expands them into a multi-lane Hub Chain built on [Hyperledger Iroha](https://iroha.tech).

> **New to SORA?** Start with the [SORA Glossary](/glossary) to learn key terms, or explore the [SORA Explorer](/explore) for curated learning paths.

## SORA's Economic Blueprint

SORA applies the monetary research of economists like [Professor Richard Werner](/richard-werner-exposes-central-banks-and-the-sora-alternative) to a decentralized setting. Instead of minting tokens through mining or fixed emissions, XOR supply flexes with demand through the [Token Bonding Curve](/glossary/tokenbondingcurve), allowing the network to fund productive projects while maintaining predictable liquidity.

The ecosystem components work like specialized departments:

- **[XOR](/glossary/xor)** — Mints elastic currency
- **[Polkaswap](/glossary/polkaswap)** — Routes liquidity
- **[Kensetsu](/glossary/kensetsu)** — Maintains a dollar-denominated unit of account
- **Nexus** — Coordinates the rules that bind them

## Key Tokens of the SORA Ecosystem

### XOR — The Monetary Base

[XOR](/glossary/xor) is SORA's primary utility token and monetary base. Key characteristics:

- **Elastic supply** via the [Token Bonding Curve](/glossary/tokenbondingcurve)
- **Network fees** paid in XOR
- **Collateral** for [Kensetsu](/glossary/kensetsu) vaults
- **20% of bonding-curve intake** routes to XOR burn

### VAL — Validator Rewards

[VAL](/glossary/val) is a deflationary token that rewards [validators](/glossary/validator):

- **50% of on-chain fees** buy back and burn VAL
- Rewards are then reminted for [staking](/glossary/staking)
- Aligns security incentives with network usage

### PSWAP — Liquidity Incentives

[PSWAP](/glossary/pswap) powers liquidity rewards on [Polkaswap](/glossary/polkaswap):

- **0.3% swap fee** buys and burns PSWAP
- 90% of burned amount was reminted for LPs at launch
- Linearly tapers to 35% after five years

### KUSD — Over-Collateralized Stablecoin

[KUSD](/glossary/kusd) is SORA's dollar-pegged stablecoin:

- Minted via [Kensetsu](/glossary/kensetsu) vaults
- Accepts XOR, VAL, PSWAP, [TBCD](/glossary/tbcd), ETH, or DAI as collateral
- **19.5% of network fees** buy back and burn KUSD

### TBCD — Algorithmic Reserve Asset

[TBCD](/glossary/tbcd) (Token Bonding Curve Dollar) is a governance-minted reserve asset:

- Tradable and collateralizable
- New issuance suspended under v3 tokenomics
- 0.5% of fees go to TBCD buyback

> **Deep dive:** Read ["How XOR, VAL, and PSWAP Power the SORA Ecosystem"](/deep-dive-into-xor-val-and-pswap) for token-by-token analysis.

## Polkaswap: SORA's Decentralized Exchange

[Polkaswap](/glossary/polkaswap) is SORA's cross-chain [liquidity](/glossary/liquidity) router. It aggregates pools across [HASHI](/glossary/hashi)-connected networks (Ethereum, Polkadot, Kusama) so users can swap assets without leaving self-custody.

**Key features:**

- **Cross-chain swaps** via HASHI bridges
- **[AMM](/glossary/amm) liquidity pools** with fee sharing
- **0.3% swap fee** funds PSWAP buyback-and-burn
- **SORA Card integration** for fiat on/off-ramps

> **Related:** ["How Polkaswap and TONSWAP Work Together on SORA"](/how-polkaswap-and-tonswap-work-together-on-sora)

## How the Token Bonding Curve Works

The [Token Bonding Curve](/glossary/tokenbondingcurve) (TBC) is SORA's algorithmic monetary policy — like an always-open central bank counter:

| Action | What Happens |
|--------|--------------|
| **Buy XOR** | Contract mints new tokens on a predictable slope |
| **Sell XOR** | Supply shrinks, reserves pay out immediately |
| **20% of intake** | Routes to XOR burn |
| **Remainder** | Adds to multi-asset reserves |

The curve also anchors [TBCD](/glossary/tbcd), an algorithmic stable asset used for governance-directed funding.

> **Learn more:** [Token Bonding Curve glossary entry](/glossary/tokenbondingcurve) or the [SORA Wiki TBC guide](https://wiki.sora.org/tbc.html)

## Kensetsu: Over-Collateralized Stablecoins

[Kensetsu](/glossary/kensetsu) functions like SORA's MakerDAO:

1. **Create a vault** with accepted collateral
2. **Mint [KUSD](/glossary/kusd)** against that collateral
3. **Monitor vault health** and maintain collateralization
4. **Repay debt + stability fee** to reclaim collateral

**Accepted collateral:** XOR, VAL, PSWAP, TBCD, ETH, DAI

KUSD is also earmarked for "builder payments" under v3 tokenomics, so development can be funded in a dollar-denominated unit without inflating XOR.

> **Vault walkthrough:** ["SORA Kensetsu Explained: Stablecoins and DeFi on Polkaswap"](/exploring-sora-kensetsu-polkaswap)

## Network Fee Distribution

SORA's v3 fee routing is deterministic — every transaction contributes to multiple sinks:

| Allocation | Purpose |
|------------|---------|
| **50%** | VAL buyback-and-burn → validator rewards |
| **20%** | Direct XOR burn |
| **19.5%** | KUSD buyback-and-burn (peg support) |
| **10%** | Referral rewards |
| **0.5%** | TBCD buyback |

This design creates automatic deflationary pressure across all major tokens.

## Governance in SORA

As of December 2025, SORA uses the **Substrate Gov1 pallet**:

- Council and referenda system
- Conviction voting with XOR
- [VAL](/glossary/val) aligns [validator](/glossary/validator) incentives via NPoS [staking](/glossary/staking)

### SORA Parliament (Coming with Nexus)

Nexus is designing a multi-body governance structure:

- **Isonomia** — Equal participation rights
- **Sortition** — Random committee selection
- **XOR bonds** — Skin-in-the-game for proposals
- **ISI execution** — Deterministic on-chain outcomes

> **Related:** ["SORA and Polkadot Governance Model"](/polkadot-sora-governance-model)

## Why SORA is Building on Hyperledger Iroha 3 (Nexus)

SORA v2 runs on [Hyperledger Iroha](https://iroha.tech), but **Nexus (v3)** pushes the architecture further:

### Multi-Lane Architecture

Think of Nexus as a multi-lane highway:

- Each **lane** finalizes transactions independently
- A **merge ledger** weaves lane tips into a single timeline
- **~1 second finality** per lane

### Key Nexus Components

| Component | Purpose |
|-----------|---------|
| **IVM** | Iroha Virtual Machine for deterministic contracts |
| **[Sumeragi](/glossary/sumeragi)** | BFT consensus with leader rotation |
| **FASTPQ** | zk-STARK proofs for lane receipts |
| **DA Sampling** | Data availability without exposing payloads |

> **Technical deep dive:** Download the [SORA Nexus Whitepaper](/documents/sora_nexus_whitepaper.pdf)

## The SORA Card: Crypto Meets Everyday Spending

The [SORA Card](https://wiki.sora.org/sora-card) bundles:

- **Self-custodial wallet** — Your keys, your crypto
- **Polkaswap access** — Swap directly from the card
