## Introduction
The <a href="https://sora.org" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SORA ecosystem</a>, developed by <a href="https://soramitsu.co.jp" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SORAMITSU</a>, represents one of the boldest attempts to rethink global finance. Combining the **SORA blockchain**, the **XOR token**, and **Polkaswap**, this ecosystem isn't just another blockchain project — it's a blueprint for a decentralized, interoperable, and community-governed economy.

With <a href="https://wiki.sora.org/sora-v3.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">SORA v3</a> in development, the network is evolving toward a Hub Chain architecture that bridges permissioned and decentralized systems, enabling seamless collaboration between CBDCs, DeFi applications, and traditional financial infrastructure.

In this guide, we'll explore how SORA works, what makes it unique, and how its components fit together to form a financial system built for the future.

## TL;DR
SORA is an economic system built around the XOR token, Polkaswap, Kensetsu (KUSD), and a forthcoming SORA v3 hub chain on Hyperledger Iroha 3.  
SORA v3 remains in active development on the Fujiwara testnet, so this guide focuses on how today’s tokenomics, governance, and infrastructure fit together while pointing to expected upgrades.  
Together, these components aim to support a cross-chain, non-debt-based economy that links DeFi platforms like Polkaswap and TONSWAP with real-world use cases such as the SORA Card.

## The Vision Behind SORA
At its core, SORA is more than a blockchain: it's an **economic system**. Guided by economic theories such as those of [Professor Richard Werner](/richard-werner-exposes-central-banks-and-the-sora-alternative), SORA aims to create a more **equitable and sustainable global economy** — enabling communities to issue credit, manage resources, and build without relying on centralized banks.

## Polkaswap: SORA's Decentralized Exchange
Polkaswap is the decentralized exchange (DEX) built on SORA. Unlike typical DEXs, Polkaswap is **interoperable**, allowing assets from different blockchains to trade in a trustless way.

Key features include:
- **Cross-chain trading** via <a href="https://wiki.sora.org/interoperability.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">HASHI bridges</a> to Ethereum, Polkadot, and Kusama.  
- **Liquidity aggregation** to optimize swaps across multiple liquidity sources.  
- **Fee structure**: 0.3% trading fees drive PSWAP buyback-and-burn with a declining remint schedule for LP rewards. {/* VERIFY percentages/timing vs current docs */}  
- **Integration with the SORA Card** for real-world spending.

For detailed documentation, see <a href="https://wiki.sora.org/polkaswap.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Polkaswap Docs</a>.

## TONSWAP: Expanding the SORA Ecosystem to TON
**TONSWAP** is a next-generation decentralized exchange built on The Open Network (TON), featuring concentrated liquidity and seamless Telegram integration.

A key innovation connects TONSWAP directly to the SORA economy: **10% of all TONSWAP trading fees are allocated to buy back and burn XOR**. This mechanism:
- Creates sustained on-chain demand for XOR from TON activity.  
- Reduces XOR circulating supply through recurring burns executed by smart contracts.  
- Aligns TON activity with the SORA economy by turning trading volume into a recurring XOR sink.

## How the Bonding Curve Powers the SORA Economy
SORA's <a href="https://wiki.sora.org/tbc.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>token bonding curve (TBC)</strong></a> governs how new XOR enters circulation. Instead of mining or fixed issuance, supply expands or contracts through buy/sell price functions, providing:
- Predictable liquidity for ecosystem growth  
- Transparent, rules-based monetary policy  
- A self-balancing system resistant to manipulation  
- Deep immediate liquidity (the curve is always a counterparty)

The bonding curve maintains **TBCD (Token Bonding Curve Dollar)**, an algorithmic, non-synthetic stablecoin whose value is maintained by the TBC and allocated via on-chain governance. While **SORA v3 tokenomics** suspend TBCD minting, TBCD remains an active asset tradable on Polkaswap and usable as Kensetsu collateral.

## Governance in SORA
SORA currently uses <a href="https://wiki.sora.org/sora-governance.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Polkadot v1 Governance</strong></a>, a decentralized system of **council-initiated proposals and referenda**. Holders of **XOR** and **VAL** participate, aligning governance and security.

Looking ahead, **SORA v3 (Nexus)** is expected to introduce the **SORA Parliament**, inspired by **Isonomia**, **Isegoria**, and **Sortition**. Citizens post XOR bonds to participate; multiple bodies with clear separation of powers oversee allocations of newly minted XOR to productive projects. Governance modules are being designed with Iroha 3 **Special Instructions (ISI)** and may incorporate OpenGov-style inclusivity while retaining parliamentary oversight. {/* Status: active development/testing */}

## Why SORA Chose Hyperledger Iroha 3
**SORA v2** built on <a href="https://iroha.tech" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Hyperledger Iroha 2</strong></a> laid groundwork for a sovereign Hub Chain. The upcoming **SORA v3 Hub Chain (SORA Nexus)** is being developed on **Hyperledger Iroha 3**, moving from Phase 1–2 prototypes on Iroha 2 toward a full implementation.

This transition provides:
- **Higher performance** with BFT consensus and a re-engineered architecture  
- **Enhanced modularity** and deterministic command sets  
- **Enterprise-proven lineage** from national-scale deployments  
- **Hub Chain architecture** to interoperate with CBDCs and public chains (TON, Polkadot, Ethereum)  
- **Deterministic governance logic** using ISI

The <a href="https://wiki.sora.org/running-a-sora-testnet-node.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Fujiwara testnet</strong></a> is the dev playground for SORA v3 features. Migration planning (Iroha 2 → Iroha 3) focuses on compatibility and a unified Hub Chain connecting external networks.
