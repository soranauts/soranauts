DeFi has a distribution problem. Most people don't have browser wallets, don't understand gas fees, and won't visit a DEX website.

TONSWAP solves this by meeting users where they already are: **Telegram.**

Built on TON (The Open Network), TONSWAP is a decentralized exchange accessible directly through Telegram's mini-app interface. No extensions, no complexity — just open a chat and trade.

This is an introduction to what TONSWAP is building. For the complete technical breakdown, see our [TONSWAP Complete Guide](/tonswap-telegram-defi-hub-on-ton-complete-guide).

## What Is TONSWAP?

TONSWAP is a **DeFi super-app** for Telegram combining:

- **Concentrated Liquidity DEX (CLMM)** — More capital-efficient than traditional AMMs
- **Token Launchpad** — Create and launch tokens directly in Telegram
- **Perpetual Futures** — Up to 100× leverage, fully on-chain
- **LP Protection** — Insurance against prolonged impermanent loss

All accessible through a Telegram mini-app interface.

## The SORA Connection

TONSWAP isn't just another TON DEX — it's integrated with the [SORA ecosystem](/sora-ecosystem-explained).

**Bridge to SORA v3:** A trust-minimized bridge connects TONSWAP to SORA's Hub Chain, enabling:
- Access to SORA assets (XOR, KUSD)
- Shared liquidity with [Polkaswap](/exploring-sora-kensetsu-polkaswap)
- Cross-chain DeFi without leaving Telegram

**Economic Link:** 10% of TONSWAP's trading fee revenue is allocated to **buy back and burn XOR**, directly connecting TON trading activity to the SORA economy. More volume on TONSWAP = more XOR burned.

This creates a symbiotic relationship: TONSWAP gains access to SORA's liquidity and stablecoins, while SORA gains a distribution channel to Telegram's massive user base.

## How TONSWAP Compares

TON already has DEXs (STON.fi, DeDust). What makes TONSWAP different?

| Feature | STON.fi / DeDust | TONSWAP |
|---------|------------------|---------|
| AMM Type | Standard | Concentrated Liquidity (CLMM) |
| Launchpad | No | Yes (integrated) |
| Derivatives | No | Perps + Options |
| LP Protection | No | Parisian-style insurance |
| Cross-chain | Limited | SORA bridge |
| Risk Engine | No | Fully on-chain |

TONSWAP's thesis: existing TON DEXs optimize for traders. TONSWAP optimizes for **everyone** — including project launchers, LPs seeking protection, and users wanting derivatives.
