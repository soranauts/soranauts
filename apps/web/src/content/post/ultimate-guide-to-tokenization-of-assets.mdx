## What Is Asset Tokenization?

Tokenization transforms ownership rights of physical or digital items — real estate, art, commodities, securities — into **digital tokens recorded on a blockchain**.

Each token represents a verified share of the asset.  
Ownership becomes:

- **Divisible** — buy fractions instead of the whole asset  
- **Transferable** — send tokens globally in seconds  
- **Transparent** — public, verifiable transaction history  

Instead of owning an entire building, you can hold small, tradeable fractions — **ownership at internet speed**.

---

## How It Works

1. **Asset Selection** — A real-world asset is chosen (real estate, art, funds, commodities).  
2. **Smart Contract Setup** — Rules for ownership, compliance, and transfers are encoded.  
3. **Token Minting** — Digital tokens representing proportional shares are created.  
4. **Oracle Verification** — External data confirms real-world value and custody.  
5. **Trading & Settlement** — Tokens are instantly tradable across networks.

These steps bridge the gap between **traditional finance** and **DeFi**.
