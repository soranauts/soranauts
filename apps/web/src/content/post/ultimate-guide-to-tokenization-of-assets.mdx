## What Is Asset Tokenization?

**Asset tokenization** turns ownership of real-world items — from buildings to artwork to gold — into **digital tokens** recorded on a blockchain. Each token represents a verified portion of the underlying asset, protected by cryptography and governed by smart contracts.

This makes ownership more **secure, divisible, and transferable**. Instead of buying an entire building, investors can own **fractional shares** tradable on global markets — like **fractional ownership at internet speed**, powered by blockchain transparency.

---

## How It Works

1. **Asset Selection** – Identify a real-world asset such as real estate, art, or a fund.  
2. **Smart Contract Creation** – Encode ownership and compliance rules on-chain.  
3. **Token Generation** – Mint digital tokens representing proportional shares.  
4. **Verification via Oracles** – Systems like Chainlink confirm the asset’s real-world value or backing.  
5. **Trading & Settlement** – Tokens trade instantly on decentralized or regulated markets.  

Together, these steps bridge **traditional finance** and **DeFi**, enabling seamless movement of value across both worlds.
