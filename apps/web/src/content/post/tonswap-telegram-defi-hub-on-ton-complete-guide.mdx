## Core DeFi Features

### The T3 Liquidity Hub

T3 is a **composite stablecoin** (USDT + USDC + SORA’s KUSD). Every pool routes through T3 for one or two-hop swaps (Token A → T3 → Token B).

**Benefits:**
- Unified liquidity backbone  
- Minimal impermanent loss (volatility on one side only)  
- CEX-level execution on-chain  
- High capital efficiency via CLMM  

TONSWAP uses **Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker (CLMM)** technology. LPs allocate capital to price ranges, with prices set by TWAP oracles. Contracts are written in TOLK for speed and safety.

### Advanced Trading: Perpetuals & Options

**Perpetual Futures:** Up to 100× leverage, on-chain funding rates, transparent liquidation. Uses impact-weighted oracles to prevent manipulation.

**Options & Derivatives:** “Shout” options (lock early profits), outperformance contracts, volatility-linked perpetuals.  
**Settlement:** Always in T3. Positions are Jettons/NFTs—composable and ownable.

### Token Launchpad

Create tokens and run IDOs directly inside Telegram. When sales end, liquidity auto-seeds pools—**instant tradability.**

**Formats:**
- Fair price sales  
- Bonding-curve launches  
- Auctions  

A viral referral system amplifies growth—*one billion users, one billion tokens.*

### LP Safety: Parisian Range Protection

If pool price stays outside your range for 24+ hours, insurance triggers automatically. This protects LPs from prolonged price shocks without false positives.

## The Risk Engine: Autonomous On-Chain Protection

In October 2025, a flash crash liquidated ~$19B across markets, revealing how slow manual risk control can be. TONSWAP solves this with its **on-chain Risk Engine**—a fully autonomous system that acts in seconds, not hours.

**Risk Controller (RC):** Calculates a Global Risk Index (GRI) with four postures:
- **Green:** Normal  
- **Yellow:** Elevated risk  
- **Red:** High risk (tightens limits)  
- **Black:** Critical (pauses risky actions)

**Control Mesh:** Sends heartbeat updates adjusting:
- Trading fees  
- Leverage caps  
- Peg rates  
- Gas vault allocation  
- LP insurance parameters  

Algorithms include Peg Control, Fee Allocation, Gas PI Controller, Perps LQR, and Insurance CVaR.

**Two-Phase Authorization:** Every high-risk action is pre-checked. The RC logs intent, evaluates exposure, and issues cryptographic authorization receipts.

**Result:** The system tightens leverage, raises fees, and stabilizes markets—**instantly, on-chain, and without human input.**

| Feature | Traditional DeFi | TONSWAP Risk Engine |
|---------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Risk Detection** | Off-chain keepers | Fully on-chain |
| **Response Time** | Minutes–hours | Seconds |
| **Transparency** | Limited | Full on-chain logic |
| **Human Dependency** | Manual | Autonomous |
| **Risk Levels** | Binary (on/off) | Four-tier (Green→Black) |

$TS (Tonswap Token) powers TONSWAP’s economy with a strictly deflationary supply.

| Mechanism | Details | Purpose |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| **Total Supply** | 100M (70M launch, 30M incentives) | Fixed cap |
| **Buy-Back-and-Burn** | 0.3% swap fee buys & burns $TS | Continuous deflation |
| **Daily Burn/Re-Mint** | 90% re-minted, 10% burned forever | Net deflation |
| **Distribution (of 90%)** | 70% LPs · 10% DAO · 10% [XOR](/deep-dive-into-xor-val-and-pswap) buy-and-burn | Aligns with SORA |
| **Governance** | Community voting | DAO-controlled |

The [buy-and-burn model](/sora-xor-token-supply-explained) creates positive feedback: more trading → more burns → greater scarcity. The XOR allocation links TONSWAP to the [SORA ecosystem](/sora-ecosystem-explained), fusing two economies.
