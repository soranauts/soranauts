## Core DeFi Features

### The T3 Liquidity Hub

T3 is a **composite stablecoin** (USDT + USDC + SORA's KUSD). Every pool routes through T3 for one or two-hop swaps (Token A → T3 → Token B).

TONSWAP uses **Concentrated Liquidity Market Maker (CLMM)** technology. LPs allocate capital to price ranges, with prices set by TWAP oracles. Contracts are written in TOLK for speed and safety.

### Advanced Trading: Perpetuals & Options

**Perpetual Futures (planned):** Targeting up to 100× leverage with on-chain funding rates and transparent liquidation. Uses impact-weighted oracles to prevent manipulation.

**Options & Derivatives (planned):** "Shout" options, outperformance contracts, and volatility-linked perpetuals are under active development.
**Settlement:** Always in T3. Positions are Jettons/NFTs—composable and ownable.

### Token Launchpad

Create tokens and run IDOs directly inside Telegram. When sales end, liquidity auto-seeds pools—**instant tradability.**

**Formats:**
- Fair price sales
- Bonding-curve launches
- Auctions

A viral referral system amplifies growth—*one billion users, one billion tokens.*

### LP Safety: Parisian Range Protection

In the proposed design, if pool price stays outside your range for 24+ hours, insurance is intended to trigger automatically. This protects LPs from prolonged price shocks without false positives.

## The Risk Engine: Autonomous On-Chain Protection

Large-scale liquidation events in 2024–2025 revealed how slow manual risk control can be. TONSWAP solves this with its **on-chain Risk Engine**—a fully autonomous system that acts in seconds, not hours.

**Risk Controller (RC):** Calculates a Global Risk Index (GRI) with four postures:

<div class="not-prose my-4 flex flex-wrap gap-2">
  <span class="px-3 py-1 rounded-full bg-green-500/20 text-green-400 text-sm font-medium">🟢 Green: Normal</span>
  <span class="px-3 py-1 rounded-full bg-yellow-500/20 text-yellow-400 text-sm font-medium">🟡 Yellow: Elevated risk</span>
  <span class="px-3 py-1 rounded-full bg-red-500/20 text-red-400 text-sm font-medium">🔴 Red: High risk (tightens limits)</span>
  <span class="px-3 py-1 rounded-full bg-gray-700 text-gray-300 text-sm font-medium">⚫ Black: Critical (pauses risky actions)</span>
</div>

**Control Mesh:** Sends heartbeat updates adjusting:
- Trading fees
- Leverage caps
- Peg rates
- Gas vault allocation
- LP insurance parameters

Algorithms include Peg Control, Fee Allocation, Gas PI Controller, Perps LQR, and Insurance CVaR.

**Two-Phase Authorization:** Every high-risk action is pre-checked. The RC logs intent, evaluates exposure, and issues cryptographic authorization receipts.

**Result:** The system is designed to tighten leverage, raise fees, and stabilize markets—**autonomously and on-chain.**
