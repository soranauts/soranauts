## Overview of SORA Network

The SORA Network is creating a new way to handle money and investments, making them smarter and more connected. It uses advanced technology like blockchain to let people vote on decisions, swap different types of digital money easily, and even spend it with a special card.

### Tokens

SORA introduces unique crypto assets that play vital roles in its ecosystem. XOR acts as the network utility token, featuring an elastic supply guided by a bonding curve for price stability and fairness.

VAL, another critical token within SORA, serves as a reward for validators and has a deflationary nature to ensure value appreciation over time. PSWAP tokens incentivize liquidity providers on Polkaswap and are burned with each swap event, making it an essential asset for sustaining the decentralized finance (DeFi) platform's health.

> SORA tokens empower users by offering stability, rewards, and incentive mechanisms designed for growth in the DeFi landscape.

### PSWAP

Moving from the topic of tokens, let's talk about PSWAP. This digital currency plays a big role in the SORA network. It rewards people who provide liquidity on Polkaswap. Every time someone swaps tokens on Polkaswap, some PSWAP gets burned.

This helps keep the network stable and growing.

PSWAP is crucial for making the SORA ecosystem strong and competitive with usual economies. It encourages people to add liquidity, which is vital for any trading platform like Polkaswap to function smoothly.

Through this process, PSWAP ensures the long-term success of the SORA community by keeping it lively and resilient against market shifts.
