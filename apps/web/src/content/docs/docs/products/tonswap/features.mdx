## Concentrated Liquidity (CLMM)

TONSWAP implements concentrated liquidity market maker technology for superior capital efficiency.

### How CLMM Works

Traditional AMMs spread liquidity across all possible prices (0 to ∞). CLMM allows liquidity providers to concentrate their capital in specific price ranges.

**Example:**

For an ETH/USDC pool:
- **Traditional AMM**: Your liquidity covers $0 to $∞
- **CLMM**: You can focus on $1,800 - $2,200 where most trading occurs

### Benefits

| Benefit | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| **Higher Returns** | More fees earned per dollar deposited |
| **Lower Slippage** | Better prices for traders |
| **Active Management** | Optimize based on market conditions |
| **Capital Efficiency** | Up to 4000x improvement vs. traditional AMM |

### Managing Positions

As a CLMM liquidity provider:

1. **Set Range** — Choose min and max price for your liquidity
2. **Monitor** — Watch if price stays in your range
3. **Adjust** — Rebalance as market moves
4. **Collect** — Claim earned fees

<Aside type="note">
If price moves outside your range, you stop earning fees but don't lose funds. You can adjust your range at any time.
</Aside>

## XOR Buyback Integration

A unique feature connecting TONSWAP to the SORA economy:

### Mechanism

```
Trading Fees → 10% allocated to XOR buyback → XOR burned
```

### How It Works

1. User makes a trade on TONSWAP
2. Standard trading fee collected (e.g., 0.3%)
3. 10% of that fee set aside for XOR
4. Smart contract buys XOR from the market
5. Purchased XOR is permanently burned

### Impact

- **For XOR Holders**: Continuous buy pressure
- **For SORA Ecosystem**: Connected economies
- **For Traders**: Contributing to ecosystem health
- **Transparency**: All buybacks on-chain
