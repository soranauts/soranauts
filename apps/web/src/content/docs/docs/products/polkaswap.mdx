# Polkaswap

[Polkaswap](/glossary/polkaswap) is a next-generation, cross-chain liquidity aggregator decentralized exchange (DEX) built on the [SORA Network](/glossary/sora). Unlike traditional DEXs that rely on a single liquidity source, Polkaswap aggregates liquidity from multiple sources to provide users with the best possible prices.

## How Polkaswap Works

### Liquidity Sources

Polkaswap doesn't rely on a single liquidity pool. Instead, it aggregates from multiple sources:

| Source | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **XYK Pools** | Traditional constant-product (x*y=k) liquidity pools |
| **Token Bonding Curve** | SORA's algorithmic market maker for XOR |
| **Order Books** | Limit orders from advanced traders |
| **External DEXs** | Cross-chain liquidity from partner exchanges |

### Smart Routing Algorithm

When you make a trade on Polkaswap:

1. **Query All Sources** — The algorithm checks available liquidity across all sources
2. **Calculate Optimal Path** — Determines the best route considering fees and slippage
3. **Split Orders** — May split large orders across multiple sources
4. **Execute Atomically** — All parts of the trade complete or none do

This approach consistently delivers better prices than single-source DEXs.

## Liquidity Provision

### Why Provide Liquidity?

Liquidity providers (LPs) earn rewards from multiple sources:

- **Trading Fees** — 0.3% of every swap goes to LPs
- **PSWAP Rewards** — Additional [PSWAP](/glossary/pswap) token incentives
- **VAL Rewards** — [VAL](/glossary/val) from network transaction fees

### XYK Pools

[XYK pools](/glossary/xykpools) are constant-product market maker pools where:

```
x * y = k
```

Where `x` and `y` are the reserves of two tokens, and `k` is a constant. This formula ensures there's always liquidity available, with prices adjusting based on supply and demand.

### Impermanent Loss Protection

Polkaswap's multi-source liquidity aggregation helps reduce impermanent loss:

- Trades are routed optimally across sources
- Less reliance on any single pool
- More stable pricing from diverse liquidity

## PSWAP Token

[PSWAP](/glossary/pswap) is the reward token for Polkaswap liquidity providers.

### Tokenomics

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Max Supply | 10 billion PSWAP |
| Model | Deflationary (decreasing over time) |
| Fee Allocation | 0.3% of trades → buyback-and-burn |
| LP Rewards | Initially 90%, decreasing to 35% over 5 years |

### Reward Mechanics

Every trade on Polkaswap contributes to PSWAP distribution:

1. **Trading fees** are collected (0.3% per swap)
2. **Buyback** — Fees are used to buy PSWAP from the market
3. **Burn** — Purchased PSWAP is permanently burned
4. **Remint** — A percentage of burned tokens is reminted for LP rewards
5. **Distribute** — Rewards are distributed to liquidity providers

<Aside type="note">
PSWAP rewards follow a vesting schedule. Earned rewards are released over time to encourage long-term participation.
</Aside>

## Cross-Chain Bridges

Polkaswap connects to external blockchains through bridges:

### HASHI Bridge (Ethereum)

The [HASHI bridge](/glossary/hashi) enables trustless transfers between Ethereum and SORA:

- Move ETH and ERC-20 tokens to SORA
- Trade on Polkaswap with low fees
- Bridge back to Ethereum when needed

### Polkadot/Kusama

Direct integration with the Polkadot ecosystem:

- Access parachain tokens
- Benefit from shared security
- Cross-chain message passing

### TON Bridge

Connection to [TONSWAP](/glossary/tonswap) and the TON ecosystem:

- Trade TON-native assets
- XOR buyback mechanism
- Telegram integration
