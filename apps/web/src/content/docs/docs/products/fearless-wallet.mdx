# Fearless Wallet

[Fearless Wallet](/glossary/fearlesswallet) is a non-custodial mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future of the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystem. Developed by [SORAMITSU](/glossary/soramitsu) and funded by the Kusama Treasury, it provides a secure and user-friendly gateway to the SORA ecosystem and beyond.

## Why Fearless Wallet?

### Security First

Fearless Wallet is designed with security as the top priority:

- **Non-custodial** — Your private keys stay on your device
- **Open source** — Code is publicly auditable on GitHub
- **Direct node connection** — Communicates directly with blockchain nodes
- **No intermediaries** — No central servers that can be compromised

### User Experience

Despite its powerful features, Fearless Wallet is designed for everyone:

- **Clean interface** — Beautiful, intuitive design
- **Fast performance** — Optimized for mobile devices
- **Simple onboarding** — Create a wallet in minutes
- **Multi-language** — Available in multiple languages

## Getting Started

<Steps>
1. **Download the App**
   
   Available for iOS, Android, and Chrome browser:
   - [iOS App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089)
   - [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless)
   - [Chrome Web Store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/fearless-wallet)

2. **Create or Import a Wallet**
   
   - **New wallet**: Generate a new 12-word recovery phrase
   - **Import**: Use an existing phrase, raw seed, or JSON file

3. **Secure Your Recovery Phrase**
   
   Write down your 12-word phrase and store it safely. Never share it with anyone. If you lose this phrase, you cannot recover your wallet.

4. **Add Networks**
   
   Enable the networks you want to use (SORA, Polkadot, Kusama, etc.)

5. **Start Using DeFi**
   
   Send, receive, stake, and trade across all supported networks.
</Steps>

## Supported Features

### Asset Management

Manage all your crypto assets in one place:

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Send/Receive** | Transfer tokens to any address |
| **Multi-Account** | Manage multiple wallets |
| **Address Book** | Save frequently used addresses |
| **Transaction History** | View all past transactions |
| **Price Tracking** | See real-time token values |

### Staking

Earn rewards by staking your tokens:

- **Nominate validators** — Choose who secures the network
- **View rewards** — Track your staking earnings
- **Compound** — Reinvest rewards automatically
- **Unstake** — Withdraw when needed (subject to unbonding periods)

Supported staking networks:
- SORA (XOR, VAL)
- Polkadot (DOT)
- Kusama (KSM)
- Parachains (various)

### DApp Browser

Connect to decentralized applications:

- **[Polkaswap](/glossary/polkaswap)** — Trade on SORA's DEX
- **NFT Marketplaces** — Buy and sell digital collectibles
- **DeFi Protocols** — Access lending, borrowing, and more
- **Governance** — Vote on network proposals

<Aside type="tip">
When connecting to DApps, always verify the URL to avoid phishing attacks.
</Aside>

## Security Best Practices

<Aside type="caution" title="Protect Your Wallet">
Follow these security guidelines to keep your funds safe.
</Aside>

### Do's

- ✅ Write down your recovery phrase on paper
- ✅ Store the phrase in multiple secure locations
- ✅ Enable biometric authentication (Face ID, fingerprint)
- ✅ Keep your app updated
- ✅ Verify addresses before sending

### Don'ts

- ❌ Never screenshot your recovery phrase
- ❌ Never store your phrase digitally (cloud, email, notes)
- ❌ Never share your phrase with anyone
- ❌ Never enter your phrase on websites
- ❌ Don't click suspicious links or connect to unknown DApps

## Wallet Management

### Multiple Accounts

Manage different wallets for different purposes:

- **Main wallet** — Primary holdings
- **Trading wallet** — Active DeFi use
- **Staking wallet** — Long-term staking
- **Hardware wallet** — Ledger integration

### Backup & Recovery

Your wallet can be recovered with:

- **Mnemonic phrase** — 12 or 24 words
- **Raw seed** — Hexadecimal private key
- **JSON file** — Encrypted backup

<Aside type="danger">
If you lose your recovery phrase and don't have any backup, your funds are permanently lost. There is no recovery option.
</Aside>
