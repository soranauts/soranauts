# SORA Card

[SORA Card](/glossary/soracard) is a decentralized debit card that bridges the gap between your cryptocurrency holdings and real-world purchases. Spend your [XOR](/glossary/xor) and other SORA ecosystem tokens at any merchant that accepts card payments—bringing the benefits of decentralized finance to everyday life.

## How It Works

SORA Card makes spending cryptocurrency as simple as using a traditional debit card:

<Steps>
1. **Hold Your Crypto**
   
   Keep XOR, VAL, PSWAP, or other supported tokens in your wallet.

2. **Link Your Wallet**
   
   Connect your wallet to the SORA Card service.

3. **Make a Purchase**
   
   Tap or swipe your card at any supported merchant.

4. **Automatic Conversion**
   
   Your crypto is converted to local currency at the current rate.

5. **Instant Settlement**
   
   The merchant receives fiat; you've spent crypto seamlessly.
</Steps>

## The Problem SORA Card Solves

### Traditional Crypto Spending

Without SORA Card, spending crypto typically requires:

1. Sell crypto on an exchange
2. Wait for funds to settle
3. Withdraw to bank account
4. Wait for bank transfer
5. Finally spend via traditional card

**Total time: 3-7 days**

### With SORA Card

1. Swipe your SORA Card

**Total time: Instant**

<Aside type="note">
SORA Card eliminates the friction between holding crypto and spending in the real world.
</Aside>

## Self-Custody Model

SORA Card uses a unique self-custody model:

### How Traditional Crypto Cards Work

- You deposit crypto to the card provider
- They hold your funds (custodial)
- Counterparty risk if provider fails
- Not your keys, not your coins

### How SORA Card Works

- Your crypto stays in your wallet
- You maintain full control
- Only converted at point of sale
- True self-custody until spending

This approach maintains the core crypto principle: **your keys, your coins**—until the moment you choose to spend.

## Getting SORA Card

### Requirements

To get a SORA Card, you'll need:

- A verified identity (KYC)
- A [Fearless Wallet](/glossary/fearlesswallet) or compatible wallet
- Supported tokens for spending

### KYC Process

<Steps>
1. **Start Application**
   
   Begin the KYC process in the SORA Card portal.

2. **Identity Verification**
   
   Provide government-issued ID and proof of address.

3. **Review Period**
   
   Application reviewed (typically 1-3 business days).

4. **Card Delivery**
   
   Physical card shipped to your address.

5. **Activation**
   
   Activate your card and link your wallet.
</Steps>

<Aside type="caution">
KYC is required for regulatory compliance. Your data is handled according to strict privacy standards.
</Aside>
