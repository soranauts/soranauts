# SORA 2024 Year in Review

**Published:** December 2024

The year 2024 marked a transformative period for the SORA ecosystem. From product launches to technical innovations and governance evolution, this review captures the key milestones that shaped SORA's trajectory.

## Executive Summary

2024 was defined by three major themes:

1. **Real-World Adoption** — SORA Card launch and mainstream usability
2. **Technical Foundation** — Preparations for SORA v3 (Nexus)
3. **Ecosystem Expansion** — New products, partnerships, and community growth

---

## Major Milestones

### Q1: SORA Card Launch

The biggest news of the year came early:

- **March** — [SORA Card](/docs/archive/2024/sora-card-launch) launched
- First self-custody crypto spending card
- European markets initial rollout
- Thousands of early adopters

### Q2: DEX Improvements

[Polkaswap](/glossary/polkaswap) received significant upgrades:

- Enhanced liquidity aggregation
- Improved smart routing efficiency
- New trading pair additions
- Mobile experience refinements

### Q3: Infrastructure Scaling

Technical improvements across the ecosystem:

- Network stability enhancements
- Bridge performance optimizations
- Reduced transaction fees
- Validator set expansion

### Q4: Nexus Preparations

Groundwork for SORA v3:

- [Fujiwara Testnet](/glossary/fujiwara-testnet) expanded testing
- Iroha 2 prototypes validated
- Migration planning initiated
- Whitepaper drafting began
