# SORA Nexus Glossary V2025 — Press Kit

**One-Pager for Partners & Media**

---

## What Is It?

The **SORA Nexus Glossary** is a comprehensive, interactive learning platform for the SORA decentralized economy ecosystem. It provides instant access to 179 curated terms covering tokenomics, governance, DeFi mechanics, and blockchain infrastructure.

---

## The Problem

Decentralized finance is complex. Users exploring SORA face:

- **Information overload** — Hundreds of terms, concepts, and mechanisms
- **Fragmented learning** — Definitions scattered across docs, wikis, and forums
- **Context switching** — Constant page navigation interrupts understanding
- **No guided paths** — Newcomers don't know where to start

---

## The Solution

The Nexus Glossary V2025 introduces:

### 🔍 Quick-View Previews
Preview any term instantly without leaving your current page. Hover, click, learn—then continue where you left off.

### 🧭 Learning Journeys
Curated paths guide users through related concepts: DeFi Basics, Governance 101, Token Economics, and more.

### ⚡ Instant Performance
62% smaller payloads and lazy loading mean the glossary loads fast on any connection.

### ♿ Full Accessibility
WCAG AA+ compliant with keyboard navigation, screen reader support, and reduced motion options.

---

## Why SORA Needs This

SORA is building a new kind of decentralized economy with:

- **XOR** — The native utility token with elastic supply
- **Polkaswap** — A non-custodial DEX with cross-chain capabilities
- **Token Bonding Curve** — Algorithmic price stability
- **SORA Parliament** — Decentralized governance

Understanding these concepts is essential for:
- Developers building on SORA
- Community members participating in governance
- DeFi users managing liquidity
- Newcomers exploring the ecosystem

The Nexus Glossary makes this knowledge accessible to everyone.
