### You Are Here

The Summer of SORA is hot and in full swing, and we are here to bring you up to speed with the latest developments. It is daunting to see how the notion of digital currencies, backed by the effort of grinding and toiling, not unlike World of Warcraft Gold, has progressed to the point where it is close to replacing what many call “real currencies.” We say close because many crypto projects have lost the plot and no longer focus on giving freedom and empowering people, but have stopped shy of the finish line to become more akin to stonks than money, with the news of cryptocurrencies going beyond the DEX to the most unsafe CEX of them all. The trend of cryptocurrency Exchange Traded Funds that started with Bitcoin and currently has Ethereum waiting in the lobby has been considered a great success for many cryptocurrency supporters, the same crowd that acquires a token and joins the community demanding an increase of value to their investment is also the one who is currently dumping bitcoin for all it’s worth on the heels of news like [Mt. Gox refunds](https://decrypt.co/236723/bitcoin-dives-to-61000-as-mt-gox-says-btc-repayments-begin-next-week)…

Indeed the bigger the market, the papier the hands as they say, but before this section devolves into something akin to financial advice, the moral of this story is that SORA doesn’t have that goal. Instead, the SORA community seeks to foster a new blockchain-enabled economy that values work and production over debt. Perhaps the lack of instruments to cause crippling debt is correlated to the lack of widespread mainstream adoption of commercial banks, but this is off the record, so don’t quote us on that.

Production-based economies are scarce nowadays. What began as funding for agriculture then became funding for industry until _muh shareholders_ decided that in order to maximise profits, processes must be optimised to the point where all the fat had to be trimmed, leading to communities ravaged and the proliferation of ghost towns that were former industry havens because paying fair wages shrinks the bottom line. This predatory practice has led the world to the current state of affairs, looking to flip coins with cats, dogs, and no utility, only to immediately dump them to pay that hefty credit card bill. On the upside, people are now more in tune with finance and no longer see it as a task left to a bro in a business suit.

This is different from the outlook that SORA seeks to achieve. Through hard work, the SORA community believes that productive output is the best source of value for a currency, although the SORA v2 tokenomics have taught us that a hodlr’s biggest fear is inflation. Lesson learnt, as the transition to SORA v3 is in full steam and no new TBCD is being emitted.

The KARMA token for chameleon pools ended the burn2earn period on the 6th of June, with 12,319,952,317 XOR burnt and 129.199 KARMA created and distributed by governance.

The Kensetsu platform was launched last month, with over 300K collateral locked so far. The system still has some upgrades to go, so please look forward to these improvements in the coming weeks. On the other hand, more assets are being added to the order book, including the TON token and the Liberland Dollar!

Work is ongoing to open HRMP channels to other Polkadot and Kusama networks. The community rallied together and lobbied to change the requirements for opening a channel to common-good infrastructure, removing the need for politics, which, as we all know, are loaded with immaturity and greed that are obstacles to the growth of a network. Unfortunately, although the governance approved the connection, there was an unforeseen issue as the upper limit of parachain connections to the Asset Hub was reached, meaning that SORA could not be added as the slots were full… Fortunately, a runtime upgrade to Polkadot will allow more connections, and the SORA channel will go operational, as this also implements the removal of an OpenGov proposal to connect to this common good infrastructure.

Upgrading the Polkadot SDK will also allow the Hashi Bridge to become BEEFier by transitioning to a trustless model, facilitating accurate burning for XOR in transactions, and reducing the constant governance processes required.

### About SORA

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is an innovative, adaptive, non-debt-based monetary framework that facilitates economic stability, particularly for financially vulnerable countries. As a groundbreaking platform, SORA exemplifies the potential of blockchain technology in fostering a more inclusive and stable global financial system. Its integration in projects like the Bokolo Cash CBDC signifies a new era in digital currency, aligning technological advancements to achieve broader economic stability and resilience. Through its unique approach and capabilities, SORA plays a pivotal role in the evolution of global finance, particularly by supporting economies that need innovative and sustainable financial solutions the most.
