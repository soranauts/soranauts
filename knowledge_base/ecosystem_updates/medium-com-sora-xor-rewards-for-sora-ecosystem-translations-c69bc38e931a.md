SORA New Translation Round Announcement! 🌉
-------------------------------------------

Dear SORA community:

We would like to announce that you can start translating the SORA, Capital (generic mobile library used in the SORA app), and Fearless Wallet apps from English into your own language! 👨🏻‍💻

In order to join the translation team, please fill out this form:

➡ [**Apply here**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/17ZXmdYYWthcXJsOZ8uRCGVjVEPwrGAtF5een_QVdQs0/edit)

Press enter or click to view image in full size

✨ It was decided to reward your contributions with VAL, this amount will change every 1–2 months, and you will be notified about changes.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

🏆 Currently the rewards for each language for translations **completed** in February are:

* **162 VAL** for SORA and Capital projects (**146 VAL** for translators, **16 VAL** for the reviewer)
* **77 VAL** for the Fearless Wallet project (**66 VAL** for translators, **11 VAL** for the reviewer)

> _❗Users will receive the reward allocated_ **_at the time_** _of full completion of the translation._ **_Please note that the reward allocation is not based on the time when the translation was started._** _If you finished your translation but it was not reviewed yet, and the reward changes — after the review you will receive the bigger one of 2 rewards._

📌 The translation is considered complete when all the phrases have been translated and also reviewed by another user. Each translation team requires a **Reviewer**, who will certify the final quality and accuracy of the translations. For this extra effort, 10% of the VAL allocated for each project are reserved for Reviewers. Everyone, Revisors included, can contribute as a **Translator.** In case there are several Translators, they will split the 90% of the allocated VAL, based on how many words they translated.

Let’s work together to make SORA and related apps easier for people all over the world!
