### You Are Here

Yes, it is incredible to think we are already halfway through 2025. In this past half a year, ongoing wars have reached critical mass, ceasefires have been attempted and failed, and a potential world war has been teased, but we keep on building.

You might point out that enriching the Substrate hasn’t been a topic, and you’d be right. Right now, we are working on updating the SORA Kusama Parachain runtime for JAM (coretime) compatibility. We apologize to the few users with stuck bridge transactions — due to scarce coretime (seemingly hoarded in the secondary market — an amazing system), those KSMs are temporarily in limbo. We’re monitoring closely and securing coretime, especially with sales starting July 21. Stay tuned for parachain and bridge updates.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Meanwhile, we’re reducing validator reward claim fees to 1/10th of what they are now, enabling more frequent claims to boost nominators and node runners.

Moving on to ongoing work, iterations of the SORA wallet supporting partner initiatives are being built and tested. There’s promising potential for the network’s infrastructure and self-custodial wallet with partners, though details must remain confidential for now — no spoilers today. Meanwhile, SORA Card has made significant progress. Users can now swap ETH, BTC, USDC, and more within the SORA Card app to and from fiat, making this the most robust on/off-ramp service available. Unfortunately, some users have attempted to use this service with malicious intent, but they’ve been quickly identified, leading to enhanced security measures. This has resulted in some delays in onboarding, offboarding, and receiving transfers due to case-by-case reviews. Rest assured, these measures exist for your security and everyone’s safety and compliance. You could bypass these hassles by using XOR, though the powers that be are still in the process of becoming SORA-pilled. Finally, you can expect XOR swaps within the app later this year.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

That was it for updates on SORA v2, but there is significant work ongoing for SORA v3. People have approached us and asked: “Why do all this? Who wants to use CBDC anyway? Isn’t it just a way for the government to control how you spend your money?” Well, Greg, this isn’t a credit card service, so you can relax about your dirty laundry being aired in public. We are focused on the bigger picture.

This update began with a brief recap of the geopolitical situation we currently face. Political and religious leadership, armies, hearts, minds, and opinions are all divided on taking sides in the various ongoing conflicts. But who is left without resources on the ground? While headlines cover enriched uranium and weapons concessions, real people are being left out of the system.

Whichever side they are on, they are victims of the choices being made for them and one way or another have been left out of the system. They own nothing and, unfortunately for Klaus are not happy. They follow the guidelines drafted by Carstens (every day is Taco Tuesday), who has the interests of big banking as a priority. This is who we are building SORA v3 for. It is not for the moonboy or the daytrader or the broker shilling ETFs to pensioners, it is for the person who’s money has become so worthless, they cannot even afford to subsist, let alone overcome. The world needs a system like SORA V3 because instead of getting bogged down in the details of yield earned, it is the difference between having a meal, getting medical assistance, or even being able to travel to school.

There are enough shitcoins and networks bringing temporary satisfaction to degenerates reading candles, we want to make a concrete difference bridging the gaps that the legacy system and the ease it has to succumb to external influences affect the lives of real people. Revolution can’t happen when there is prosperity, but that ship has sailed and it is time to rise up and make a positive change for those who have been left behind. Moving forward, development will mostly focus on making the goal of SORA v3 a reality. Take that however suits you best within your strategy and focus, but remember there are many out there who aren’t as lucky.

You’re now caught up on the latest developments in the SORA ecosystem and the SORA Integrated Plan. Stay tuned for more updates from our contributors — we’ll be back with more news soon. **Now, let’s get back to work!**

### About SORA

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is an innovative, adaptive, non-debt-based monetary framework that facilitates economic stability, particularly for financially vulnerable countries. As a groundbreaking platform, SORA exemplifies the potential of blockchain technology in fostering a more inclusive and stable global financial system. Its integration in projects like the Bokolo Cash CBDC signifies a new era in digital currency, aligning technological advancements to achieve broader economic stability and resilience. Through its unique approach and capabilities, SORA plays a pivotal role in the evolution of global finance, particularly by supporting economies that need innovative and sustainable financial solutions the most.
