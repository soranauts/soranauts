**Many worlds, one economy.**

The Good Doctor said once “there are weeks where decades happen” and last week is a prime example. On one hand, the Web3 Foundation’s legal officer attended the House Committee hearing on [The Future of Digital Assets](https://www.congress.gov/event/118th-congress/house-event/115885?s=1&r=1), where this interesting [snippet](https://np.reddit.com/r/StockMarket/comments/13e12mn/crypto_bros_made_over_a_trillion_dollars_out_of/) was dropped.

Congressman Brad Sherman mentions that the US government “mints money out of thin air,” which is pretty inaccurate (the Federal Reserve is not part of the government and on top of that, 98% of USD are liability of commercial banks), but some similarities can be drawn between Mr. Sherman’s statement and many people who are shocked to hear that there is no maximum supply for the XOR token.

The SORA economy is based on increasing the supply of the XOR token to fund goods and services, and if these services are productive, the utility of XOR increases due to this intrinsic link. The way speculative cryptocurrencies are valued (not XOR), require tokens to have scarcity to increase in value; they are assigned some form of utility, such as participation in governance, but this is mostly symbolic because there is not much utility you can give a token with a limited supply. You can’t enable the production of goods and services at scale with a limited supply of tokens, because when you reach the max, there is no more production (ΔM = ΔP + ΔY).

While many [currencies inflate their supply](https://youtu.be/KEfdlctvHH4) through loans (_many of which are not productive, which affects the people’s purchasing power_), the SORA economy increases the tokens in circulation to funds services, such as the development of the SORA blockchain infrastructure, which benefits the entire SORA community by creating more utility for SORA, and beyond, as many of the innovations developed through this system also benefit the DOTSAMA ecosystem as a whole.

Back to the weeks in which decades happen, the [SORA Substrate backend was upgraded](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/releases/tag/1.10.0), allowing for better and more efficient development of innovative features, as well as a streamlined release cycle.

SORA Network Upgrade v1.10

This update also brings the order book skeleton to the LiquidityProxy and one step closer to the creation of bid/ask market orders. Advanced traders can look forward to all their favourite order book features on SORA once development is complete.

Stability is key for traders, and we are happy to inform great progress on the XST Synthetics platform. Swaps will have a totally different liquidity source when synthetic assets are involved, so plenty of work is going into making sure that this system works without a hitch, test to make sure that there are no issues and once that is ready, we will send it over to the oracle partner for further testing. This platform needs to be flawless, so we appreciate your ongoing patience and look forward to releasing this feature soon.

Moving on to bridges, the Substrate bridge that will connect the SORA mainnet to the SORA Kusama parachain needed some extra work under the hood, as the [BEEFY](https://github.com/paritytech/grandpa-bridge-gadget) dependencies were not ready, so the transport layer needed to be reworked. Fortunately, the backend implementation is mostly ready and the focus is on a simple, yet intuitive and powerful bridge UI.

The trustless multi-EVM bridge has great progress as well. After much insisting, we managed to get some interesting alpha from the developers that has not been shared before, but let’s keep this between us… The trustless multi-EVM bridge will be running in parallel with the current Hashi bridge, and will feature connections to the BSC and Klaytn networks out in the near term, with the possibility to increase its reach to other EVM-based networks. Users will not be aware that they are using a different bridge, the interface will be so stylish and free that they will be able to bring their liquidity into the SORA network, even if they have never used a Substrate-based blockchain before.

The key to this is a great wallet to make adoption easier for new users and simplify complicated processes for existing ones. The new SORA Wallet release is due to be available for download on your device very soon.

This update covers essential UX improvements and a stunning wallet redesign to ensure you have the best possible experience. We understand the importance of addressing bugs promptly, and this update covers several issues to enhance stability and overall performance.

We are constantly striving to meet your needs and expectations, and the next release has something truly exciting in store for you: SORA Card sign-ups!

We know that many of you have been eagerly awaiting this feature, and we are actively working to make it available as soon as possible. In our commitment to delivering a seamless user experience, however, we had to prioritize resolving essential bugs and implementing UX improvements in this update. As soon as the banking partners provide us the necessary credentials, SORA Card signups will be available.

Moving on to Fearless Wallet developments, Polkaswap has been available for swaps already, but a lot of work is going into implementing liquidity provision features. Meanwhile, XCM implementations, SORA Card integration, as well as EVM compatibility, and overall improvements to Fearless Wallet are in the pipeline. Other improvements such as SORA and Ternoa native staking are in the final phases of testing and will be available soon.

Finally, there was an open mic event on the SORA Daily Twitter account where your favourite SORA contributors (other than yourself) took to the stage and answered community questions, and gave updates on different projects being worked on. Tune in with this [link](https://twitter.com/i/spaces/1yNxaNvoPyqKj?s=20) in case you missed it or if you want to hear it again.

About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is a new economic order for the world, aimed at high growth and empowering human progress. The SORA network includes interoperability with the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) networks, as well as the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a non-custodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem. It offers boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
