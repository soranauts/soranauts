October 17, 2020, is the 1 year anniversary of the SORA network launch. To celebrate, we want to focus on what’s really important in life — **memes**. That’s right, we are going to hold a **meme contest!**

Press enter or click to view image in full size

#SoraMEME

Meme Contest Prizes
-------------------

* First place memes: 625 VAL tokens and 6 cans of [Noir](https://drinknoir.com) (if shipping is available, otherwise 200 VAL will be given)
* Second place memes: 500 VAL tokens and 3 cans of [Noir](https://drinknoir.com) (if shipping is available, otherwise 100 VAL will be given)
* Third place memes: 300 VAL tokens

Further 10 memes will be rewarded with 50 VAL tokens by our memetic researchers

**The winners will receive the VAL tokens after the launch of VAL in November.**

Timeline
--------

Start: Today (October 17th, 2020)

End: All submissions are due at 23:59 UTC on October 24th, 2020

Winners announcement: October 31st, 2020

Twitter Submission Guidelines
-----------------------------

1. Each contribution must submit its meme on Twitter
2. **Please tag #SORAmeme,** [**sora.org**](http://sora.org/)**,** [**polkaswap.io**](http://polkaswap.io/)**, and** [**fearlesswallet.io**](http://fearlesswallet.io/) **in your submission tweet**

**Judging ends on October 31st and the prize winners will be announced on the** [**SORA Twitter**](https://twitter.com/sora_xor) **page.**

Rules
-----

* Be creative, have fun, meme something extraordinary
* Submit as many memes as you want and like
* Memes should be related to [SORA](http://www.sora.org/), [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) and/or [Fearless Wallet](http://www.fearlesswallet.io/)
* Make funny memes
* Do _not_ use the memes created by SORA Memetic Warfare Telegram channel ([https://t.me/soramemes](https://t.me/soramemes))
* Please make sure not to include NSFW, harmful, or illegal content. Also hateful or racist content is not accepted.

Branding Guidelines
-------------------

Memes are about fun and creativity foremost, we don’t expect you sticking to corporate branding guidelines in this case — you can download the SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet brand assets here and use them as you please: [https://github.com/sora-xor/sora-branding](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora-branding)

Judging
-------

Our memetic engineers will judge on creativity, design, humor, and uniqueness. 13 memes will be selected for the prize and will be chosen by the 4 criteria (creativity, design, humor and uniqueness) to be the winners. All memes must use the “**Submission Requirements**” to be eligible for prizes. The memes must be SORA, Polkaswap, or Fearless Wallet-related and must comply with the rules above.

About SORA NEO Network, Polkaswap, and Fearless
-----------------------------------------------

[**SORA NEO (New Economic Order)**](https://medium.com/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new world economic system aimed at creating a supranational, decentralized central bank, featuring a pre-built toolkit for decentralized finance (DeFi). SORA v2 is built on [Substrate](https://web3-technology-stack.readthedocs.io/en/latest/layer_1/protocols/substrate/) and is preparing to become a [Kusama and Polkadot parachain](https://medium.com/@polkaswap/sora-network-parachain-for-polkaswap-intro-859558753f48). One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a non-custodial AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity enabled by groundbreaking **Liquidity Aggregation Technology (LAT)**, with the security and convenience of a DEX.

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Kusama network, with support for iOS and Android platforms. An awesome user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. The Fearless wallet will integrate Polkaswap for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
