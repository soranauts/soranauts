**Many worlds, one economy.**

A man walks into a bank and requests to know the total supply of the local currency. The bank teller, perplexed, reminds the man that currencies have varying supplies and that fixed-circulation currencies are not viable for funding things “in the real world.” The value of a currency is increased by producing goods and services, so the more it is used to create value, the more it is worth; money has to “work.”

The bank teller had to get back to work, examining every detail to ensure that the SORA card would be bulletproof. Taking no risks, everything must be approved before anyone can sign up. Waiting for approval allows us to focus on other things. The SORA Kusama parachain bridge will be available for testing soon, and shortly thereafter, it will have a UI on [test.polkaswap.io](http://test.polkaswap.io/).

Press enter or click to view image in full size

The SORA network has reached a significant milestone, crossing over 10 MILLION blocks recently

Great progress is being made on implementing the Orderbook, as part of the [RFP](https://github.com/sora-xor/rfps/pull/21) which includes advanced trading features and technology advances at an astonishing speed. Also, the XST synthetic asset platform is now almost ready for integration.

“Excuse me,” said the teller, “since you’re dealing with a tool as important as money, the pain of waiting does not compare to the pain of losing.” She’s right. The pain that any issues would cause is significant, so it’s worth doing things right. The XST platform will undergo rigorous QA before being available on the mainnet, so we hope you’re looking forward to it; your cooperation will also be needed to help test on the testnet.

On the Polkaswap front, we are currently working on the UI for the Trustless EVM bridge, following the completion of the work for the Substrate bridge. We are also improving the user experience on Polkaswap with style enhancements that adapt to SORA’s increasing capabilities. Polkaswap will become a powerful center of liquidity that is easy to use and intuitive, with an even easier UI, new pools and tokenomics, support for multiple bridges, and the integration of SORA card and Orderbook features. It will be like a liquidity Lambo.

On the wallet front, SORA Card mobile signups are revving up, waiting for the green light. “Excuse me, miss, how long did you say this would take?”

Unfortunately, the freedom to have control over your finances in the palm of your hand, whether in fiat or cryptocurrencies, comes with many responsibilities. Every line must be scrutinized to detect and prevent any issues.

The Fearless Wallet ethos is that everyone should have access to their finances freely and fearlessly. That’s why we’re integrating SORA Card functionality, so you can access your assets on more networks and use them easily with your SORA Card in real life. Additionally, the latest release of Fearless Wallet features a mobile dApp signer. Please note that this is an experimental feature, and contact [Fearless Happiness](https://t.me/fearlesshappiness) if you encounter any issues (and remember that anyone who DMs you first on telegram is a scammer, so block and report them).

The Fearless Wallet team is also working hard on SORA and Ternoa native staking integrations. The Fearless Wallet browser extension is working on a new release that will include Polkaswap swaps and UI improvements based on your feedback. Thanks!

We look forward to new and innovative ways of accessing revolutionary technologies. SORA is the only cryptocurrency in the world that has a rational and active monetary policy and this makes SORA the only viable system to compete with traditional economic systems. This is quite different from the tokenomics considered in other cryptocurrencies. XOR is intended to be used and recognized as legal tender, the same as the kind in your pocket and bank account, to fund the production of goods and services.

If this is the first time you’re reading this section, please [take the red pill](https://sora.org/pub/The-Case-for-XOR.pdf), then [contact us](https://discord.gg/4TXRN6Y4gb) in the morning at [SORA’s Telegram group](https://t.me/sora_xor).

About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is a new economic order for the world, aimed at high growth and empowering human progress. The SORA network includes interoperability with the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) networks, as well as the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a non-custodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem. It offers boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
