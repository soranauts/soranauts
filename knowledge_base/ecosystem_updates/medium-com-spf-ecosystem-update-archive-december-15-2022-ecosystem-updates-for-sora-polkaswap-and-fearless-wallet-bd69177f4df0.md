The SORA Integrated Plan [https://sora.org/plan](https://sora.org/plan)

We **are halfway through the last month of 2022, but barely scratching the surface of the great things being worked on as part of the SORA Integrated Plan. This is as good a time as any to remember that the ultimate goal of SORA is to be a New Economic World Order, as the existing one is corrupt and broken. Indeed, this may sound utopic or even naïve, but all it takes is individual acceptance, one user at a time. This is easier said than done, of course, as we are all traumatised by the current system and therefore seek individual security before considering a system that is beneficial to the majority.**

**However, it takes only a few people to adopt SORA as the new system and gradually we can all work our way up to becoming the new status quo… _SORA is built to deliver financial inclusion for all, without discrimination_, we have this mantra on the** [**very first page of the SORA Wiki**](https://wiki.sora.org/master)**, and it is the goal and motivation that keeps the entire community working hard to provide the tools and infrastructure that will make this vision a reality. Now that we are on the same page, here’s what’s been going on for the past two weeks:**

Dr Makoto Takemiya explaining the core ideas behind the SORA decentralized economic system at the annual Web Summit technology conference in Lisbon last month. Find out how and why appropriate monetary policy for money allocation in an economy for productive output is the key to sustainable economic growth and how SORA visions a decentralized governance system combined with token allocation to achieve this, and more!

The transport layer for the SORA Kusama parachain bridge is almost complete. There is business logic being planned that will make SORA the obvious decentralized finance hub of the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems. Hard work is being done to reiterate the “Polka” in [Polkaswap.io](https://polkaswap.io) as we set up the parachain campaign for a Polkadot parachain crowdloan. If Polkadot and its DOT token are the “software” behind the network, Polkaswap aims to be the go-to liquidity hub for the ecosystem, where users can find all parachain, common good, and solo chain tokens available from across DOTSAMA.

Polkaswap will be the place where tokens are valued and traded with style and freedom. You won’t need a centralized exchange, as you will have the power to provide liquidity, stake, market make, and even mint your own tokens and NFTs! This way, the DOTSAMA ecosystem will stand out from other blockchain networks as a pioneer in DeFi that does not end in exit collateral, and with the imminent launch of the SORA card, you will be able to bridge the gap between crypto and fiat, through a decentralized and self-custodial platform to manage your assets — no Sam or CZ to play with your funds!

Professor Richard Werner and Ben Senn (Head of Strategy at Valhalla Network), together with Dr Makoto Takemiya, tackle the question of central planning vs decentralized governance, financial speculation and boom-bust cycles, regional banking and local lending, and how to use credit creation for productive outcomes achieving sustainable high economic growth, and how it all can be environmentally friendly. At the annual Web Summit technology conference in Lisbon last month.

Order book functionality is also under development, so advanced functions will be available soon for those market makers who have been waiting for better and more efficient platforms, while work on the XST platform is steadily progressing. XST is very exciting, as it will be possible to implement algorithmic assets pegged to real-world currencies and commodities while being backed by the XST token. Through governance, users will be able to propose new assets to be added within [Polkaswap.io](http://polkaswap.io/); think of XSTRUB, XSTEUR, or even XSTAU!

XSTUSD-USDT and XSTUSD-DAI pools, with more XSTUSD pools to come, now available on [Polkaswap.io](https://polkaswap.io)

More information on the XST platform will be released soon in the form of an article, but in the meantime, the XSTUSD-USDT, XSTUSD-DAI, XSTUSD-BUSD, and XSTUSD-USDC pools, with more XSTUSD-backed pools to come, have just been made available on Polkaswap.io and Ceres Liquidity locker, and can be monitored through [Ceres Tools](https://tools.cerestoken.io/pairs). There is also a change underway for XSTUSD backing from XOR to XST, which is coming soon. It’s worth remembering that XST liquidity providers receive seigniorage from every XST mint done, that even [Lord Rothschild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayer_Amschel_Rothschild) or [John Rockefeller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_D._Rockefeller) would be jealous of.\*

We will keep you up to date with more news as its release draws closer. As if that were not all, the Fearless Wallet team is also working to make the Polkaswap swapping experience fearlessly stylish! ### **SORA Card Update on Pre-Registrations**

Dear Community,

More than anyone, the SORA Card team is excited to release Phase 1: Pre-Registrations. We are moving it slightly up the calendar to either by the end of this year or very early in January. The main reason for this is something we care about highly: the security of your finances and data.

Before releasing the new application publicly we want to finalize a complete security audit of the whole newly built solution. We work with multiple 3rd parties, and although everything is looking very promising, our unique product requires quite some customized solutions.

As an example, it was the first time our partners worked on a solution where the client (SORA dApp) did not want to have any data of the users. This feature differentiates us from other crypto card solutions and we overcame a couple of challenges to safeguard your privacy.

Next to this, SORA wallet has undergone an entirely new design that prepares it for all planned future features to be added in style and with great user experience above all. It made sense to us to release the new wallet design together with SORA Card integration.

Furthermore, and this is a recurring theme: Substrate networks take some extra research and work since most developers outside of the Polkadot ecosystem have little to no experience with the technology. We are grateful to see an uptrend curve when it comes to the number of developers in the ecosystem, over time Substrate will hopefully become more the standard.

We are excited to share the release of Phase 1 with you very soon at SORA quality standards. Nothing less.

### About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a noncustodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile and desktop wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystem, with native support for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) mobile platforms, as well as desktop platforms. A premium user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
