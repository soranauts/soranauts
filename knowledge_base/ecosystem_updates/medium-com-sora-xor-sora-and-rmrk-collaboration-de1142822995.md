TL;DR
-----

* [RMRK](https://www.rmrk.app) recently announced a partnership with SORA to implement RMRK pallets on the SORA network
* The scope of the partnership includes a reward from RMRK in the form of a special Kanaria NFT item for the top 100 SORA crowdloan contributors
* If you already have a Kanaria bird, while being in the top 100 contributors when the crowdloan ends, you will get two items!

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Welcome to _Skybreach_, a happy place in the clouds comprising of different Skylands where curious birds named Kanaria live. Kanaria live in groups named flocks that can have one or more leaders. Another interesting thing about Kanaria is that they love items. Kanaria can be equipped with them to help them gain special skills.

Although Kanaria can fly, sometimes there are other inhabitants or visitors of Skybreach who cannot, so for them (_and lazy Kanaria_) there are airships to get around different skylands. This is only the tip of the iceberg, for more details on the lives of Skybreach inhabitants, [take a look at this](https://app.subsocial.network/@rmrkapp/introducing-kanaria-skybreach-27024).

What are Kanaria?
-----------------

At this point you might be wondering _why am I reading about Kanaria in a SORA blogpost? or,_ you might also be fully aware that the top 100 SORA Kusama Parachain crowdloan contributors will get a special SORA-insipired item to equip to Kanaria NFT, and are keen to know what makes it so special.

When [RMRK announced on Dec 16th](https://app.subsocial.network/@rmrkapp/announcement-sora-s-community-and-rmrk-partner-up-27835) that they would be partnering up with SORA and providing support for the SORA Kusama Parachain auction in the form of special rewards for the top hundred crowdloan contributors, while SORA would be integrating [RMRK](https://twitter.com/RmrkApp) pallets to their runtime, for native integration with the world’s most advanced logic, in line with the [Many Worlds, One Economy vision](/sora-xor/many-worlds-one-economy-1ce709d4fb42), everyone was quite excited, but also very curious about the nature of these rewards. _What kind of NFT will be provided? Will they be Kanaria themselves or special items? What if I already have a Kanaria?_ Don’t worry, you will find the answers here.

Examples of different items in the Kanaria catalogue

SORA x RMRK special items
-------------------------

The inhabitants of Skybreach have all kinds of interests. The ones that are close to SORA are keen practitioners of kyūdō (弓道), the Japanese martial art of archery. Although they are not _kyūdōka_ (弓道家) who hunt, the practice and discipline does provide them ZEN, more specifically [_shin-zen-bi_](https://www.ikyf.org/shin_zen_bi.html), roughly “truth-goodness-beauty”.

It is said that a true shot in kyūdō is not one that hits the center of the target, but one where the arrow is can be said to exist in the target before it released. Kanaria from the SORA network all practice kyūdō, and with the SORA Kusama Parachain crowdloan, one hundred special yumi 弓 and ya 矢 (bow and arrow) handheld items, designed in collaboration with SORA, will be rewarded to the top hundred contributors in the SORA Kusama crowdloan for their Kanaria to proudly hold.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

The special SORA X RMRK yumi and ya (bow and arrow) handheld item

You do not have to have a Kanaria bird to get this bonus item, but you do have to be in the top 100 contributors, and among those among the top 100 contributors who do have a bird (_any_ [_Kanaria_](https://kanaria.rmrk.app) _bird_) when the crowdloan closes will get two items so that they can use them in the RMRK metaverse, and SORA’s! (_wait, does this mean that Skybreach will be in the SORA network too?_)

> **Note: Having multiple birds does not mean that you would get multiple items, however, if you are within the top 100 contributors with more than 1 account, then, in that case, you would indeed qualify for multiple items**

The SORA Kusama Parachain Auction crowdloan kicked off on the 20th of January. For more information take a look at this [Medium article](/sora-xor/the-sora-network-kusama-parachain-auction-5a6fe3a5f35f), or read the [SORA Kusama Parachain Crowdloan article in the SORA Wiki](https://wiki.sora.org/sora-kusama-parachain-crowdloan).

If you have any further questions, join the SORA community on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/sora_xor) and [Telegram](https://t.me/sora_xor).

About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------

Press enter or click to view image in full size

[SORA](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.
