The SORA Integrated Plan [https://sora.org/plan](https://sora.org/plan)

The ninth update in the ninth month of the year is quite a coincidence, however, nothing is left to chance when it comes to the development progress of the Integrated Plan! With a successful Ethereum hard fork (**The Merge**) happening last week, the HASHI Bridge is still closed for maintenance while the Ethereum network(s) stabilise. Please bear with us while we work on getting the bridge back online and stay tuned for further updates, as well as the reopening schedule. Restarting the bridge will require an on-chain referendum, so keep an eye out for it.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Stay tuned for further updates, restarting the bridge will require an on-chain referendum

In other updates, **Substrate 4 implementation** and Order book functionality were put on hold while the Merge was addressed. this means that the dependencies and developments requiring Substrate 4 were also paused. Fortunately, now that the Ethereum network has caught up with the times, we can continue to work on executing the Integrated Plan as usual.

Developers were hard at work on the **SORA Kusama Parachain Bridge** and there has been great progress with the architecture and implementation. On the other hand, Parity has provided some updates to Beefy proofs, so we continue to monitor their progress closely and extend our development support and solidarity toward their team.

A version of the bridge has been [available for testing](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/wiki/Trustless-EVM-Bridge), and a new version will be available for the community to test in the upcoming week. We will continue to update you on these functions once they are ready to test.

### About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a noncustodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystem, with native support for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) platforms. A premium user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
