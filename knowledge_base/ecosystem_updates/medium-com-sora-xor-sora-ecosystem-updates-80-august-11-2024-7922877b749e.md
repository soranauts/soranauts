### You Are Here

As the sun continues to shine in the northern hemisphere, bringing waves of heat, it is important to avoid dehydration in its many forms. With so many crashes and droughts affecting those around us, jumping into a liquid pool is the most straightforward way. You’re in luck, as KEN and KARMA rewards are now available.

Not Barbie’s meathead friend, but rather the charming namesake of the Kensetsu platform, KEN is available as a farming reward for the [XOR-KUSD](https://exchange.stg2.sora2.tachi.soramitsu.co.jp/#/swap/XOR/KUSD) pair on Polkaswap. On the other hand, everyone’s favorite reptile (other than Moonboy’s infatuation with the Coinlizard) is available as a farming reward for the [XOR-ETH](https://exchange.stg2.sora2.tachi.soramitsu.co.jp/#/swap/XOR/ETH) chameleon pool! This update also means that the transition to SORA v3 tokenomics is complete! Those eagle-eyed of you might also wonder why the 9th and 10th secrets have not yet been revealed yet… And continue wondering, you must, as they will be revealed soon enough!

Still on the Kensetsu front, Kensetsu Gold vaults have been approved by the community. Soon, the community will vote on distributing the Polkadot parachain crowdloan rewards. As stonks crash and burn all around us, Gold might be a good way to provide value as the bubbles of debt slowly pop in the heat of the ongoing crises (especially if you are a rich elite owning all the world’s gold mines)… Apologies for the digression. Let's get back to the updates.

Some more great news comes from the Horizontal Relay, rather Horizontal Relay-routed Message Passing or _HRMP_ for short. The SORA network has finally opened the Polkadot and Kusama Asset Hub channels. With the implementation of JAM making the Polkadot Relay Chain effectively an online computer, the assets within will be moving to its namesake hub. The community was very vocal about the assets to be made available in Polkaswap, mainly consisting of the Ded, the funny to play Pink, and the basic USDT. There are other tokens in the hubs for sure, so stay tuned for an update containing these assets and let us know what you would like to see in Polkaswap. Unfortunately, BILL might not cut, but we’re happy to be proven otherwise.

Moving on, the HASHI bridge is getting plenty of work done under the hood, on the one hand, Multi EVM support is progressing smoothly, while on the other a bridge to TON is under heavy development, as TON is clearly going to be the next big ecosystem. With the rise in the adoption of The Open Network thanks to the ease of use and large user base from all the degens already scrolling Telegram night and day, it makes sense to bring this interesting and innovative token to Polkaswap.

A very interesting development happening within the ecosystem is Soulbound tokens, a take on the non-fungibles, but this time, they’re also nontransferrable. What is yours is yours, uniquely tagging your account with metadata. This could be used to give you access to trade certain assets, perhaps regulated or community whitelisted, for example, and there is a world of possibilities. For now, we’ll leave this hint here, and you can think about the opportunities these assets bring for a myriad of applications.

[https://x.com/soratopia\_game/status/1817944689573613851](https://x.com/soratopia_game/status/1817944689573613851)

The temple of SORATOPIA has welcomed many newcomers with the _bushi katagi (武士氣質 — samurai spirit)_ to join the warrior way ([武士道](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E6%AD%A6%E5%A3%AB%E9%81%93#Japanese)) through prayer and training added constantly. Take a look at the new features added and make your _way_ forward as you practice righteousness ([義](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E7%BE%A9)), courage ([勇](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%8B%87%E6%B0%97)), benevolence ([仁](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E4%BB%81)), respect ([礼](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E7%A4%BC)), honor ([名誉](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%90%8D%E8%AA%89)), duty and loyalty ([忠義](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E5%BF%A0%E7%BE%A9)), self-control ([自制](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E8%87%AA%E5%88%B6)) and honesty ([誠](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/%E8%AA%A0)). In the words of Makoto Takemiya, _let’s get back to work_.

### 🎮 SORATOPIA

We’re excited to share some major updates to [SORATOPIA](https://t.me/soratopia), the vibrant Telegram game where players explore, complete quests, and earn rewards in a community-driven digital world. Developers have been working hard implementing new features and refining existing ones to enhance your gaming experience. Here’s a rundown of the latest developments:

* **Pray-to-Earn: Points for Devotion**

The new **Pray-to-Earn** feature allows players to collect points every 4 hours by tapping a button under the Shrine. This feature rewards consistent engagement, allowing players to gather points simply by checking in regularly. The more you play, the more you earn — so keep an eye on the 神社!

* **Referral System: Share the Fun, Reap the Rewards**

The **Referral System** is a win-win for everyone. Invite your friends to join SORATOPIA, and you’ll receive 10% of the points they earn. This system not only helps grow our community but also rewards you for being an ambassador of the game. The more friends you invite, the more you benefit!

* **Buy Invites: Expand Your Network**

In SORATOPIA, networking is key. With the new **Buy Invites** feature, you can use your earned points to unlock more invitations. This allows you to expand your circle within the game and maximize your potential earnings. It’s a strategic choice along your SORATOPIA journey!

* **Quests: More Ways to Earn Points**

A **Quests** feature has been added: Here, you will find tasks to complete, providing even more ways to earn points. Whether exploring new areas, engaging with community challenges, or participating in special events, these tasks offer excitement and rewards. Keep an eye on the task board for opportunities to boost your points!

* **2X Points for Early Birds**

Early supporters of SORATOPIA are in for a special treat! Enjoy double points on all activities before the official game launch.

About SORA
----------

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is an innovative, adaptive, non-debt-based monetary framework that facilitates economic stability, particularly for financially vulnerable countries. As a groundbreaking platform, SORA exemplifies the potential of blockchain technology in fostering a more inclusive and stable global financial system. Its integration in projects like the Bokolo Cash CBDC signifies a new era in digital currency, aligning technological advancements to achieve broader economic stability and resilience. Through its unique approach and capabilities, SORA plays a pivotal role in the evolution of global finance, particularly by supporting economies that need innovative and sustainable financial solutions the most.
