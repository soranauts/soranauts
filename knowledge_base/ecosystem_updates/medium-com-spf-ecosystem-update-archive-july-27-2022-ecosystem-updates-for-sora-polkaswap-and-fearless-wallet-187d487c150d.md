It has been a busy two weeks since the last updates, with most of the development work focusing on the SORA Kusama parachain. We are happy to announce that the parachain is up and running, producing blocks, and you can find it listed on Polkadot.js!

The trustless multi-EVM network bridge has great progress as well. It recently underwent an audit and the team is working on addressing the points raised. A bridge, especially a trustless one needs to be waterproof and bulletproof, so no corners can be cut because the consequences could be tragic… but don’t worry, we are working hard to ensure that the bridge is safu.

Last fortnight, the main overlapping aspect was the upgrade to Substrate 4. We are happy to announce that the upgrade is live in the dev environment and undergoing testing. The update should be ready to be rolled out soon! This fortnight, we are working hard on the implementation of [Beefy proofs](https://github.com/paritytech/grandpa-bridge-gadget/blob/master/docs/beefy.md). Beefy’s implementation is an important factor to ensure the EVM bridge’s security.

The progress of this implementation is also dependent on the team over at Parity, and we stand in solidarity with their team and look forward to the developments achieved by them. We are also open and available for cross-team collaborations to ensure that the overall Polkadot ecosystem can benefit from the advantages of this innovative technology.

There have also been some important progress with the SORA Builders Programme. We onboarded ADAR last week and the Ceres team has made huge steps forward with the development of DEO Arena. The [Alpha version](https://deoarena.io) is currently live, so you can get a taste of the gameplay ahead of the release and first season. See you in the Arena!

That’s all for the summary of the current SORA Integrated Plan updates. Let’s move on to the development highlights… But first, **we are happy to announce that the SORA Economic Forum will be held on the 23rd of September.** More news on the SORA Economic Forum will be shared soon! Thank you for following the SORA Integrated Plan progress and we look forward to updating you again in two weeks!

### 👷🏽‍♀️ SORA Builders Programme updates:

Press enter or click to view image in full size

It has taken some time, but we have onboarded the next SORA Builder. **We would like to welcome the Abed group**, who are developing **Advanced Digital Asset Routing (ADAR)** to the SORA Builders Programme.

ADAR is a solution that will allow businesses to seamlessly transmit digital assets to payees around the world. This will be done at a lower cost, higher speed, and with less red tape than the current implementations.

The team at ADAR requested a grant that will be divided into three milestones;

* $50k released on completion of first market validation and product design finalization (already completed)
* $50k to be released on launch of MVP (in progress)
* $50k to be released on the signing of the first two paying customers (in progress)

[Here is the proposal submitted by ADAR](https://github.com/sora-xor/rfps/blob/master/open_rfps/ADARSORAProposal.md), for more information on the Abed Group, [take a look at this deck](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1i6AdhPuCdAQgrs0NVMUo0fs7L8jJiFUv8H8qRg1-fgo/edit?usp=sharing), and to try the version available from the completion of the first milestone, [visit the ADAR website](https://adar.com/).

### About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a noncustodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystem, with native support for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) platforms. A premium user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
