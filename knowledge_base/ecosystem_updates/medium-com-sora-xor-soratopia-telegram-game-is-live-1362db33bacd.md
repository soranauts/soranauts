SORATOPIA Telegram Game is Live

_Many centuries ago, an ancient prophet foretold that one day, great Samurais would come to the land of SORATOPIA to save it from inevitable doom. Now that moment has come._

We are excited to announce that [SORATOPIA](https://t.me/soratopia_bot), the thrilling new Telegram game, is now live! This game combines engaging mechanics, community-driven initiatives, and rewarding experiences.

Are you still unfamiliar with SORATOPIA? For all Ronin, here’s everything you need to know about the launch and how to get involved.

We Are Live!
------------

Brave Samurai, can now join the path of SORATOPIA. Dive into the game with check-ins (up to 6 times a day). Choose between the paths of Bushido or Degeneracy — the choice is yours.

For the past two weeks, we’ve hosted Haiku contests and guessing games on our social media. Those who guessed correctly will receive their invites shortly! You can now share two invites from the game with your fellow Samurais — choose wisely!

For those who haven’t received an invite yet, stay tuned to our social media channels; your chance to join SORATOPIA might be just around the corner.

### How to Play?

**Tap to Earn:** One of SORATOPIA’s initial mechanics is “Tap to Earn.” Players can tap the button under the ‘Temple’ every 4 hours to collect points.

**Invite Friends:** Sharing is caring in SORATOPIA. Invite your friends to join the game and receive 10% of their earned points. You can now invite two friends by sharing codes in the game. Coming soon: Use your earned points to unlock additional invites and expand your referrals, boosting your earnings.

**Boosts (Coming soon):** To maximize your point earnings, SORATOPIA offers various boosts that players can unlock by completing tasks. These boosts enhance your efficiency and earnings! More info coming soon!

**Massive Airdrop (Coming soon):** As players engage with the game and accumulate points, they become eligible for a massive airdrop. The rewards are based on players’ performance, points, and overall engagement within the game. A token will be launched and distributed among the top-performing players, providing a significant incentive to stay active and competitive. More info coming soon!

### Stay Tuned!

Stay tuned to our official announcement channel on Telegram ([https://t.me/soratopia\_announcements](https://t.me/soratopia_announcements)) and X ([https://x.com/soratopia\_game](https://x.com/soratopia_game)).

You might just become a part of SORATOPIA.

Warning: Once you join SORATOPIA, your life will never be the same…

### About SORA

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is an innovative, adaptive, non-debt-based monetary framework that facilitates economic stability, particularly for financially vulnerable countries. As a groundbreaking platform, SORA exemplifies the potential of blockchain technology in fostering a more inclusive and stable global financial system. Its integration in projects like the [Bokolo Cash CBDC](https://sora.org/cbdc) signifies a new era in digital currency, aligning technological advancements to achieve broader economic stability and resilience. Through its unique approach and capabilities, SORA plays a pivotal role in the evolution of global finance, particularly by supporting economies that need innovative and sustainable financial solutions the most.
