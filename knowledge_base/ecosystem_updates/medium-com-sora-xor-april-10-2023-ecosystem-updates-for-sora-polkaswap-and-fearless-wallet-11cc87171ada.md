### **Many worlds, one economy. Everything is united under SORA.**

The first quarter of 2023 has ended, and industries and projects are reviewing their achievements while planning for future progress. This is an opportunity to reflect on SORA’s accomplishments towards its goals across various tracks and to reiterate the value that a new economic system brings to the world. To learn more about this, read [this article](/sora-xor/many-worlds-one-economy-1ce709d4fb42).

Systems provide structure and support for creating innovative tools that can enhance users’ lives and the societies they serve. SORA aims to be a novel and rational economic system, as discussed in this [article](/sora-xor/sora-v2-implementation-1febd3260b87). The tools available within the network prioritize making economic systems accessible to all users, not just seasoned experts. This accessibility should make it easy for anyone to take advantage of these breakthroughs.

The main tools that the SORA network provides come in the form of wallets, such as the [SORA wallet](https://sora.org/#wallet) for interacting with native network tokens like XOR, VAL, PSWAP, TBCD, XST, and more, as well as [Fearless Wallet](https://fearlesswallet.io/), the interoperable [Substrate super wallet](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/wallets). With the wallet in place, you can fearlessly access SORA’s economy and own your assets. Next comes the beating heart of the economy: [Polkaswap.io](https://polkaswap.io/), which offers all the token swapping, staking, and farming tools in a beautiful, intuitive, and stylish interface.

Decentralized finance allows everyone to have direct control over their assets without intermediaries like brokers or bankers. However, the inherent responsibility that DeFi brings to users is not without its risks. Therefore, it is crucial to have access to data. Without data, you are navigating in the dark. Data shines a light on the vast unknown, revealing its secrets and helping you choose the best path to follow. The light at the end of the tunnel leads to a bright outcome.

As the spring arrives, we continue to work hard to advance the progress of the SORA integrated plan. [In the last update](/sora-xor/march-23-2023-ecosystem-updates-for-sora-polkaswap-and-fearless-wallet-7a04becec28a), we shared the great news that the SORA Kusama parachain lease had been renewed, thanks to all the contributors that locked KSM to help this happen. With the renewed lease, work on the Substrate bridge continues, with public testing coming soon. We hope you will help ensure that the final bridge is up to SORA standards.

The trustless multi-EVM bridge is getting a UI, and work is ongoing to make it as easy and intuitive for you to use, just like the [HASHI bridge](https://polkaswap.io/#/bridge/). Order book development is progressing smoothly, and the core implementation is ongoing. SORA release 1.9.0 was successfully implemented, improving network stability and laying the foundation for great innovations coming down the pipeline. For those who are interested, [a changelog is available here](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/releases/tag/1.9.0). We ask all SORA network validators, affectionately known as node runners, to update their nodes to v1.9.0.

We [published an exciting article about the SORA VALidator token](https://sora-xor.medium.com/to-the-future-baeb3b0f9af) and its new TradFi use. Along with the exciting utilities that the **VAL** token brings, having a tie to real-life finance cements its position as the store of value for the network. If you are not familiar with **VAL**, [take a look at this article](/sora-xor/sora-validator-reward-token-val-c96a8afb8541) to get yourself up to speed.

[Velocity coefficient (VC)](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/v/velocity.asp) is a term used in TradFi to define the monthly average of times that a currency changes hands and is a good indicator for the asset’s liquidity and trading activity.

Along with all the information and features available on Polkaswap, we are working hard in collaboration with other project teams through the [Polkaswap-as-a-service initiative](/polkaswap/polkaswap-as-a-service-ca862afefefa) to provide you with all the major tokens within the blockchain space. About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is a new economic order for the world, aimed at high growth and empowering human progress. The SORA network includes interoperability with the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) networks, as well as the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a non-custodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem. It offers boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
