### You Are Here

Welcome to the Autumn edition of the SORA Ecosystem Updates! This month, there has been amazing progress. ALT is now live on the network, and there have been several runtime updates since we last updated you on what is going on. Progress on bridges this month is also quite significant. The multi-EVM bridge and TON are currently being worked on, so the outlook is very exciting!

Yes, there were several releases the past couple of months, SORA has been busy buidling.

Regarding HRMP channels, the focus is on Polkadot Asset Hub to ensure smooth transfers across both networks. The task of opening channels to other networks is complete. The SORA community reached out to several peers on Polkadot; however, although users were keen on interoperability, other players in the space had other plans. Fear not, this will not stop the goal to make SORA the new economic order and if anything, it will allow the focus to shift entirely to SORA v3, so please look forward to that.

The much-awaited SORA Card has made significant progress this month, with very encouraging updates from the team after overcoming challenges with legacy systems — a common theme in the current narrative. As BRICS nations meet to develop an alternative to the status quo and a new U.S. administration approaches, the need for monetary policy that advances humanity becomes clearer. When financial access is weaponized through sanctions and other draconian measures against countries, it’s not the politicians and leaders who suffer the most.

Instead, ordinary people become oppressed, unable to access their earnings or provide for their dependents. While these human consequences often get lost in the bigger geopolitical picture, the solution is clear: a currency free from political agendas and equally accessible to all, regardless of location, is the path toward true freedom.

Speaking of the value of freedom, it is good to know that we are all a step closer to the light at the end of the tunnel, which seems to have been [longer than the Gotthard base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gotthard_Base_Tunnel) at some points. Needless to say, the entire community looks forward to the SORA Card launch.

SORA Builders have made significant progress. SORA attended the [Tech Forum Argentina](https://techforum.com.ar/), where President Javier Milei was present, and aligned with Parity’s Shawn Tabrizi’s vision that resilience will lead to unprecedented innovations for a better future.

Finally, the Good Doctor has been busy speaking with world leaders. He travelled to speak at Hong Kong Fintech Week, CamEd Business School, and the Singapore Fintech Festival. He then joined a national delegation at the [United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Framework_Convention_on_Climate_Change) (UNFCCC) [Conference of the Parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_of_the_parties) (COP29) in Baku, Azerbaijan, discussing decarbonising non-XOR token holders for a sustainable phuture. He shared insights about SORA’s benefits — not just as technical infrastructure but as a monetary system focused on productive output and fair resource allocation. As this vision seems increasingly important in today’s world, we continue BUIDLing tirelessly to create and guide humanity’s better future actively.

In other news, SORA made headlines with the announcement of Palau Digital Savings Bonds, an innovation powered by the network that aims to enhance the well-being of the entire island nation and its people.

If you are part of the [SORA Telegram community](https://t.me/sora_xor), you were among the first to hear some [great alpha from the Good Doctor himself on the 23rd of October](https://t.me/SORAdaily/1135). Secret ten has been revealed, with the information that the third iteration of the SORA network will be based on [Hyperledger Iroha 2 technology](https://iroha.tech/). This will allow seamless connections to Central Banks and other networks that jealously guard their assets. Along with this good news, SORA received some accolades that will be announced in due time.

Finally, the [SORA Economic Forum 2024](https://soranomics.com) was held on November 20th, with many exciting speakers, including from 4 central banks.

You are now up to date with the recent happenings in the SORA ecosystem and the current status of the SORA Integrated Plan. Stay tuned for upcoming news from all the SORA contributors, and we’ll meet again soon. **Now, let’s get back to work!**

### ALT (Aggregate Liquidity Technology) Integration with the Order book

ALT is an innovative mechanism based on Dr [Makoto Takemiya’s paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10174911), which **aims to optimally combine different liquidity sources** in [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) **to provide the best trading experience**. By aggregating liquidity from multiple sources, ALT ensures that users receive the most efficient and competitive trades.

Now that the **tech has been released**, you can **enjoy an optimised trading experience**. Swaps now consider the different liquidity sources — XYK pool, TBC, XST, and Order Book — to give you the best output for your input.

### Tokenomics Upgrade — KUSD

To position KUSD as the leading decentralized and unstoppable USD-pegged stablecoin, the SORA community voted to update the tokenomics. Since the November 22 runtime upgrade, 20% of all burned XOR transaction fees will now be used to buy back and burn KUSD, while 30% will continue to be burned directly.

Additionally, the VAL and PSWAP remint mechanism will now buy back and burn 39% of KUSD and 1% of TBCD, with this reminted amount coming from the VAL airdrop and PSWAP strategic reward vestings, respectively (so the burned amounts of VAL and PSWAP don’t change).

The entire 20% margin between the TBC (Token Bonding Curve) buy-price and sell-price functions will also be used to buy back and burn KUSD. The SORA builders are continuously updating the [wiki](https://wiki.sora.org/) to reflect these latest improvements.

### Bridges

Bridges are crucial to the SORA ecosystem, offering best-in-class interoperability and enabling smooth asset transfers between ecosystems. With XORless transfers coming soon, users can bridge their assets into SORA and use their preferred tokens across the ecosystem’s comprehensive range of services, products, and tools.

SORA continues to expand its bridge offerings and forge strategic partnerships, enhancing network connectivity and creating more opportunities for our community in this next-generation DeFi hub.

### Multi-EVM Bridge

EVM-based networks comprise some of the most popular ecosystems today, including Ethereum, Binance, and Kaia. Enabling our community to access the tokens, assets, and services on these networks is strategically important for our ecosystem and helps onboard their communities into the SORA New Economic Order.

The multi-EVM bridge will streamline connections to any EVM-based ecosystem by minimizing the development work needed for each additional bridge.

* The BUIDLers is currently focused on higher priority initiatives, so progress is slow but steady.
* Testing is in progress to verify smooth integration between multiple EVM-based networks.
* Analyzing and optimizing fee structures across networks ensures users get the most cost-effective transactions.

### TON Bridge

TON (The Open Network) is working toward people’s freedom and gaining popularity daily by delivering innovative features (and [addictive games](https://t.me/soratopia_bot/app?startapp=9K5R4FZZCEY32) on the Telegram messaging app).

Connecting with ecosystems that share our values has always been a core priority for SORA. To maintain flexibility, we have divided the bridge development into distinct phases.

* The first phase, which enables one-way transfers from TON to SORA to support SORATOPIA use cases, is in final testing. This brings us closer to connecting the TON blockchain with the SORA ecosystem for enhanced interoperability.
* The second phase will enable two-way transfers between SORA and TON, allowing SORA tokens and assets to flow into the TON ecosystem. This will help our community and ambassadors connect with potential allies who share our vision of freedom and a New Economic Order.

### About SORA

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is an innovative, adaptive, non-debt-based monetary framework that facilitates economic stability, particularly for financially vulnerable countries. As a groundbreaking platform, SORA exemplifies the potential of blockchain technology in fostering a more inclusive and stable global financial system. Its integration in projects like the Bokolo Cash CBDC signifies a new era in digital currency, aligning technological advancements to achieve broader economic stability and resilience. Through its unique approach and capabilities, SORA plays a pivotal role in the evolution of global finance, particularly by supporting economies that need innovative and sustainable financial solutions the most.
