TL;DR
-----

* A new token is being created for productive economic expansion: SORA Vested XOR (vXOR)
* 99% of the daily remint of XOR is proposed to buy back and burn vXOR
* v3 tokenomics will, of course, continue, with XOR supply decreasing daily
* The buy-back-and-burn mechanism acts as vesting (hence the name of vXOR), so to encourage builders (and countries using XOR) to be motivated to work towards an increased XOR price, every week, the general price trend of XOR will be calculated, and if positive, will result in more vXOR being given to vXOR stakers and XOR-vXOR LP farmers
* Not only SORA builders, but also countries that are **_SORAized_** (XOR-using countries) can be given vXOR via on-chain governance to expand their economics (because ΔMV=ΔPY, so **you need an increase in purchasing power in an economy to expand the economy**…otherwise you don’t get economic growth)

Remember, Remember, the 25th of September
-----------------------------------------

September 25, S3Y1 (**_S_**_ORA v_**_3_** **Y**ear 1, also known as 2024; _yes, SORA v3 is such a big deal there’s a new calendar based on it_). The date vXOR was proposed by the SORA community to the SORA community for the SORA community. vXOR is a key part of the SORA v3 ecosystem. It solves the logical gap between the need for economic expansion to increase production (needed for any viable economy) and the desire for XOR to decrease supply, thus increasing scarcity.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

While inflation is demonized in the mainstream zeitgeist, and rightly so, productive economic expansion is required for society to make any progress and improve the state of the world around us. With SORA v3, the goal is to have a high-growth economy while keeping the native token of the SORA network, XOR, deflationary, as this gives forward guidance about the stability and viability of the token.

SORA v3 is Designed to be the Realization of the Japanese High-Growth Model on a Global Scale
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Since its first conception in 2017, SORA has been based on Professor Richard Werner's research and his [explanation of how the Japanese postwar economy experienced massive financial growth](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p5Ac7ap_MAY).

Press enter or click to view image in full size

As explained at the [second SORA Economic Forum](https://soranomics.com/#content_20_fancybox-8), Professor Richard Werner discovered the General Quantity Theory (also known as the Disaggregated Quantity Theory of Credit) by studying how the Bank of Japan used credit guidance to guide commercial banks in allocating credit to productive uses. If the allocation decisions are made correctly, then creating new purchasing power in an economy is necessary and sufficient for nominal GDP growth.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

This is also [shown by Professor Richard Werner](https://professorwerner.org/pubs/banks-and-economic-growth-the-general-theory-in-a-basic-disequilibrium-model-with-five-rationing-regimes/) more precisely as:

While you can read the [full paper for all the mathematical details](https://professorwerner.org/pubs/banks-and-economic-growth-the-general-theory-in-a-basic-disequilibrium-model-with-five-rationing-regimes/), keep in mind that this is not based on abstract theories but rather on observed empirical reality. This equation shows that if you increase credit for real economic transactions (i.e., introducing new purchasing power into an economy for production), you will get a corresponding change in the price level and the output. Importantly, to use the words of Professor Richard Werner, this equation shows _“that for nominal GDP (i.e., the value of transactions that make up nominal GDP) to grow, there must be an increase in credit creation used to fund this growth.”_

The implication, of course, is that nominal GDP growth cannot occur without more “money” in an economy to fulfil those transactions (_you can read the full paper to convince yourself of this)_.

For SORA, this means that it is necessary to find a way to introduce new money into the SORA economy without increasing the supply of the XOR token. This is important not only because of perceptions that increasing token supply can have but also because there are two issues with the General Quantity Theory that can hurt small economies, namely:

1. There is a time lag between funding production and delivery of new goods and services
2. There are second- and later-order effects that the money created for funding has on the economy (for example, even if you create money to pay for new production, those companies will pay others who can use the money for consumption, which has some non-zero inflationary pressure).

For 1) above, there’s no cure except to become large enough so that any new purchasing power in an economy created for production is not significant in the short term and is easily covered by other economic activity. For 2), however, the SORA community has proposed a dual-token model, where a new token, vXOR, can be a “productive credit token” backed by labour/output.

v for vXOR Tokenomics
---------------------

**_This notion brings us to the virtuous and valuable vindication to verify this vexing verbiage, vXOR_**.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Put simply, vXOR is vested XOR. It is called **_vested_** XOR because it is “turned into” XOR over time, by taking a part of the daily burned XOR amount and buying back and burning vXOR. This is done because there needs to be a way to expand the purchasing power in the SORA economy to fund the creation of new goods and services, as explained in the previous section, without inflating the supply of XOR (because it is necessary to avoid higher-order effects of currency creation). As it eventually can be turned into XOR, it is a companion token. All new vXOR has to be backed by output and will be created by on-chain governance, voted on by XOR token holders.

At the same time, vXOR is in line with the values and objectives of SORA v3, since it harnesses both the concept of reducing the XOR supply, while at the same time supporting the value generated by the production of goods and services in the SORA economy, with benefits for vXOR stakers, XOR-vXOR LP providers, and the entire SORA community whenever there are positive performance trends.

vXOR will, of course, vest faster into XOR when the XOR price is low, so it is important to incentivize builders in the SORA economy to want to create value for XOR too. To do this, once a day at a random block, the price of XOR will be saved, and once a week, the trend line will be analyzed, and vXOR stakers and XOR-vXOR LP farmers on Demeter farm will get new vXOR as a reward weekly if the trend was positive over the week. Otherwise, they get no bonus. This way, all the incentives will be aligned with the different SORA ecosystem participants.

Because SORA aims not _only_ to build a cryptoeconomic system for a niche internet community of degens, but rather for everyone in the whole world, it is important to note that this same methodology can also be used to allocate new purchasing power in contemporary economies, all over the world. In fact, SORAMITSU, one of the builders in the SORA ecosystem, has been partnering with government agencies like [NISER](https://news.bitcoin.com/nigerian-think-tank-japanese-tech-firm-partner-on-blockchain-research/) to share knowledge about the exciting economic innovation happening at SORA. When designing SORA v3, economics comes first, and the goal of SORA has always been to power real economies, _not only virtual ones_.

Final Thoughts: XOR will Continue to use v3 Tokenomics and be Deflationary in Supply
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[In SORA v3](/sora-xor/sora-v3-669d0619b141), XOR became effectively deflationary in supply because it is burned with each transaction, but there is no longer a way to mint new XOR over the long run as new TBCD are no longer created. It is important to note that the XOR supply will continue to decrease daily, even with this new model. **1% of the net burned amount will be gone forever.** This is important to give a clear signal and expectation to both producers and consumers in the XOR economy, which will lead to price predictability and, thus, stability.

At the same time, it is important to always keep the need for productive output at the forefront, making the SORA economy proliferate and expand worldwide. With the introduction of vXOR, buying back and burning XOR will be even more important for the economy, which, at the same time, should encourage network use through building and transacting, as every operation means more growth. As you read these lines, vXOR is being diligently worked on, and shortly, you, the SORA citizen, will have to give birth to bring this new system and token into existence.

Soon, a new utility for vXOR will also be introduced as part of a larger strategy for the SORA ecosystem to expand to the The Open Network (TON) on Telegram ecosystem and gain access to millions of users, so keep an eye out for hints and new information (and play [SORATOPIA](https://t.me/soratopia_bot/) for now).
