The SORA Integrated Plan [https://sora.org/plan](https://sora.org/plan)

**Many worlds, one economy.**

The last day of May… The year is almost halfway through and as the Summer starts in some parts of the world, SORA never stops! There is a lot to talk about this time, for starters, users can now nominate SORA network validators from the palm of their hand as of the latest Fearless Wallet update. This is the first Fearless feature available to SORA users as part of their membership in the [SORA Builders Programme](/sora-xor/sora-builders-programme-979bea8831ed).

Gone are the cold days of winter when you needed to follow a series of steps in the platform now known as [dotapps.io](https://dotapps.io)… Summer has brought the ability to nominate validators on the network to the palm of your hand.

With vast oceans of liquidity, more than one bridge is needed to cross. After all the hard work put into building secure bridges, the Multi-EVM bridge is extending the reach of the HASHI bridge to more people, allowing them to join the SORA (Supranational Output Rationalization Alliance) movement. The SORA Kusama parachain bridge is also receiving a seamless, stylish, and fresh user interface.

With plenty of high-profile examples of what not to do, the SORA Synthetics XST Platform will need to withstand all the obstacles and dangers that financial instruments carry. After extensive internal testing, the product will be available for public testing. It has been quite the journey. And life-changing journeys are always better with company.

The interesting thing about travelling with someone else is having to learn to adapt to other people’s rhythms and cycles. Some people wake up early in the morning and gain traction during the day, others are more nocturnal, diligently working into the early hours of the night under the cover of moonlight. When collaborating with other teams, you also need to adhere to their processes and pace. And SORA Card is such an example.

Too much is at stake to afford any mistake, so at the risk of sounding like a broken record, no stone can be left unturned with due diligence, especially when there is money involved, so unfortunately, the SORA Card release will take however long it has to take. Thank you for your ongoing patience and support, and sorry for the inconvenience caused, but there is nothing we can do as we wait for our partners to finish their work. They are all highly motivated to finish as soon as possible so revenue can start to come in, so it will happen as quickly as physically possible.

On to the SORA mobile wallet, the internet’s best-kept secret, the Demeter Farming platform will be available there soon. There is still much progress ahead, so stay tuned for the implementation of this feature. SORA Builders have plenty of interesting updates this time, so find out more in the following sections of the SORA updates. In particular, CERES, ADAR, and Fearless Wallet are making great strides to make the SORA economic system available to everyone.

In a productive economy like SORA, it is crucial for everyone, whether individuals or businesses, to easily access and manage their assets to successfully participate in and expand the economic system. This sets SORA apart from your average tokenomics.

You are now up to date with what has happened in the last few weeks in the SORA ecosystem and the current state of the SORA Integrated Plan. Please stay tuned for upcoming news from all the SORA contributors, and we’ll see you again in two weeks. Let’s get back to work!

About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is a new economic order for the world aimed at high growth and empowering human progress. The SORA network includes interoperability with the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) networks, as well as the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a non-custodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem. It offers boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
