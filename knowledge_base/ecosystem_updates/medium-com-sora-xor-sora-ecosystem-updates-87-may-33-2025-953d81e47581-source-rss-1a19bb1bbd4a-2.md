### You Are Here

Thursday, 22nd of May, was a significant day for the SORA ecosystem, after the second attempt at the repackaging of XOR updated the network assets. With the first attempt not processing due to a non-permitted symbol in the token name, rolling back the entire process, the second successful referendum updated the name of the SORA token to 100x XOR and the SORA Token Bonding Curve Dollar (AKA XOR Redback) to 100x SORA TBC Dollar.

Thanks to this minor yet significant change, where every hundred XOR is repackaged into one, reading and inputting the amounts of tokens will be much easier and more intuitive. Additionally, several measures have been taken so that this change only affects assets within the SORA network. If you had XOR on another blockchain, such as Ethereum, this change will not affect your tokens. What’s more, when and if you decide to bridge these tokens into the SORA network, they will automagically be converted into the new denomination. Nice.

SORA V3 development continues at full speed. The Hub Chain architecture serves as the foundation of the system, providing a unified and scalable platform that enables diverse financial systems to interact securely and efficiently. This design allows both centralized entities — such as governments and central banks — and decentralized platforms to work together, fostering innovation while maintaining necessary control and compliance standards.

The Hub Chain strengthens the resilience of the system by reducing dependence on individual national systems, preserves economic sovereignty through local control, and enables global financial inclusion. Through asset creation, secure private transactions, and cross-network interoperability, the hub chain serves as essential infrastructure for building robust, future-proof financial applications.

SORA V3 will support these networks while addressing the limitations, security issues, and trust concerns inherent in legacy systems and older blockchain protocols that have revealed technical gaps — including those that necessitated the repackaging of XOR.

The Fujiwara testnet is still ongoing, and you can join the family [following the tutorial available in the SORA wiki](https://wiki.sora.org/running-a-sora-testnet-node.html). Help us test the new features that are constantly released in SORA v3, and stay informed about the major developments of [Hyperledger Iroha by joining our Telegram community today](https://t.me/hyperledgeriroha). There are weekly development updates, monthly developer calls and best of all, this is all open source, so you can begin to build your Hyperledger Iroha-based application now. Follow the process every step of the way so you don’t end up out of the game like Richie Aprile.

Moving on to the Polkaswap family, [Polkaswap.io](http://Polkaswap.io) released major updates this month. The most significant addition is the Multi-signature feature in the UI, which lets users protect their assets by requiring multiple signatories for transactions. This feature aggregates signatures in a trustless way for users who use the [Fearless Wallet](https://fearlesswallet.io) plugin in Chrome. These transactions can also be set with expiry times — if the deadline passes, the transaction automatically cancels. [Follow these instructions to set up Multi-sig with your team](https://wiki.sora.org/running-a-sora-testnet-node.html).

Finally, there has been substantial progress on a very special version of SORA Wallet that supports an innovative use case for builders, the likes of which you have never seen in the cryptocurrency space. Due to the code of silence, more details cannot be provided at this point, so please look forward to more updates where the details are revealed. _Silence is golden_.

You’re now caught up on the latest developments in the SORA ecosystem and the SORA Integrated Plan. Stay tuned for more updates from our contributors — we’ll be back with more news soon. **Now, let’s get back to work!**

### About SORA

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is an innovative, adaptive, non-debt-based monetary framework that facilitates economic stability, particularly for financially vulnerable countries. As a groundbreaking platform, SORA exemplifies the potential of blockchain technology in fostering a more inclusive and stable global financial system. Its integration in projects like the Bokolo Cash CBDC signifies a new era in digital currency, aligning technological advancements to achieve broader economic stability and resilience. Through its unique approach and capabilities, SORA plays a pivotal role in the evolution of global finance, particularly by supporting economies that need innovative and sustainable financial solutions the most.
