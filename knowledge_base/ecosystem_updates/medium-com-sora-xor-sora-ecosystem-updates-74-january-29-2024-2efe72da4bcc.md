2024 has started with a bang; thanks to your support, the SORA Polkadot parachain was secured after winning the 59th auction against an anonymous parathread. Thanks to the community’s support and a 41.6% lead, the candle blew out on the 9th of January at 22:07hr, with 46,422 DOT contributed by all of you. Please look forward to the reward distribution of Kensetsu Gold, which will be vested for the duration of the parachain lease.

Right after securing the parachain, the bridge between Polkadot and SORA was up and running, so for the first time ecosystem-wide, **you can trade both DOT and KSM in Polkaswap.io**!

Press enter or click to view image in full size

At this moment, you must be wondering “wen Kensetsu Gold,” which is a very valid question, as the last time you read this section, the base Kensetsu overcollateralised stable asset was still under development. Happily, there is much more progress than last time, and you will soon be able to try out the platform on the testnet. Remember that the SORA standard is very high. Therefore, new features will only be released after undergoing intensive testing, gathering plenty of user feedback, and considering every possible scenario to ensure successful quality.

Another great achievement this month is securing another ~1-year lease for our SORA Kusama Parachain, which was self-funded with 11 KSM. It’s important to keep all our existing links to other networks active.

Before the holidays passed, there was a mention of the integrated plan receiving an upgrade, with new milestones added, which take into consideration the current technological, geopolitical, and user-requested features that make sense and support the development of the SORA new world economic order. Although this time we won’t be dropping any mad ⍺lpha, here are some insights on what to expect moving forward:

* The **order book** will support the creation of marketplaces, including RWA and stablecoin trading; advanced trading will be possible to toggle on Polkaswap in the future.
* **Aggregate Liquidity Technology** will someday power trades across boundless universes (including other chains).
* **XORless transactions** will be available for all swaps, similar to the system used for the CBDC proof of concept (this will still use XOR for transaction fees, just that the XOR is taken from the swap as part of the process behind the scenes).
* **Kensetsu assets** will be pegged to different currencies, and RWA, and the community will decide which ones and have the power to add or remove assets.
* **Kensetsu** will have a $KEN token (no $Barbie, though).
* **Liquid staking** will be made available for SORA network nominators.
* **The HASHI bridge** will break free from the desktop and be available on mobile devices, first the Substrate version, then the Multi EVM.
* **SORA card** on and off-ramps will be the easiest way to get your favourite SORA network tokens and more crypto assets.
* _Much more…_

What do you think? The future looks bright for SORA and the community, and contributors [don’t stop, can’t stop, won’t stop buidling](https://youtu.be/SPlQpGeTbIE?si=NJeNEJhIEjwDdsur). Please look forward to more details on these points and more to come, as well as an updated version of the integrated plan to include these new milestones.

Today, work is ongoing to update the Polkadot SDK version to upgrade the Substrate (_or Polkadot now_) bridge. Additionally, work is ongoing to bridge the sovereign and ultra-based country to the SORA network, so stay tuned for an announcement.

Work on the order book is progressing steadily, focusing on indexers to ensure that the information provided is up-to-date and uptime does not suffer from a high load triggered by order book transactions. No one likes inaccurate data.

On the mobile side, Demeter farming is available within the SORA wallet, allowing you to carry the best-kept secret in crypto in the palm of your hand wherever you go. Adding or removing liquidity and claiming rewards will be a breeze!

Finally, SORA card development is progressing steadily. Collaborating with external parties, especially legacy ones, is always challenging, and progress has taken longer than anticipated between compliance and other delays. A message from the SORA card team below is very inspiring, and the community can look forward to great things in 2024!

The year has started strong, and it will only pick up from here, so buckle up!

**Please stay tuned for further updates from the SORA ecosystem, not only for the entire Dotsama community but also for the blockchain space.**

You are now up to date with the recent happenings in the SORA ecosystem and the current status of the SORA Integrated Plan. Stay tuned for upcoming news from all the SORA contributors, and we’ll meet again in two weeks. **Now, let’s get back to work!**

### ⚙️ SORA Blockchain Update

* Updating the polkadotSDK (substrate) version to 1.1.0. This will enable an architecture upgrade for the Substrate bridge.

### Substrate bridge ([Github milestone](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/milestone/10))

* Working on adapting the bridge to connect to a non-parachain Substrate-based chain. The chain is developed by a sovereign country.

### Order book ([Github milestone](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/milestone/4))

* Investigating how indexers might react to high load on the order book to ensure sufficient uptime and performance.

### Multi-EVM bridge ([Github milestone](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/milestone/1))

* The team is mainly focused on other initiatives.

### Kensetsu ([Github milestone](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/milestone/15))

* Going over test cases to ensure the platform is robust;
* Preparing for the release to public test.

### Aggregate Liquidity Technology ([Github milestone](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/milestone/21))

* Working on the architecture upgrade to better accommodate integrating new liquidity sources into the algorithm; first up: the order book liquidity source!

### Support for Ink! smart contracts ([Github milestone](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/milestone/18))

* There has been no progress recently. The team is focused on other initiatives.

### About SORA

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is a new economic order for the world, aimed at high growth and empowering human progress. The SORA network includes interoperability with the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) networks, as well as the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.
