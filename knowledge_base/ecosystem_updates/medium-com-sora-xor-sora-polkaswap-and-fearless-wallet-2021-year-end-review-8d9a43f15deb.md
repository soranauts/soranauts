Getting Ready for the New Year by Looking Back at the Past
----------------------------------------------------------

2021 was a truly unique year for most of us. In this regard, when looking at all the activity from the SORA community, it is important to realize how tremendously impactful this year has been.

We have altogether taken another big step towards SORA’s great vision this year, a multiverse economic system. We believe that SORA, its economy, and its technology, are a great and important tool for empowering people around the multiverse.

Together, we have outgrown ourselves as we now support an interoperability protocol, an Ethereum bridge, an Integrated DEX for the Polkadot ecosystem, as well as a robust community of **69 comprehensive validators**, who all play an active role in the security of the network. Together, in less than one year, we have executed more than **3.1M transactions** on the SORA network.

With this great preamble, we are proud to present the SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet Year-End Review. Join us as we update you on the ongoing projects, new implementations, and successes that 2021 has brought all of us. We thank you for being a part of this amazing journey, although so much has been done, this is only the beginning.

SORA: Welcoming the New Year by Looking Back
--------------------------------------------

On April 27, 2021, we **launched the SORA substrate network** with the vision to provide a new infrastructure for faster, cost-effective networking with and for the Polkadot community, offering a wide range of developer tools for DeFi, NFTs, and the Metaverse. 2021 was an important year for us as we shifted our vision forward, _from One World, One Economy, to Many Worlds, One Economy._ Join us for a summary of this year’s highlights.

> **With SORA at its core, our shared vision is to unite the Metaverse and physical worlds to create new financial opportunities for both worlds and lay the foundation for the next generations and connect** [**Many Worlds**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_KGkQ9x-ydQ) **to One Economy.**
> 
> Excellent read- [SORA: Many Worlds One Economy](/sora-xor/many-worlds-one-economy-1ce709d4fb42)

### Into the Metaverse, SORA, and JP Games

We begin the highlights of 2021 with the implementation of XOR in the Metaverse. After a community referendum with a positive outcome, it was decided that SORA would power the [**JPGames’ Metaverse Pegasus City**](/sora-xor/sora-xor-could-be-the-official-native-token-of-the-pegasus-world-kit-4ac45fd7cc32) and other upcoming projects. This event cemented SORA’s view of Many Worlds, One Economy and proved to be a further step in the adoption of SORA and a start in the new world.

### XSTUSD Launch

Although it was proposed in 2018 (and much earlier in the 1910’s), 2021 was the year that the [**SORA synthetic asset USD (XSTUSD)**](/sora-xor/introducing-xstusd-a-stablecoin-on-sora-for-polkadot-ecosystem-public-testing-a3861c13643e) was officially launched. Within a few months, the [amount of XSTUSD in circulation](https://mof.sora.org/qty/xstusd) was close to $700k. XSTUSD positions itself as a key stablecoin within the Polkadot ecosystem that enables and empowers the community to use it in DeFi dApps.

### SORA Builders Programme Launch

November 22nd of this year marked the launch of the [**SORA Builders Programme**](/sora-xor/sora-builders-programme-979bea8831ed)**,** this initiative aims to develop and scale the SORA network. Concretely, it will allow the SORA community to port and build protocols and dApps on the SORA network. In doing so, teams building on top of SORA will be offered grants and proven expertise to help design new and innovative solutions for DeFi and NFTs.

Polkaswap: The Future of Trading
--------------------------------

We now move on to the main highlights of the year for [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/), and what a year it has been! From the launch that took place in April this year to the latest developments, let’s take a look at how 2021 has been for the SORA network’s liquidity hub.

### Polkaswap Release 𒊹𒂵𒆜

Polkaswap’s release was quite an eventful landmark for the SORA network, being the first of its kind as a next-generation, cross-chain liquidity aggregator DEX protocol within SORA. Polkaswap boasts a bridge between the Ethereum and SORA networks, as well as hosting the [SORA Token Bonding Curve](https://www.reddit.com/r/Polkadot/comments/rq60na/eliminating_irrational_token_allocation_and/). You can read all about [Polkaswap’s launch here](/polkaswap/polkaswap-soft-launch-a033d1d49b57).

### Strategic rewards

Another great achievement in 2021, was the Polkaswap Strategic Rewards. With these awesome rewards, the possible ways to earn PSWAP are:

1. **Adding liquidity on Polkaswap**
2. **Buying XOR from the Token Bonding Curve (TBC), with ETH or DAI**
3. **Earning market-making rebates for trading at least 1 XOR, at least 500 times a month**

For more details regarding how to obtain and claim these rewards, [take a look at this article](/polkaswap/pswap-rules-everything-around-me-three-pathways-to-polkaswap-rewards-63842caf88c0).

### HASHI Bridge: ERC-20 token trading

As mentioned earlier, an important part of Polkaswap (so important it has been deemed [**Systemically Important Infrastructure**](/sora-xor/social-insurance-for-systematically-important-infrastructure-18a63ef711ca)) is the HASHI bridge, the only way to cross between the Ethereum and SORA networks, back and forth, and one of a kind infrastructure currently within the ecosystem. The HASHI bridge opened this year and there have been proposals for bridges to other networks, so let’s see what other bridges we can cross in 2022!

### Polkswap goes Mobile

After a successful launch, 2021 was also the year where Polkaswap sought to expand to other platforms. Needless to say, mobile has been a key factor of adoption for crypto platforms, and Polkaswap is not the exception. Earlier this year, [Polkaswap was announced on the SORA testnet](/polkaswap/polkaswap-goes-mobile-lets-start-testing-the-soralution-mobile-app-46e7e25360ea) app, it shouldn’t be long before Polkaswap’s innovative functions are also enjoyed in the palm of your hand.

### XSTUSD Liquidity on Polkaswap

A big headline for the SORA ecosystem and Polkaswap this year was the [launch of XSTUSD](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tatianakoffman/2021/11/23/the-rise-of-decentralized-money-on-polkadotnew-algorithmic-stablecoin-launches-on-sora/?sh=7499e10731bc), an algorithmic stablecoin pegged to the price of DAI. This is a highlight for Polkaswap as it is the main source of liquidity for XSTUSD. As well as being [covered by Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/tatianakoffman/2021/11/23/the-rise-of-decentralized-money-on-polkadotnew-algorithmic-stablecoin-launches-on-sora/?sh=7499e10731bc), there was a [comprehensive article written](/sora-xor/introducing-xstusd-a-stablecoin-on-sora-for-polkadot-ecosystem-public-testing-a3861c13643e) about the origins of stablecoins and the innovations that XSTUSD brings. If you paid attention to the SORA Economic Forum opening, you will also notice that Makoto mentions XSTUSD as a goal for the SORA ecosystem to achieve in 2021!

### Polkaswap 1.6.0 Update

Although Polkaswap was launched this year, towards the end of 2021, it also got a facelift. Polkaswap 1.6.0 featured major UI improvements that further strengthened the Polkaswap experience, along with privacy-focused functions, faster transaction speeds, and more improvements.

### MoonPay in Polkaswap

The latest and one of the greatest implementations to Polkaswap in 2021, was a fiat-onramp via MoonPay. Addressing a question frequently asked about this, credit card companies and other fiat financial entities are still wary about crypto, but optimistic. This is relevant because it is possible to purchase ETH and other ERC-20 tokens (after intense KYC/AML scrutiny), but the same cannot be said for cryptocurrencies in other networks. To make fiat onboarding possible given the current regulatory constraints, first users onramp to the Ethereum network and then transfer to the SORA network. This is still more convenient and less costly in terms of fees than purchasing from a CEX and transferring to Polkaswap. For more information about MoonPay on Polkaswap, [look at this article](/polkaswap/introducing-moonpay-fiat-onramp-in-polkaswap-937570a7448a).
