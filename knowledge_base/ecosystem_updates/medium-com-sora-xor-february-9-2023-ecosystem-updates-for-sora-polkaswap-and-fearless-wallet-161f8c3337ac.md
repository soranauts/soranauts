First, we will cover the business track. Ongoing activities to increase SORA network adoption and educate new users as well as crypto degens on what makes SORA special, never end and are being done continuously. Thank you to each and every community member who provides suggestions and constructive solutions to improve the educational messaging about SORA.

We are happy to announce that there is great progress with the **SORA Card and SORA IBAN**. This is more complicated than the movement of a Swiss clock, so it has taken plenty of time to get each wheel moving precisely, although some wheels are more difficult to integrate than others. There may have been a few delays along the way, but there is always progress. This time is no different, and the SORA Card Phase 1 is right around the corner, with public testing of the SORA Wallet’s **SORAlution test app**, available today on [Google Play for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.sora.communitytesting&hl=en_GB&gl=US) and on [TestFlight for iOS](https://testflight.apple.com/join/670hF438)! SORA Wallet’s **SORAlution test app** available today on [Google Play for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.sora.communitytesting&hl=en_GB&gl=US) and on [TestFlight for iOS](https://testflight.apple.com/join/670hF438)!

The new focus in the business track will be on **Polkaswap as a Service**. On a high level, this is a program to provide increased liquidity for tokens within the DOTSAMA space, including parachains. Many times tokens are only available on CEXs or from parachain lease auctions, making it difficult for users to obtain them. The Polkaswap as a Service program seeks to help make these (_and all tokens_) to be available to users with the style and freedom they have become accustomed to with [Polkaswap.io](http://polkaswap.io/). More information and progress will be available soon, so watch this space!

Let’s move on to the backend track. **The SORA Kusama parachain** and subsequent SORA Polkadot parachains are a strong focus, however, there are fundamentals that need to be in place before bidding in the candlelight auction; in our case, that would be the parachain bridges that will connect these slots to the SORA mainnet. There is great progress on the **Kusama parachain bridge** and it is now undergoing rigorous testing by the QA team, while some devs continue to work on addressing all the issues raised, like clockwork. **The trustless EVM bridge** will come further along the pipeline. Focusing on each piece of crucial infrastructure at a time allows for work of better quality, which in turn is safer and more efficient.

Along with the work connecting the SORA network to other networks, there is also plenty of internal work going on to strengthen, upgrade, and future-proof the SORA blockchain. Work on **Order book functionality** being one of them. That’s right, Polkaswap will have order books added to it to allow for limit orders to be executed in combination with XYK liquidity pools.

The SORA economy guides the development of the network, and a key element to this is the creation of money for productive purposes. The only way that money can be productive is if it holds it’s value, or in other words, if it’s stable. This is where the **SORA Synthetics platform** comes in. Backed by the XST token, and ruled by **XST Tokenomics**, the platform will be able to produce stable assets that hold their value against many existing real-world assets, reducing the risk of volatility that cryptos are subject to due to their speculative nature. On the other hand, we also mentioned that [TBCD, a new stablecoin which is now live, will be used to fund project development and the creation of goods and services in the SORA economy](/sora-xor/introducing-tbcd-ffdb115c9fb8).

Moving on to the web track, [Polkaswap.io](http://Polkaswap.io) is constantly improving the amount and quality of the data available to users. As the main liquidity source of the SORA network and of the SORA economy, it is important that the flow of information to gauge the health of the network is as accurate, accessible, and stylish as possible.

On the other hand, user adoption of the SORA network and also the Substrate ecosystem is a key priority. As an ecosystem, we all benefit from user adoption, and as humanity, we all benefit from decentralization and inclusion to the services that the Polkadot, Kusama, and SORA networks build 24/7/365.

Fearless Wallet as a Substrate-standard that even other teams have built upon, seeks to be the most convenient and user-friendly interface for users of all experience levels. In the last update, we mentioned that the ßeta release of the [**Fearless Wallet Browser extension** is available in the Chrome web store](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/fearless-wallet/nhlnehondigmgckngjomcpcefcdplmgc?utm_source=telegram&utm_medium=post&utm_campaign=announcement), and we extend the ongoing invitation to try it out and give us your feedback and thoughts as work continues toward the full release.

Finally, on the mobile track, with the monumental amount of work that went into the Substrate 4 integration, SORA network assets are now available within Fearless Wallet!

As if that was not enough, **Polkaswap functionality within Fearless Wallet,** although currently limited to swaps, is being worked on as we speak. The team is hard at work implementing liquidity provision, which is a much-loved function by many users.

The business and mobile tracks cross at the **SORA wallet integration of the SORA card**. As mentioned earlier, there has been a lot of time, effort and love invested into a test version of this much-awaited functionality. It is important to mention that after testing is completed, **Phase one** will be available, which means **XOR onramps and signups to the SORA card**. Soon after, **Phase two** will be announced, which will include **fiat top-up and ordering the physical SORA card**. Stay tuned for these important milestones, which you can rest assured you will find in this section of the SORA ecosystem updates.

* Fixing PriceTools which didn’t use XYK market for the XOR-XSTUSD pair

### About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a noncustodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystem, with native support for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) platforms. A premium user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
