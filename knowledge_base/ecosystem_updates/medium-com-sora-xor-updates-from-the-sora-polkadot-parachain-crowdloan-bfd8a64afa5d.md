### TL;DR

* The SORA Polkadot parachain project could not secure a parachain in the 46th to 52nd batch of parachain auctions. Congratulations to the winners.
* The SORA Polkadot Parachain auction continues in batches 53 to 59 and needs your support to win the coveted Polkadot Parachain Slot.
* Rewards for the SORA Crowdloan have been updated to 0.1337gr of synthetic gold in the form of the upcoming Kensetsu Gold token.

The Pursuit of a Polkadot Parachain
-----------------------------------

Securing a Polkadot parachain has been much more complex than securing a Kusama one. The competition, with parachains fiercely outbidding each other and the major ecosystem players with ample liquidity self-funding their slots, has made what could potentially be the last full-cycle prepaid parachains before the pay-as-you-go model is introduced in the Polkadot relay chain and projects either want to have a safe spot before finding out the details of the new system or want to ensure that the current parachain period that is ending transitions smoothly into the new one, for the benefit and peace of mind of network users.

Whichever one, Polkadot parachains are coveted in the Substrate space; for SORA, it is no different. By having a Polkadot parachain, putting the Polka in Polkaswap (or the DOT) will be a full circle and a testament to the belief in the interoperability values that the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems stand for. And as we can see, SORA isn’t the only team that believes this.

### Where Are We at?

A new cycle of auctions began on the 24th of October, and with that, the SORA community embarked on another Polkadot Parachain project. The contribution cap remains the same, at a maximum of **333 333 DOT** to secure the parachain (_the highest contribution so far was by the EWT community, who secured their parachain with 299 999 DOT and only 114 contributors_).

The rewards per DOT will be different this time. It is still 0.1337 gr of gold. However, it will change from the XSTXAU synthetic gold token to the upcoming Kensetsu Gold. Unfortunately, more information cannot be provided about the Kensetsu tokenomics, besides being **an over-collateralised stable asset pegged to a value index**, but stay tuned to the SORA bi-weekly ecosystem updates for more insights.

### How Can I Participate?

This is twice as exciting as the only parachain project giving out gilded rewards to their contributors and ahead of an interesting token unique to the entire DOTsama ecosystem.

Participating in the SORA Polkadot Parachain crowdloan is easy; you can pledge your DOT in your favourite wallet supporting Substrate networks ([Fearless](https://fearlesswallet.io/), Talisman, Subwallet, etc.) or directly in the [Polkadot.js (Dotapps.io) interface](https://polkadot.js.org/apps/?rpc=wss%3A%2F%2Frpc.polkadot.io&ref=parachains-info#/parachains/crowdloan). Additionally, you will soon be able to contribute using the [Bifrost V-crowdloan interface](https://bifrost.app/vcrowdloan), receiving SALP in the process.

Finally, remember that apart from the rewards, if the SORA Polkadot Parachain project is successful, you will also be returned the amount contributed at the end of the parachain lease.

### About SORA

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is a new economic order for the world, aimed at high growth and empowering human progress. The SORA network includes interoperability with the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) networks, as well as the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.
