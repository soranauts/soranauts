SORA Integrated Plan
--------------------

Press enter or click to view image in full size

The SORA Integrated Plan [https://sora.org/plan](https://sora.org/plan)

**Many worlds, one economy.**

This section is typically reserved for bringing you up to speed with the last two weeks of development in the SORA ecosystem, as well as other interesting occurrences. However, today’s update is different and exciting, as the entire update has been revamped to make it easier to stay informed about the topics that interest you most in the ecosystem. As they say, the only constant is change, and these updates are no exception.

We introduced this section when the SORA Integrated Plan came into effect, mapping out the development and giving you a glimpse into the direction that the project was heading. This time, the updates themselves are changing. Therefore, in this particular article, you will only see updates for SORA. If you are interested in Polkaswap or Fearless Wallet updates, those will be available on their respective Medium pages.

Don’t worry too much, though; the updates will still be released at the same time on the same day, so you can still catch up on what developers are saying. Alright, let’s dive into SORA summer!

The much-awaited SORA Kusama parachain bridge is almost ready for release. It is currently undergoing internal testing, with a focus on the user interface. Specifically, transfers from the SORA network to Kusama parachains are being tested, while transfers from Kusama parachains to the SORA network are having their UI built. Once both are ready, you will be able to test them!

Meanwhile, work on the multi-EVM bridge is still ongoing, so patience will be required for that. Thank you very much for your support. Let’s cross this bridge when we get there.

The development of order books is progressing well, although a few bugs still need to be addressed before testing can begin. The addition of order book features complements trading very well and is integrated directly into Polkaswap’s Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT) algorithm, and the XST platform is currently undergoing public testing. Band protocol will soon be integrated into the production network, and before you know it, XST assets will be available for stylish and free trading on Polkaswap. Stay tuned for more updates as the release date approaches!

On the mobile front, the network’s best kept secret, the Demeter Farming platform, is coming soon to mobile. Work is steadily progressing on giving it a simple yet intuitive UI/UX to match, so stay tuned!

Finally, a special message from SORA Card:

“_SORA Card is progressing every day, the final hiccups are being ironed out and reviewed. We were asked to communicate less details about the process status. Unfortunately, this can only jeopardise the delicate protocol involving highly regulated stakeholders. Furthermore, elaborate updates will not help delivery go faster. When we go live, you will be the first to know._ **_SORA Card is coming!”_**

It is unfortunate that the current financial system doesn’t respond well to transparency, but we hope that through the SORA new economic order, this obscure status quo will change for the better.

You are now up to date with what has happened in the last few weeks in the SORA ecosystem and the current state of the SORA Integrated Plan. Please stay tuned for upcoming news from all the SORA contributors, and we’ll see you again in two weeks. Let’s get back to work!

\***This is not financial advice. Always make sure to do your own research!**

**_You can find the summary of the integrated plan points in the_** [**_SORA Wiki_**](https://wiki.sora.org/sora-integrated-plan)**_._**

⚙️ SORA Blockchain Update
-------------------------

* Preparing for the launch of the new wiki as a support for newcomers, advanced users, and SORA builders.

Substrate and EVM bridges
-------------------------

The Polkaswap team has released the Substrate bridge UI on the internal testnet to test transfers from SORA to Karura (Kusama parachain).

* Transfers are working properly.
* We are currently working on implementing transfers on Polkaswap for transfers from Karura (Kusama parachains) to SORA before releasing them to the public testnet.

Preparing the infrastructure to release the Substrate bridge and UI on the public testnet:

* Synchronising a Relay Chain
* Setting up Relayers
* Indexing off-chain workers

XST Platform ([Github Milestone](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/milestone/14))
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* XST platform support has been implemented on Polkaswap.
* The tokens whitelist has been updated with the 30 new synthetic tokens on public testnet. The XST platform can be tested on [https://test.polkaswap.io/#/swap](https://test.polkaswap.io/#/swap) and on mobile testing apps ([iOS](https://testflight.apple.com/join/670hF438), [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.sora.communitytesting&hl=en_GB&gl=US)).
* Synchronising with the Oracle data feed provider to finalise testing on public test before starting the integration on the production environment.
* Working on the architecture and implementation of the solution to mitigate front-running on synthetic assets on the network.

Order book ([Github Milestone](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora2-network/milestone/4))
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

* Finalising the review of the implementation of swaps through order books.
* Preparing for the release on the staging (public test) environment.
* Collaborating with the frontend application teams to integrate into Polkaswap and the SORA and Fearless wallets.

About SORA
----------

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is a new economic order for the world aimed at high growth and empowering human progress. The SORA network includes interoperability with the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) networks, as well as the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.
