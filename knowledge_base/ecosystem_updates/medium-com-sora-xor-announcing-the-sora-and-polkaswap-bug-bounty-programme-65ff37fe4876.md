TL;DR
-----

* In collaboration with Immunefi, a bug bounty of up to $70K USD will be provided to whoever finds a bug in SORA and Polkaswap
* Rewards will be distributed according to the [Immunefi Vulnerability Severity Classification System](https://immunefi.com/immunefi-vulnerability-severity-classification-system-v2-1/)
* The SORA Bug Bounty Programme is live now at [https://immunefi.com/bounty/sora/](https://immunefi.com/bounty/sora/)

🛡 Calling all bug hunters to help us strengthen the security of the space

Calling all bug hunters to help us strengthen the security of the space.

> **_Attention Doctors, Engineers, power users, etc, this is your opportunity to put your unique set of skills to the test and help improve the safety of SORA and Polkaswap in the process (_as well as a nice bounty_)._**

Together with the utmost authority in web3 when it comes to bug bounties, [**Immunefi**](https://twitter.com/immunefi), this programme will help the team find and fix vulnerabilities that will help secure the network and its systemically important infrastructure. This means that we will be playing by [**Immunefi’s rules**](https://immunefi.com/rules/), so make sure that you familiarise yourself with them before participating.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

🔬🐞 SORA and Polkaswap Bug Bounty Programme is live on [Immunefi](https://immunefi.com/bounty/sora/)!

Rewards will be distributed based on the [**Immunefi V.S.C. system**](https://immunefi.com/immunefi-vulnerability-severity-classification-system-v2-1/), so even those of you without a doctorate in Computer Science, but a lot of enthusiasm and self-taught skills can participate. It is important to note that you must provide a proof of concept with your submission, otherwise it will not qualify.

We appreciate your interest in keeping the SORA network and its SII secure for the wellbeing of the entire community.

### **_🔬🐞 SORA and Polkaswap bug bounty programme is live NOW on_** [**_https://immunefi.com/bounty/sora/_**](https://immunefi.com/bounty/sora/)

About Immunefi
--------------

[**Immunefi**](https://immunefi.com/) is the premier bug bounty platform for smart contracts and DeFi projects, where security researchers review code, disclose vulnerabilities, get paid, and make crypto safer. [Immunefi](https://medium.com/immunefi) removes security risks through bug bounties and comprehensive security services.

About SORA and Polkaswap
------------------------

Press enter or click to view image in full size

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a noncustodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).
