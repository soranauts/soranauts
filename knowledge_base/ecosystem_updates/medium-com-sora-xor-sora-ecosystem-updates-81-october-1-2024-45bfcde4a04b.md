### You Are Here

As the Earth slowly begins to rotate away from the sun and days become shorter in the Northern Hemisphere, the state of mind known as the Summer of SORA shows no signs of stopping. With the Kensetsu platform live and KUSD-based liquidity and order book pairs available on Polkaswap, the pivot toward the SORA v3 tokenomics is in full swing.

The most exciting part of SORA v3 tokenomics was also proposed, voted on, and released: [SORA Vested XOR (vXOR)](https://medium.com/sora-xor/v-for-vxor-841d92157225). This new companion token to XOR will be used to incentivize producers in the SORA economy.

Also, teased as Secret 9 and taking sweet summertime to change the plan, for those eagle-eyed who pointed out in the chats that this secret had been revealed, but the plan had not been updated accordingly, the tokenomics upgrade has been a multifaceted process. Rome wasn’t built in a day, nor was the SORA economy.

_”You say, ‘I am rich; I have acquired wealth and do not need a thing.’ But you do not realize that you are wretched, pitiful, poor, blind and naked.”_ — [**_Revelation 3:17_**](https://biblia.com/bible/esv/revelation/3/17)

Revelations aside, this edition is a little different. Usually, all the fun and the bulk of the work done is summarised in this section, leaving the buidlrs feeling somewhat neglected, as the details are pretty much overlooked, so this time, the spotlight leaves this, your favourite part of the SORA Ecosystem Updates, and spreads this across the publication. Take a look at the interesting work being done for SORATOPIA, and don’t forget to [pray](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FyXGjT3oPb8) before undertaking the tasks as you journey toward Bushido or degeneracy; to each their own.

Then, follow the Silk Road to enlightenment about the different milestones being worked on. From ALT to Kensetsu, by way of the Bridges and then on to wallets and community builders, the progress made in September has been spectacular.

Without further ado, read on to discover what the SORA Builders have been up to and hopefully, this also inspires you to make your blockchain beeswax a reality. You have all the tools available to you, including community support; all that remains is for you to stack the first block.

You are now up to date with the recent happenings in the SORA ecosystem and the current status of the SORA Integrated Plan. Stay tuned for upcoming news from all the SORA contributors, and we’ll meet again soon. **Now, let’s get back to work!**

### ALT (Aggregate Liquidity Technology) Integration with the Order book

ALT is an innovative mechanism based on Dr [Makoto Takemiya’s paper](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10174911), which aims to optimally combine different liquidity sources in Polkaswap to provide the best trading experience. By aggregating liquidity from multiple sources, ALT ensures that users receive the most efficient and competitive trades.

* **Final Testing Ongoing**: The last stages of testing the ALT integration are ongoing to ensure everything runs smoothly.
* **Continuous Synchronization**: The front-end developers are closely coordinating to make real-time adjustments, ensuring a seamless user experience for all.

### Kensetsu

Kensetsu, the overcollateralized stablecoin platform, is making strides with several key updates this month. Starting with a stable asset pegged to USD and one to the Troy ounce of Gold RWA, Kensetsu is expanding its offerings and opportunities for users. Here’s what’s new:

**KUSD-Based Pools Now Available on Polkaswap**

* Users can now create pools with KUSD on Polkaswap, providing a new avenue to increase liquidity and rewards.

**KUSD-Based Order Books are Live on Polkaswap**

* Users can now add KUSD pairs to the Polkaswap order book and start trading, unlocking more beneficial opportunities with this stable asset.

**New Collateral Tokens**

* The list of collateral tokens has been expanded to include **DOT, PUSSY, SB, DEO, and KARMA**. This means more flexibility and collateral opportunities to obtain Kensetsu tokens.

**XOR-KUSD Farming Pool with KEN Rewards**

* A new farming pool is now available on the Ceres platform, where users can stake XOR-KUSD pairs and earn KEN rewards.

**KGOLD Borrowing Now Available**

* The recently released stablecoin, **KGOLD**, pegged to Gold, is now available for borrowing. Users can use a variety of collateral tokens, including **VAL, PSWAP, TBCD, DAI, DOT, ETH, KXOR, PUSSY, and SB**, to borrow KGOLD. Soon, Polkadot crowdloan rewards in KGOLD will also be distributed.

Stay tuned for more updates. The Kensetsu platform is continuously being enhanced and expanded!

### Bridges

Bridges are a vital component of the SORA ecosystem, enabling interoperability that is unavailable anywhere else to this degree and providing seamless asset transfers between connected networks. Once assets are bridged into SORA, you can use your favourite tokens across the wide range of services, products, and tools available within the ecosystem. SORA is committed to continuously expanding its bridge offerings, forging meaningful partnerships to enhance connectivity and provide greater opportunities for our community.

### **Multi-EVM Bridge**

* Currently undergoing testing to ensure seamless integration across multiple EVM-based networks.
* Evaluating and optimizing commission structures across different networks to provide the most efficient transactions for users.

### **TON Bridge**

* The network is in the final stages of testing, bringing it closer to connecting the TON blockchain to the SORA ecosystem for greater interoperability and asset flow.

### About SORA

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is an innovative, adaptive, non-debt-based monetary framework that facilitates economic stability, particularly for financially vulnerable countries. As a groundbreaking platform, SORA exemplifies the potential of blockchain technology in fostering a more inclusive and stable global financial system. Its integration in projects like the Bokolo Cash CBDC signifies a new era in digital currency, aligning technological advancements to achieve broader economic stability and resilience. Through its unique approach and capabilities, SORA plays a pivotal role in the evolution of global finance, particularly by supporting economies that need innovative and sustainable financial solutions the most.
