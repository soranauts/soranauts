Join us to celebrate the SORA ecosystem's remarkable potential through the **SORA Kusama two-way bridge campaign**, the second phase in our campaign series. This campaign is your gateway to discovering the interoperability between the Kusama Relay chain and the SORA network, enhancing your blockchain experience.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

### Campaign Overview

* **🏁 Participate via Gleam:** [**here**](https://gleam.io/SpDKM/30000-sora-kusama-bridge-giveaway-phase-2)**.**
* **Duration:** 3 weeks, from November 27th to December 27th, 2023.
* **Prize pool:** $30,000 in XOR tokens.
* **Maximum reward for participants:** $60.

### Rewards Distribution

* Follow, retweet, and bridge: **$15.**
* Liquidity provision for two weeks: **$20**.
* Liquidity provision for four weeks: **$30**.

**❗️Note: Rewards for LP are not summarized.**

* Reward for crowdloan participation: **$10.**
* Early bird reward for participation within 48 hours of the campaign start: **$5**

### Campaign Requirements

1. **Proof Requirement.** Share transaction hashes for liquidity provision tasks.
2. **Social Media Post Requirement.** You must share the airdrop post on social media (copy to get the link and paste it on your tweet and share) and follow these SORA ecosystem accounts [https://t.me/sora\_xor](https://t.me/sora_xor), [https://twitter.com/sora\_xor](https://twitter.com/sora_xor), [https://discord.com/invite/4TXRN6Y4gb](https://discord.com/invite/4TXRN6Y4gb), and [https://medium.com/sora-xor](https://medium.com/sora-xor).

🚨**IMPORTANT RULE**🚨

* **_Your actions will ONLY count if performed AFTER the airdrop campaign kick-off. So, keep your eyes on the clock and ensure you’re on the mark!_**
* **_Even if the Gleam platform shows your action as completed, it doesn’t guarantee it’s done right or that the reward will be paid. Ensure you follow the guidelines correctly._**
* **_The bridge is a mandatory step that needs to be done to proceed with further steps._**

Additionally, you must provide a link as evidence that you shared the airdrop post.

### How to Participate: Steps and Guidelines

### Step 1: Social Media Engagement

* **Follow SORA social media**: Follow SORA on [X](https://twitter.com/sora_xor), [Telegram](https://t.me/sora_announcements), [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/4TXRN6Y4gb), and [Medium](/@sora-xor).
* **Proof of Participation**: Submit your usernames as proof of the following on Gleam.

### Step 2: Campaign Promotion

* Spread awareness by sharing informative posts about the SORA Kusama Bridge Campaign. Use our unique campaign hashtag #SORAManyChains
* Retweet the campaign announcement.
* Provide links to your post as proof of sharing on Gleam.

### Step 3: Buy KSM (in case you don’t have any)

Suppose you don’t own KSM tokens and would like to buy them; in that case, you can do it directly on [**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) ([here’s how to do it](https://wiki.fearlesswallet.io/getting-started/how-do-i-buy-crypto-in-fearless-wallet)), or you can buy KSM anywhere else and send the tokens to your Fearless Wallet account ([here’s how to receive tokens to your Fearless Wallet](https://wiki.fearlesswallet.io/getting-started/how-do-i-send-and-receive-crypto#receive-crypto)).

### Step 4: Bridge KSM Tokens via [Polkaswap’s Bridge](https://polkaswap.io/#/bridge/) (MANDATORY STEP)

* Bridging process: **Bridge a minimum of $10 KSM tokens from the Kusama Relay Chain to the SORA network using** [**Polkaswap DEX**](https://polkaswap.io/)**.** Be mindful of the network fees (_approximately $0.01 Kusama-SORA, ~$2.5 SORA-Kusama_).
* Proof of bridging: Copy your transaction IDs as proof and submit them on Gleam.
* **You must download browser extension wallets like** [**Fearless Wallet**](https://qrcodes.pro/dr0Fwi), Polkadot.JS, or any of your favourite Substrate wallets in order to bridge on Polkaswap.
* Reward: $15 for successful bridging.

### Step 5: Provide Liquidity (Optional)

* Buy XOR (to pay tx fees): Swap KSM or other tokens for XOR before adding liquidity.

[**Tutorial: How to swap on Polkaswap**](https://wiki.sora.org/swap.html)**.**

* Add Liquidity: Contribute to the XOR-KSM pool on Polkaswap with at least $100 ($50 in XOR and $50 in KSM).

**Tutorial**[**: How to Provide Liquidity on Polkaswap**](https://wiki.sora.org/provide-liquidity-to-xyk-pools.html)**.**

* Duration: Provide liquidity for 2 or 4 weeks at the very least.

**Rewards:**

$20 for two weeks of liquidity provision

$30 for four weeks of liquidity provision.

_🌾 Aside from the campaign reward, you will also earn built-in double rewards in Polkaswap (Fees gained from LP and strategic rewards, also known as farming rewards in Polkaswap)._

### Step 5: Participate in SORA Polkadot Crowdloan (Optional)

* The SORA Polkadot Parachain Crowdloan Campaign is ongoing; with a Polkadot parachain secured, you will be able to trade DOT and other Polkadot parachain tokens within Polkaswap, making it the interoperable finance hub of the Polkadot/Kusama ecosystem.
* Participate in the SORA Polkadot Crowdloan by contributing DOT for SORA to secure a parachain lease.
* Reward: $10
* 🎁 Contributors will be rewarded 0.1337 gr of synthetic Gold (Kensetsu Gold) for every DOT contributed.

### Tutorial: How to participate in SORA Polkadot Crowdloan using the Fearless Wallet mobile app

**First and foremost, download the** [**Fearless Wallet mobile app**](https://qrcodes.pro/03tA5y), Polkadot Js, or any of your favourite substrate wallets that allow Polkadot crowdloan contributions.

Ensure you have enough **DOT** balance; the minimum contribution amount is 5 DOT. With that sorted, navigate to the **Crowdloans** section at the bottom of the Fearless Wallet interface.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

**NOTE:** Tokens used to participate must be transferable — that is, **not locked for any reason, including staking, vesting, and governance** — because they will be moved into a module-controlled account generated uniquely for this campaign. [More information here.](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/learn-crowdloans)

Then, determine how many tokens you contribute, preview the reward you could receive and the leasing period, and more information, such as the amount raised by the project and the time left to contribute.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

The SORA Polkadot Crowdloan should now appear in the “Your contributions” section of the Crowdloan screen on Fearless Wallet.

Share the crowdloan transaction ID on Gleam to qualify for this task’s rewards.

**Receive an additional $5 bonus for completing tasks within the first 48 hours of the campaign.**

Stay tuned for future innovative opportunities in our campaign series and Phase 3! Your participation is crucial in shaping an interconnected blockchain landscape.

### About SORA

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is a new economic order for the world, aimed at high growth and empowering human progress. The SORA network includes interoperability with the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) networks, as well as the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.
