TL;DR
-----

* As of the [November 3rd Ecosystem Update](/sora-xor/november-3rd-2021-ecosystem-updates-for-sora-polkaswap-and-fearless-wallet-412bb2283eeb), RFP status updates have been included, however, they are limited to outlining the status, not the contents.
* This post will provide a high-level description of each RFP submitted, as well as the Proposals open currently.
* RFP requests can be submitted by any community member [following these guidelines](/sora-xor/sora-polkaswap-decentralized-development-process-6df207d7518a). If you have never drafted a proposal before, [start here](/sora-xor/how-to-rfp-proposing-new-functionalities-to-polkaswap-and-the-sora-network-4af099b15f75).

Ongoing and Requested Proposals
-------------------------------

For the RFP to be implemented, first it requires approval, and then it becomes a proposal. The main difference between an RFP and a proposal is that there is a due date and a development budget included.

It is important to note that although Requested Proposals have been submitted, and are available within the RFP Repo, they are not certain to be developed, and if they are, there is no established deadline, until a proposal including this information is merged.

Follow-up
---------

After this summary covering the proposals and requests currently active, better details will be provided for the ongoing RFP’s and PR’s as part of the [SORA Ecosystem Updates](/sora-xor/december-15th-2021-ecosystem-updates-for-sora-polkaswap-and-fearless-wallet-e2dcbbb66dd5). The goal of this summary is to provide the community with an easy-to-digest insight on the current proposals, as well as the RFP submitted from the beginning of the program that may not have been explained to the general public and to help users with different levels of technological prowess, or users that do not have access to or are not comfortable with Github.

_If you liked what you saw in the summary, consider giving the proposal a thumbs-up 👍 or loveheart ❤️ to show your support, otherwise, if you have any feedback or reservations on a proposal, consider leaving a comment within the PR, as comments to this article will have no effect in addressing a particular proposal or RFP._

**_To see all the RFP proposed,_** [**_look here_**](https://github.com/sora-xor/rfps/pulls)**_. To see the RFP that have passed the proposal process and are currently being worked on, you can_** [**_find those here_**](https://github.com/sora-xor/rfps/tree/master/open_rfps)**_. Finally, if you want to make a proposal of your own, the RFP Guidelines are available_** [**_here_**](/sora-xor/sora-polkaswap-decentralized-development-process-6df207d7518a)**_, a beginner’s guide to RFP is available_** [**_here_**](/sora-xor/how-to-rfp-proposing-new-functionalities-to-polkaswap-and-the-sora-network-4af099b15f75)**_, or how to participate in the SORA Builders Programme is available_** [**_here_**](/sora-xor/sora-builders-programme-979bea8831ed)**_._**

About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------

[SORA](https://sora.org/)
-------------------------

**SORA** is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

[Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/)
----------------------------------

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is **Polkaswap**, a non-custodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind **Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT)**.

[Fearless Wallet](https://fearlesswallet.io/)
---------------------------------------------

**Fearless Wallet** is a mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Kusama and Polkadot ecosystem, with support for iOS and Android platforms. An awesome user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate Polkaswap for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
