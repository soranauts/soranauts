At this point, you might be wondering what the benefit of having a bank is anyway since it’s the flaws in the banking system that led you to cryptocurrencies in the first place. It is a valid question for sure, and one that is often taken for granted, especially if you have been following [SORA Daily](https://t.me/Soradaily). In the latest video, a snippet from the 2021 SORA Economic Forum where Professor Richard Werner explains the “Quantitative Theory of Disaggregated Credit”:

Well, as it turns out, you need a way for the economy to be circular, constantly buying and selling, instead of accumulating and speculating, for it to make the biggest impact. Initially, users will be able to apply for a European International Bank Account Number (IBAN), with more fiat currencies becoming available, that will bridge a gap that is wider than the Great Rift Valley, which is the void between DeFi and TradFi. SORA is working very hard to implement XOR as legal tender. This is an uphill battle, but thanks to the hard work and support of all the people interested and collaborating, we are able to keep on climbing until we are finally successful.

In the meantime, having access to the “traditional” system, through SORA Card will allow the SORA economy to begin the proverbial economic circle, as people will be able to use their cryptocurrencies, which are a **unit of account**, to pay for and receive payment for IRL goods and services, which will help XOR become a **medium of exchange**, and be more widely recognised as a **store of value**, accomplishing the key functions of money.

“_What’s the difference between SORA Card and any other neobanking offering?_” is a frequently asked community question, and the simplest answer is **freedom**. SORA Card gives you the freedom to manage your finances however you please. Using [Polkaswap.io](http://Polkaswap.io) as the hub for your banking activities gives you the power, and the responsibility that comes from the freedom of decentralized finance. The next FAQ is “_why do I need to do KYC for the IBAN?_” some people even follow up by saying that it’s contradictory or hypocritical, given that you are disclosing your personal details. However, think of it this way: if you’re crossing the HASHI bridge from the Ethereum network into SORA, you pay gas fees in ETH. Similarly, if you are accessing TradFi through DeFi, you need to comply with the rules of each network you interact with.

### About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a noncustodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystem, with native support for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) platforms. A premium user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
