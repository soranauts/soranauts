Fearless Wallet Ecosystem Update #28
------------------------------------

🎁 **The demo** for Sprint 0709 was conducted today (22nd of September). Recent dev updates and roadmap refinements were presented. Watch the recording on [YouTube](https://youtu.be/5svrHj3RzsE) and get the [slides here](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1EmW_8VgVAYrybW0qOzs6bRYd6nO3R_ePZK6S0xIpP4c/edit?usp=sharing)

🎉 **New releases are available in the Google Play & AppStore**, we highly recommend updating your apps! Update overviews:

🚀 **Android v1.9.4** now populates history on the Wallet screen with Staking rewards and other operations, migration from Subscan API to SubQuery & Coingecko API, as well as fixed false-positive alerts of Oversubscribed validators, min stake & other issues. **Changelog:** [https://github.com/soramitsu/fearless-Android/releases/tag/v1.9.4](https://github.com/soramitsu/fearless-Android/releases/tag/v1.9.4)

🍎 **iOS v1.9.5–1.9.7** brings networking optimization & issue fixes. **Changelogs:** [https://github.com/soramitsu/fearless-iOS/releases](https://github.com/soramitsu/fearless-iOS/releases)

📊 **The upcoming release of Staking analytics on iOS (v1.10)** is undergoing QA verification — the team is testing & improving features and we are planning to push it to the AppStore this week.

🌐 **Multi-asset & New network release (v2.0)** is in progress:

* A new model has been implemented for the storage of Accounts & Secrets, as well as migration from the old data structure to a new one.
* A new networking approach which allows the mobile app to connect to multiple networks is finished
* The team is close to completing a re-write of the domain logic of the app to use a new networking approach
* The next Sprint will focus on the new UI features, such as a multi-asset Wallet, updated screens for Crowdloans & Staking, and updates for the accounts screen
* The FW team expects to launch open beta testing of v2.0 in 3 weeks

About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------

Press enter or click to view image in full size

[SORA](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/), a non custodial liquidity aggregating, cross chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

Press enter or click to view image in full size

[Fearless Wallet](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Kusama and Polkadot ecosystem, with support for iOS and Android platforms. An awesome user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate Polkaswap for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
