### You Are Here

We have made it to December in one piece. This is the final update of the year, but certainly not the least exciting! We’ll open this edition with the biggest news so far: **SORA Card is now live!** Yes, after much duress and regulatory complexity, the virtual card and IBAN features are now available to all (EEA residents, for now)!

We’ve made significant progress with the TON bridge, which is now operational (for now, only one-way, from TON→SORA). While the user interface is still in development, once complete, it will greatly enhance the SORATOPIA experience! If you haven’t been keeping up with your SORATOPIA tasks yet, don’t worry — [there’s still time to join in](https://t.me/soratopia_bot/app?startapp=9K5R4FZZCEY32).

While stability is a valuable feature for a means of exchange and store of value, it may not be as exciting as trading volatile shitcoins (or fartcoins?). However, for many users, reliability matters more than thrills. Behind the scenes, significant changes have strengthened KUSD’s dollar peg, making it the preferred token for builders.

Adding Buy-Back-and-Burn features, where a portion of transaction fees on the SORA network and Polkaswap go to buy back and burn KUSD, reinforces KUSD’s stability with each transaction. Users can participate by opening a Kensetsu vault position in Polkaswap or providing LP on the Demeter platform for KUSD to receive KEN. A stable future makes all the difference — and now is the ideal time to get involved.

Community contributors have been hard at work on [TONSWAP](https://tonswap-org.medium.com/introducing-tonswap-260536a78c6b), the best new TON DEX you never knew you needed. Stepping away from the TON DEXs that are unfashionable and slow, [TONSWAP will be using SORA technology](https://tonswap-org.medium.com/tonswap-a-telegram-native-dex-for-mass-adoption-bcf83c1ab4eb), _SORA v3,_ to be more precise, will allow for a better user experience with much more powerful features.

Before you even blurt it out, yes, it makes sense for a TON DEX to be on another network because the user experience, a native Telegram app, will be so seamless, that you won’t even notice you are not on the token’s native chain. I mean, does anyone really care about specific blockchains anymore? All that matters is that you have your assets, and your transactions are quick, easy, and, for many people, mobile. On the other hand, TONSWAP base pairs will be with TON, so it’s all good….

The annual SORA Economic Forum in November provided valuable insights into various aspects of economics and finance — from SORA V3 developments to the inner workings of banking systems. If you missed the live event, recordings are now available on the SORA X account and Telegram chat. Among the standout presentations was Clement Berthou’s explanation of payment flows, which highlighted an important principle: to transform the legacy system, we must first understand how it operates.

“What is SORA v3?”, “When will CBDCs operate on a public blockchain?”, “What makes you so special?” Find answers to these existential questions and more in Dr. Takemiya’s keynote about the next level upgrade for the SORA network…

Recently, both LP swap fees and transaction fees have increased. While swap fees maintain a steady fiat value of ¢20, the higher LP fees will provide greater rewards to liquidity providers — a welcome holiday gift for the community.

Stay tuned for the _Year in Review_ publication coming at the end of the month, where we’ll celebrate the remarkable developments and milestones achieved this year as we look ahead to a promising 2025. ### Tokenomics Upgrade

To ensure the SORA ecosystem remains responsive to the market dynamics, the SORA community is always hard at work to fine-tune the tokenomics parameters to align the incentives with the needs of the ecosystem.

Seeing the current needs in increasing the incentives to provide liquidity, we introduced an increase in the LP swap fee from 0.3% to 0.6% on Polkaswap.

### Bridges

Bridges form a vital part of the SORA ecosystem, providing top-tier interoperability for seamless asset transfers between different networks. Soon, with XORless transfers, users will be able to bridge their assets into SORA and use their preferred tokens throughout the ecosystem’s wide array of services, products, and tools.

SORA is expanding its bridge offerings and building strategic partnerships to enhance network connectivity and create more opportunities for our community in this next-generation DeFi hub.

### TON Bridge

* The work on the 1st phase of the bridge (TON → SORA transfers only) is complete and the bridge has been released. Operationalisation of the bridge has been ongoing by registering the federated relayers to ensure smooth and secured communication between the TON and SORA ecosystems.
* The community can already add liquidity to the XOR/TON liquidity pool to facilitate onboarding our new friends from the TON network, into the SORA ecosystem!
* Because SORATOPIA is a Telegram mini app, we thought it was important to enable TON users to onboard as soon and as easily as possible. For that reason, we haven’t waited for the TON bridge to be operational in both directions, but instead to make it available one way at at time. The blockchain development and SORATOPIA teams are continuously collaborating to make the experience as best can be.
* Following the release of the 1st phase (TON → SORA transfers only), the team has been continuing working on phase 2 to unlock transfers of assets between the TON and SORA ecosystems in both directions.

### Multi-EVM Bridge

Most of the efforts is being focus on fully enabling the TON bridge; nevertheless, the testing of transfers to BNB Smart Chain has seen some progress on the test environment. We are hopeful that we can release the Multi-EVM bridge on public testnet soon, to enable the broader community to help us ensure the best possible experience before releasing it to production.

### Builders

* The development team has been working on an innovative solution for a globally known financial institution. The solution has been presented and approved by the institution and we are hopeful to officially announce its development on the SORA network as soon as we can legally do so!
* SORA is all about building a new global financial system as a community. We’ve been developing tools for builders to support business use cases:
* Vesting feature to support the decentralised, scheduled distribution of tokens to ecosystem supporters
* To reduce operational and financial risks, having a robust multi-signature functionality is essential for any business that wants to operate on the SORA network. We’ve been collaborating closely with the builders to understand their needs and reflect it in the implementation and user experience.
* Users shouldn’t have to worry about holding a specific token for transaction fees. We’ve developed transactions that let users pay fees in their preferred token instead of requiring XOR explicitly. While XOR remains essential for tokenomics and will be used behind the scenes, users won’t need to manage it directly. The team is currently testing these XORless transactions and plans to release them in the coming weeks.

### 📱 SORA Wallet Updates

SORA is dedicated to designing the Future for the People. In our modern world, we must empower anyone to access their finance and favourite tools anywhere, anytime.

Wallets are a critical infrastructure for a New Economic Order where self-custody keeps people’s digital belongings safe. For this reason, we are continuously working to enhance the wallet experience.

### SORA Card

While we still interact with traditional financial systems, we offer users secure self-custody of their assets with the ability to convert to fiat currency as needed — enabling payments for products and services that haven’t yet integrated with SORA.

We continuously collaborate with external partners to provide the best experience for our users and community.

* Apple (iOS) and Google (Android) are reviewing improvements to the implementation of the SORA wallet. Once it is ready and approved, we will release a new app dedicated to your SORA Card operations.
* The development of the crypto-to-fiat exchange functionality continues, enabling a seamless transition to SORA.

### About SORA

[**SORA**](https://sora.org/) is an innovative, adaptive, non-debt-based monetary framework that facilitates economic stability, particularly for financially vulnerable countries. As a groundbreaking platform, SORA exemplifies the potential of blockchain technology in fostering a more inclusive and stable global financial system. Its integration in projects like the Bokolo Cash CBDC signifies a new era in digital currency, aligning technological advancements to achieve broader economic stability and resilience. Through its unique approach and capabilities, SORA plays a pivotal role in the evolution of global finance, particularly by supporting economies that need innovative and sustainable financial solutions the most.
