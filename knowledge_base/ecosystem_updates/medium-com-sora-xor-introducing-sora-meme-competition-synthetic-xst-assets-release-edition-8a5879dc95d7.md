Let the meme games begin…
-------------------------

Last week’s release of the XOR SynThetix asset $XSTUSD was smooth as silk. Therefore, to celebrate the launch of $XSTUSD, a **meme contest** will be taking place. Show your creativity by designing a meme inspired by SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet and you could win one of several prizes described below…

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Meme Submission Guidelines
--------------------------

1. All contributions must be submitted on [Twitter](https://twitter.com)
2. **Submission tweets must have the #SORAmeme hashtag and $XOR $PSWAP $VAL ca$htags**

Rules
-----

* Be creative, have fun, meme something extraordinary
* Submit as many memes as you can
* Memes must be related to [SORA](http://www.sora.org), [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) and/or [Fearless Wallet](http://www.fearlesswallet.io)
* Memes should be funny and dank
* **Content that is hateful or racist, as well as NSFW, harmful, or illegal will not be accepted.**

Meme Contest Prizes 🏆
----------------------

* 🥇First place memes: 625 VAL tokens and 6 cans of Noir (if shipping is available, otherwise 200 VAL will be given)
* 🥈Second place memes: 500 VAL tokens and 3 cans of Noir (if shipping is available, otherwise 100 VAL will be given)
* 🥉Third place memes: 300 VAL tokens

🏅Further 10 memes will be rewarded with 50 VAL tokens by our memetic researchers

Timeline
--------

* Start: Today (November 24th, 2021)
* End: All submissions are due at 00:00 UTC on December 1st, 2021
* Winners announcement: December 8th, 2021

Branding Guidelines
-------------------

Memes are about fun and creativity first and foremost, we don’t expect you to stick to branding guidelines in this case — but if you want, you can download SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet brand assets here and use them as you please: [https://github.com/sora-xor/sora-branding](https://github.com/sora-xor/sora-branding)

Judging
-------

Our professional team of memetic engineers will judge entries based on creativity, design, humor, and uniqueness.

* Out of all the submissions, only 13 memes will be selected for prizes, based on the 4 criteria mentioned earlier (**_creativity, design, humor, and uniqueness…in case you forgot_**) to be the winners.
* All memes must use the “**Submission Requirements**” to be eligible for prizes.
* Memes must be SORA, Polkaswap, or Fearless Wallet-related and must comply with the rules above or will be disqualified.

**About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet**
----------------------------------------------

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/), a non-custodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).
