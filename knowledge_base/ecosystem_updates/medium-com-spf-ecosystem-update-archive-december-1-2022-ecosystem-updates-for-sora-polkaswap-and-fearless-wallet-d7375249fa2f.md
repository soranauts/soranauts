The SORA Integrated Plan [https://sora.org/plan](https://sora.org/plan)

We **have arrived to the final stretch of 2022! As December begins, the hard work and dedication to achieving the SORA Integrated Plan’s goals does not end, and in fact it grows stronger as more goals are unlocked. The month of December begins with great news and achievements. The Substrate 4 integration last week was a success, and now, our focus shifts towards building the bridges that will connect the SORA network to the SORA Kusama parachain, other EVM networks and beyond.**

SORA network’s Substrate 4 upgrade has been successfully deployed!

Work on the SORA Kusama parachain has some great progress, with integrated testing beginning this month. More information for community testing of this exciting milestone will be available soon. Along with the bridge, work on BEEFY proofs is also progressing smoothly. We continue to monitor the work being carried out by the team at Parity, as well as our ongoing support and solidarity toward their development team.

XSTUSD base pools are finally live!

While XSTUSD base pairs for liquidity provision are complete, there are still some details that are being worked on in collaboration with the Ceres team, in order to provide a better experience with liquidity provision on the SORA network. Along with this significant milestone, plenty of work is being put into concreting XST tokenomics. Please look forward to this exciting upgrade to the overall SORA tokenomics that will bring us a step closer to the goal of Many Worlds, One Economy.

Polkaswap v1.10 has landed!

The Substrate 4 integration brought with it many improvements, one of them being the implementation of charts in Polkaswap. After much duress, this functionality was released in the latest runtime upgrade, however, the data is still undergoing indexing, so there is currently information for October and November 2022, and it is consistently expanding, so please bear with us. Another improvement being worked on is the ability to view SORA network assets within Fearless Wallet (and other DOTSAMA wallets too), this progress also goes hand in hand with the Fearless Wallet browser extension and desktop app, which will be available for everyone to try out very soon!

Fearless Wallet is quickly becoming part of SORA with XOR integration already live on the iOS Beta version.

Finally, the news that everyone has been looking forward to, when will SORA card registration begin… Well, there have been a few delays, however, building innovative and safe Substrate-based fintech, which is also a self-custodial and decentralized solution regularly requires more work and there are more unexpected challenges than can arise at any point. SORA Card registration and launch will of only go live with the highest quality standards, as you have come to expect from any SORA product or service. Therefore, phase 1 ( pre-registration) and phase 2 (card, wallet and exchange operational) have been moved further ahead in the calendar than initially scheduled. Early December for Phase 1 and January for phase 2 are the new release timeframes. We sincerely apologise for any inconvenience this causes and we look forward to your ongoing support.

Look out for SORA + Polkaswap + Fearless Wallet #WinterQuest, complete daily challenges and win nifty prizes!

**_You can find the summary of the integrated plan points in the_** [**_SORA Wiki_**](https://wiki.sora.org/sora-integrated-plan)**_._**

### About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a noncustodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystem, with native support for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) platforms. A premium user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
