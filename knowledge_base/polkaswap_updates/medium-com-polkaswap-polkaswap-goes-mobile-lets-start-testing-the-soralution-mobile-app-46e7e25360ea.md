\*_iOS version still in development_

Polkaswap Mobile Overview
-------------------------

You can easily find the Polkaswap tab in the [SORAlution mobile app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.sora.communitytesting&hl=en&gl=US) tabs bar located at the bottom of the screen.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

After you tap on the Polkaswap icon, the main page for swapping features opens. There are 2 main panels here — Swap and Pool. The pool option is currently unavailable and will be introduced in upcoming releases.

The Main Controls on the Swap Tab Are:
--------------------------------------

1. **From** — the asset that should be swapped
2. **To** — the asset that you would like to receive (the result of the swap)
3. The **Slippage Tolerance** controller

Press enter or click to view image in full size

**Before using any of the Polkaswap features, make sure you read the disclaimer carefully.** You need to click on the **Disclaimer** button for it to appear.

Let’s Polkaswap!
----------------

Swapping is simplified with the mobile application. Tokens for testing are available from the [SORA testnet faucet](https://testfaucet.polkaswap.io/). SORAlution and the [Polkaswap testnet](https://test.polkaswap.io/) web application are based on the same test network

1. Select the **From** and **To** assets

Press enter or click to view image in full size

2\. Enter the **amount** that you would like to swap

Press enter or click to view image in full size

3\. Tap the **Confirm** button

Press enter or click to view image in full size

4\. And congratulations, the swap is completed!

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Advanced Swapping Functions
---------------------------

### Select the Market

The preferred market (**TBC** _\[Token Bonding Curve\]_ or **Smart**) can be selected in the **Market** section located on the top right corner. When you tap on it you will see the available markets and their description.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

After you select the Market algorithm it will be displayed in the top right corner of the Swap screen.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

### Advanced Input of the Asset Amount

You can specify the percentage of the amount that you’d like to swap, you can select 75%, 50%, or 25%. If you select to swap 100%, a small amount of XOR will be reserved for paying fees.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

### Changing The Swap Direction

The swap direction may be changed by clicking the button with arrows between assets.

1. Select a XOR → VAL swap, then tap the arrows.
2. The swap direction will be changed and now you will see a VAL → XOR swap.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational, world economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a non custodial liquidity aggregating, cross chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind **Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT)**.

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Kusama and Polkadot networks, with support for iOS and Android platforms. An awesome user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless wallet will integrate Polkaswap for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
