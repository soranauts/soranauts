This month’s Polkaswap updates highlight the platform’s continuous progress. Over the past few weeks, Polkaswap has introduced new features and made significant technological advancements, bringing it closer to delivering a fully decentralized and interoperable trading experience.

In this update, we’ll cover the key highlights, ongoing developments, and upcoming opportunities within the Polkaswap ecosystem.

### Key Highlights

**Polkaswap testnet with DeFi-R**

Press enter or click to view image in full size

DeFi-R enables regulated financial institutions to participate in decentralized finance while adhering to strict compliance standards. Using **Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)**, DeFi-R ensures secure and verified access to regulated assets, bridging the gap between decentralized innovation and regulatory requirements. DeFi-R is currently available to try out on the [Polkaswap Testnet](https://test.polkaswap.io/). Learn more about the functionalities in the recent [article](/sora-xor/introducing-defi-r-on-sora-a-new-era-of-regulated-token-economy-05c6d1260afb).

### Polkaswap version 1.40.0

Press enter or click to view image in full size

**🌉 HASHI bridge now supports Curio network**

This bridge is crucial for expanding Polkaswap’s cross-chain functionality, enhancing liquidity, and increasing interoperability. Once launched, Curio DAO’s **CGT** token will be listed on [Polkaswap.io](http://polkaswap.io/), and cross-chain transfers will be enabled. Additionally, **XOR**, **VAL**, **PSWAP**, and **KSM** have been whitelisted for XCM transfers to the Curio parachain, with these assets now available on Capital DEX.

**📱 Picture-in-Picture mode for widgets on the Swap page**

Use the \[expand\] button to pop out widgets over your screen neatly. With this mode, your widgets can now break free from the browser. They’ll stay visible even when you minimize your browser, floating above all your open windows for easy access.

Here’s how:

1️⃣ Navigate to the Swap page

2️⃣ Tap the ‘Open in top window’

3️⃣ Enjoy Picture-in-Picture with widgets floating with style and freedom 💱

**⚡ Aggregate Liquidity Technology support**

Disabled on-chain; stay tuned for the next release.

### Polkaswap version 1.39.0

Press enter or click to view image in full size

**🔥 Point System page**

This release marks the first phase of the **Point System**, which tracks and accumulates user actions within the SORA network. In the upcoming version, accounts will earn a score based on their on-chain activity. **SORATOPIA** points from off-chain tasks will also be included in the score. This system encourages active participation in the ecosystem by providing new ways to earn rewards.

[**Explore the Point System**](https://polkaswap.io/#/points)

[**Play SORATOPIA**](https://t.me/soratopia_bot/app)

**📲 Telegram Mini App improvements**

The **Polkaswap Telegram Mini App** has been upgraded to support the native Telegram API, making it more accessible and easier to use. Additionally, **haptic feedback** has been added, improving the app’s interactivity. These updates make trading on Polkaswap through Telegram more seamless and intuitive.

[**Try it now**](https://t.me/polkaswap_io_bot/app)

**🖼 Swap Page improvements**

The **Swap Page** has undergone a significant redesign to offer more customization options. Users can now drag and resize widgets, creating a more personalized interface. Key updates include:

* New widgets, such as price charts for selected tokens and a supply chart.
* The ability to rearrange and resize widgets in “Edit” mode.

These changes enhance the trading experience by allowing users to tailor the interface to their preferences.

**🔑 WalletConnect support for SORA accounts**

Polkaswap now supports **WalletConnect** as an alternative login method for SORA accounts. This feature improves user flexibility by offering a more convenient way to access the platform. WalletConnect is also integrated into the Polkaswap Telegram Mini App, making it easier than ever to connect and trade.

### Ongoing Developments

Polkaswap is actively working on several projects that will further expand its capabilities and improve user experience:

**Asset Owner Page — 95% Complete**

The new **Asset Owner Page** simplifies the token issuance process, allowing creators to issue tokens in just a few clicks. This feature lowers the barriers for token creation, fostering innovation and increasing liquidity. Future updates will expand the types of assets that can be created, including the introduction of **Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)**, which will serve as digital identity tokens representing unique traits or achievements.

**Point System Improvements — 10% Complete**

Following the release of the **Point System MVP**, development is now focused on enhancing the system by incorporating more on-chain data and refining the indexer logic. New criteria for tasks, achievements, and points will be introduced in upcoming versions, making the system more rewarding for active participants.

**HASHI Bridge with TON — 5% Complete**

Following the listing of the Polkaswap Telegram Mini App on [ton.app](https://ton.app/dex/polkaswap-dex?id=2380), work has begun on the **HASHI Bridge with the TON network**. This bridge will bring native TON tokens to Polkaswap, enhancing cross-chain interoperability. Stay tuned for more updates in future development cycles.

**Pool Page Redesign — 5% Complete**

Work has started on a redesign of the **Pool Page** to provide detailed statistics for account pools and simplify the liquidity-adding process. The goal is to make liquidity management more intuitive and efficient. Additional features will be rolled out in upcoming updates to improve the user experience further.

The latest updates on the Polkaswap reflect significant progress. Over the past month, Polkaswap has continued to enhance its seamless, secure, and efficient trading experience. Key feature enhancements, ecosystem growth, and technological advancements have pushed the platform closer to delivering a truly decentralized, interoperable trading environment.

This update will cover key highlights, ongoing developments, and the exciting opportunities ahead for Polkaswap and its ecosystem.

### About Polkaswap

Polkaswap is a next-generation, cross-chain liquidity aggregator and hybrid AMM and order book DEX on the Polkadot (and Kusama) network(s), parachains, and blockchains connected via bridges. Through the development of bridge technologies, Polkaswap enables Ethereum-based tokens to be traded. This is done seamlessly, at high speed and low fees, while exchanging assets in a non-custodial manner on the SORA network.

Polkaswap utilizes liquidity aggregation technology to trade against multiple sources, promising to solve critical problems AMM DEXs face, such as impermanent loss and pair fragmentation. As an open-source project, it allows the community to add more liquidity sources, enhancing its potential. Currently, Polkaswap offers cross-chain asset exchange with major Polkadot and EVM chains with TON coming soon.
