TL;DR
-----

* Participate in the SORA Testnet on your phone or your web browser and help find any bugs or issues.
* Try out new functions such as NFT or Referrals before they’re released to the mainnet
* You will be rewarded with an NFT for your participation in the testnet.

There is a lot of work that has been done recently on Polkaswap’s infrastructure. NFT functionality and a referral system to invite others to trade with style and freedom are the most recent developments, and we would like you to help test them out.

As the [Polkaswap testnet](https://test.polkaswap.io/) resets every week, beginning from the 28th of February, to the 6th of March 2022 you can help us test NFT and Referral program functionality. If you follow these rules, you can receive a special NFT as a non-fungible token of our appreciation!

Polkaswap Testnet Program Rules
-------------------------------

Let’s get down to business, here are the rules to participate in the Polkaswap Incentivised Testnet;

1- Visit the [SORA Wiki](https://wiki.sora.org/master) and follow the [NFT tutorial](https://wiki.sora.org/guides/nft-on-polkaswap-testnet) and the [Referral System tutorial](https://wiki.sora.org/sora-academy/sora-referral-system), and watch this video:

Then complete these tasks on the [Polkaswap Testnet](https://test.polkaswap.io/):

* Use the SORA Testnet [faucet](https://testfaucet.polkaswap.io/) to get test XOR tokens
* Swap between XOR and other tokens, get some XSTUSD.
* Pool your tokens.
* Use the referral function to invite someone to participate in the testnet.
* Mint an NFT and exchange it with another user on the testnet.

2- You must be in the [SORA](https://t.me/sora_xor) and [Polkaswap](https://t.me/polkaswap) Telegram groups.

3- Tweet from your account: I have just completed the @polkaswap testnet! #𒊹𒂵𒆜

NFT Rewards for the Incentivised Testnet Users
----------------------------------------------

Once you’re done with these three steps, you can sit back, relax, and wait for your special NFT badge of honor while you take some time to appreciate the help you have just provided to improve Polkaswap for yourself, and the community. Unless you cheated, then you will be immediately disqualified. _There are bots constantly monitoring the testnet, so don’t cheat, you will get caught._

With the rules addressed, you’re very welcome to participate in the Polkaswap testnet, which **began on the 28th of February at 00:00 UTC and will run until the 6th of March at 00:00 UTC**!

If you have any further questions or doubts, feel free to ask in the [Polkaswap Telegram](https://t.me/polkaswap) group, or consult the [SORA Wiki](https://wiki.sora.org/polkaswap/what-is-polkaswap).

About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a noncustodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystem, with support for iOS and Android platforms. An awesome user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate Polkaswap for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
