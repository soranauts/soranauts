Welcome to Polkaswap Ecosystem Updates #84!

Press enter or click to view image in full size

**Hello Polkaswap community!** We’re excited to share a fresh development update. Our latest changes bring **multi-signature account support** and **token denomination enhancements** to [Polkaswap.io](http://Polkaswap.io). These updates aim to boost security for collaborative users and polish the overall user experience on the platform.

Keep reading to explore how these updates are shaping the future of decentralized finance!

### Ongoing Developments

Polkaswap is actively working on several projects that will further expand its capabilities and improve user experience:

### 🔐 Multi-Signature Support (MST) Comes to Polkaswap

One of the most significant updates is the introduction of **multi-signature account** support (abbreviated _MST_) right in Polkaswap’s web UI. **Multi-signature** means an account can require multiple approvals (signatures) before a transaction is executed, which is great for teams, DAOs, or any group managing shared funds. This feature was long anticipated and is now integrated into the Polkaswap development branch:

* **Enhanced Security for Shared Accounts:** Multi-sig functionality adds an extra layer of security for joint accounts. Instead of one person controlling assets, multiple designated signers must approve a transaction. This dramatically **reduces the** risk of single-point failure and insider threats — essential for any business or organisation using SORA’s network. In short, community treasuries and project funds can be managed more safely.
* **Convenient Polkaswap UI Integration:** The development team finished implementing multi-signature accounts on the network side and worked with Polkaswap developers to build a **user-friendly interface** for it. Users will be able to initiate trades, liquidity provisions, or transfers from a multi-sig wallet on Polkaswap, and the app will handle the signing flow (e.g., prompting multiple approvals) seamlessly. This means DAOs and collaborative groups can soon use Polkaswap just as easily as individual users, but with added checks and balances.
* **Use Cases — DAOs and Team Funds:** With MST support, **DAOs, project teams, or even friends sharing an account** can trade on Polkaswap without compromising security. For example, a DAO treasury can now provide liquidity or swap tokens on Polkaswap, but any action can require, say, 3 out of 5 members to approve. This update empowers more community-driven and enterprise use of Polkaswap, aligning with SORA’s goal of being enterprise-ready and safe for serious use.

_Multi-signature support is a significant step toward making Polkaswap a secure platform for managing group funds. Try it now on_ [_Polkaswap.io_](http://Polkaswap.io) _or read the_ [_Multi-signature tutorial on the SORA Wiki._](https://wiki.sora.org/multisig-account.html)

### 📊 Improved Token Denomination & Bridging Experience

Another focus of this update is on **token denominations and bridging**, particularly for SORA’s native tokens **XOR** and **TBCD**:

* **Accurate Handling of XOR & TBCD in Bridges:** Polkaswap’s bridge interface received improvements to properly support the unique **denomination and conversion rules for XOR and TBCD**. In practice, this means when you bridge XOR (the SORA native utility token) or **TBCD** (the Token Bonding Curve Dollar) the amounts and units are now handled correctly across networks. This prevents any confusion or errors when moving these assets between SORA and external chains. For users, bridging XOR or TBCD will feel more straightforward — the app takes care of the unit conversions, so what you send is what arrives on the other side.
* **Hiding Fiat Values for Certain Assets:** To further improve clarity, the UI will **no longer display fiat conversion values** for XOR and TBCD when those assets are in an _external/bridged form_. Why? Because XOR’s price on external networks might not be directly tracked in the Polkaswap interface. Removing the fiat readout in those cases avoids potentially misleading information. Users will still see prices for most assets, but **for XOR and TBCD in cross-network contexts, the focus stays on their crypto value** (which is the relevant metric during a bridge transfer).
* **Smoother Denomination Flow in UI:** The update also **refines how the app displays and converts token units**. Polkaswap handles both small “base units” (for precise blockchain amounts) and user-friendly formats (such as displaying 1.5 XOR instead of a large integer of the most minor units). The denomination flow improvements ensure that switching between these (for example, entering an amount vs. viewing balances) is consistent and error-free. This is a behind-the-scenes tweak, but users might notice more **consistent rounding, formatting, and conversion of token amounts** during swaps or transfers.

Overall, these changes make interacting with SORA’s core assets more intuitive. **Whether you’re swapping XOR, using TBCD in DeFi, or bridging assets, the UI now presents information in a cleaner, stylish, and more accurate way.**

### 🔧 Additional Enhancements and Fixes

Alongside the headline features above, the development effort included several other improvements to Polkaswap’s web app. Here are some noteworthy enhancements and fixes that come with this update:

* **Optimized Token Icons and Images:** V**arious images in the wallet interface have been compressed and updated**, including token icons and logos. This optimisation **res**ults in faster load times and a more responsive experience when you open Polkaswap or view your wallet balances, especially on slower connections or devices.
* **UI Bug Fixes:** A few user interface bugs have been squashed. Notably, a previous issue with **bridge warnings** has been fixed — the bridge page will now correctly show alerts or messages only when relevant (no more erroneous warnings). Additionally, minor quirks (like a sign button that wasn’t disabled at the right time) have been addressed to make the UI more polished and user-friendly.
* **Language Updates:** The application’s **localizations have been updated**, meaning improved or new translations for supported languages. Non-English speaking community members should see more accurate and up-to-date text in the Polkaswap UI, reflecting the new features (like multi-sig) in their native language.
* **Upgraded Dependencies:** Under the hood, Polkaswap is now running on the **latest SORA API and wallet libraries** (v1.43.0 and above), which include the MST features. By updating these dependencies, the app benefits from all recent backend improvements and **prepares for upcoming features** (_for example, the SORA dev team is testing “XOR-less transactions” on chain — paying fees in any token — and the updated Polkaswap will be ready to support such innovations when they roll out_). These upgrades also generally improve the stability and performance of the DEX.
* **Infrastructure & DevOps:** Additionally, there were some behind-the-scenes updates, while not directly visible to users, these changes help the dev team test and deploy Polkaswap more effectively, ultimately resulting in a smoother end-user experience and quicker turnaround for future fixes.

### 🚀 What’s Next?

With **multi-signature support** and **denomination improvements** merged into Polkaswap’s development branch, **here’s what to look forward to:**

* **Even More User Experience Refinements:** The team will monitor feedback on the new denomination handling. If any edge cases or confusions remain (for example, with price displays or bridging flows), expect iterative improvements. User feedback is crucial, so the community is encouraged to report any odd behavior or suggestions as they try the new features.
* **Continuous Improvements:** Polkaswap development doesn’t stop here. Future updates will build on this foundation — from enabling _XOR-less fee transactions_ (so you can pay fees in any token) to integrating new networks into the bridge. By staying up-to-date with the latest SORA tech (as seen in this update), Polkaswap is ready to roll out new capabilities faster when the core network introduces them.

**In summary**, this development update delivers a robust mix of security and usability enhancements. Multi-signature transaction support gives our community and developers a powerful tool for secure collaborative trading , and the tweaks to token handling smooth out the DeFi experience on Polkaswap. We believe these changes will benefit both everyday users and power-users of the SORA ecosystem.

_As always, thank you for being part of SORA and Polkaswap!_ We invite everyone to try out these new features when they go live and provide feedback. Together, we’re making decentralized trading on Polkaswap more secure, accessible, and user-friendly. **Stay tuned for the official release announcement**, and happy swapping! 🎉

About Polkaswap
---------------

Polkaswap is a next-generation, cross-chain liquidity aggregator and hybrid AMM and order book DEX on the Polkadot (and Kusama) network(s), parachains, and blockchains connected via bridges. Through the development of bridge technologies, Polkaswap enables Ethereum-based tokens to be traded. This is done seamlessly, at high speed and low fees, while exchanging assets in a non-custodial manner on the SORA network.

Polkaswap utilizes liquidity aggregation technology to trade against multiple sources, promising to solve critical problems AMM DEXs face, such as impermanent loss and pair fragmentation. As an open-source project, it allows the community to add more liquidity sources, enhancing its potential. Currently, Polkaswap offers cross-chain asset exchange with major Polkadot and EVM chains with TON coming soon.
