**Phase 1:**
------------

**🏁 Participate via Gleam:** [**here**](https://gleam.io/zHQmD/polkaswap-34000-xst-airdrop-campaign)**.**

* Total prize pool: $34,000.
* Duration: 4 weeks.
* Max reward per participant: $140.

_💡More details on Phase 2 and Phase 3 will be added later._

**Here are the Rules and Guidelines to Participate in the SORA Synthetics Airdrop Campaign**
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

### Campaign Duration

The SORA Synthetics Airdrop campaign will run for four weeks, from the 31st of August to the 28th of September.

### You Must Fulfill These Airdrop Requirements

1. The claiming process will involve **signing up with and providing a SORA network address**.

2\. Your ETH account **must** exist for at least three months with 5+ transactions or more (**no bots**). You can paste your ETH address into [etherscan.io](http://etherscan.io) and search for your first transaction (_this would help identify your account's “age”_).

3\. Your social media accounts must be **verified via** [**Gleam**](https://gleam.io/zHQmD/polkaswap-34000-xst-airdrop-campaign)**.**

4\. You **must** share the airdrop post on social media (Copy to get the link and paste it on your tweet and share) and follow these SORA ecosystem accounts: [https://t.me/sora\_xor](https://t.me/sora_xor), [https://twitter.com/sora\_xor](https://twitter.com/sora_xor), [https://discord.com/invite/4TXRN6Y4gb](https://discord.com/invite/4TXRN6Y4gb), and [https://medium.com/sora-xor](https://medium.com/sora-xor).

5\. If you participate in bridging and liquidity provision steps, you **must** share your transaction hash as proof to claim your bonus.

**🚨IMPORTANT RULE🚨 
Your actions will ONLY count if they’re performed AFTER the kick-off of the airdrop campaign. So, eyes on the clock and make sure you’re on the mark! Even if the Gleam platform shows your action as completed, it doesn’t guarantee it’s done right or that the reward will be paid out. Ensure you follow the guidelines to the T!**

_Additionally, you must provide a link as evidence that you shared the airdrop post._

4\. Then, choose any EVM token you want (ETH and DAI are recommended due to the highest liquidity in the platform, etc.)

> _💡 If you don’t have enough funds in your Metamask to participate in the campaign, you can buy XOR via Moonpay. Here is a_ [_tutorial_](https://wiki.sora.org/on-ramp-polkaswap.html)_._

Press enter or click to view image in full size

7\. To find your bridge transaction ID, navigate to the “Bridge” page and click on the Bridge History button.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

8\. Then tap on your last transaction.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

9\. Copy the TX ID (required as proof for this task).

Press enter or click to view image in full size

------------------------

1. Go to [https://polkaswap.io/#/swap](https://polkaswap.io/#/swap)

Press enter or click to view image in full size

2\. Choose the token you have previously bridged from Ethereum (in our example, we use DAI.)

Press enter or click to view image in full size

3\. Choose XST or other synthetic token as the destination token (TO).

Press enter or click to view image in full size

4\. Enter the number of tokens you want to swap and click ‘Swap.’

> **_💡 Liquidity provision_** _in the XOR-XST pool: earn $30 (participants need to provide transaction ID) — tx minimum is $100 and must be locked for two weeks ($1,000+ LP — Discord guild role)_ **_$18,000 top 600 users._**

How to Provide Liquidity in Polkaswap
-------------------------------------

**To fulfill this task, you must lock a minimum of $100 ($50 XOR + $50 XST) for two weeks. The reward will be $30 for completing this simple action.**

_💡 If, for any reason, $100 is not enough, you need to withdraw the money from the pool and deposit it again with a slightly more significant amount._

1. Go to [https://polkaswap.io/#/pool/](https://polkaswap.io/#/pool/), or click ‘Pool.’

Press enter or click to view image in full size

2\. Click ‘Add Liquidity’.

3\. Enter the token pairs we showed in the example.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

4\. Deposit XST and XOR (most liquidity pools on SORA are paired with XOR) and lock at least $100 for two weeks.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

5\. Click on ‘Add liquidity,’ then sign the transaction.

6\. Send the transaction hash to approve your submission (you can find your TX ID at “Account” → “Activity”; search for the transaction that mentions added liquidity).

Press enter or click to view image in full size

When you provide liquidity, you are guaranteed to receive airdrop rewards.

**🔥Additionally, you will benefit from:**

1. Fees collected from [liquidity provision (LP)](http://polkaswap.io/#/explore/pools), paid in PSWAP tokens.
2. Strategic rewards in PSWAP.
3. The opportunity to participate in [Demeter Farming](http://polkaswap.io/#/explore/demeter/farming) using your XOR-XST LP, which can earn you extra rewards (up to $5).

**Refer a Friend: The Top 10 Referral Earns $100**
--------------------------------------------------

\*Earn 10 points for each successful referral (Participants must provide the referred friend’s transaction ID).

* The referral must make a bridge + make at least two transactions during the campaign.
* A successful referral is valid when your friend joins the Polkaswap Airdrop using your unique referral link and confirms you as their referrer.
* Each participant can use only one unique referral link or code, which cannot be modified.
* A single wallet address can only refer to one other wallet address.
* Participants are eligible for referral rewards only if no other participant has referred the invited user.

> 💡To qualify for the referrer bonus, you must fulfil the exact requirements as other airdrop participants (**a**. ETH account must exist for at least three months with 5+ transactions or more. **b.** Complete a bridge transaction).

**Here’s what you need to do:**

1. Go to [https://polkaswap.io/#/referral](https://polkaswap.io/#/referral)

2\. Click on XOR Bonded, and then on ‘Bond more.’

Press enter or click to view image in full size

3\. You can enter any amount, but remember that it should be more than 0.7 XOR to pay a fee for the referral.

4\. Now copy the link and send it to your friends. You’ll see them in the referrals list when they join the network.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

5\. Share your Polkaswap referral link to get 10% of your referral’s network fees.

### About Polkaswap

[Polkaswap](http://polkaswap.io) is a DeFi application on the SORA network, and it is a non-custodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem. It offers boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

Polkaswap utilizes [SORA](http://sora.org) network interoperability with the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) networks, as well as the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.
