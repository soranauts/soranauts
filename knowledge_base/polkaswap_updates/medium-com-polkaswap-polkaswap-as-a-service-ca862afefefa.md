### TL;DR:

* Polkaswap is a decentralized AMM DEX built on the SORA network that allows communities to trade in a decentralized, fast, cheap, and trustworthy manner.
* With Polkaswap-as-a-service, builders can scale their token utility and benefit from single-sided liquidity provision, additional revenue streams, and reaching new users.
* Polkaswap-as-a-service is designed for the future of interoperability. Being listed on Polkaswap allows access to Ethereum, Polkadot, and Kusama networks.

As a project in Web3, finding a decentralized marketplace for your community can be challenging. It is necessary to provide liquidity for both tokens of the pool, which significantly impacts your treasury. Furthermore, liquidity provider rewards do not always cover your impermanent loss. On top of that, your treasury is often asked to provide incentives to attract other liquidity providers on a DEX.

With high listing requirements and hefty market maker fees on a centralized exchange, the idea of bearing the monthly costs becomes less exciting to commit to. Oftentimes, the team’s capital allocated to market making on CEXes is not used in an optimal way and does not generate revenue for builders. All this leaves users unable to access your project’s token, as promising or established as it may be.

With recent events in the space that have shaken the trust of CEXes, community members and traders are seeking decentralized self-custodial solutions. Factors like privacy and ownership are leading users to focus more on DEXes to eliminate or mitigate scenarios like regular CEX hacks, lack of access to their tokens, and single-sided centralized decision-making, which limits their freedom, ownership and privacy.

Needless to say, this can limit a project’s growth and adoption, hindering it from achieving its full potential.

The [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) AMM DEX (with order book feature coming soon) built on the SORA Substrate-based network enables builders to offer their community a better trading experience with a significantly lower burden on the team´s treasury than other exchange listings.

Polkaswap-as-a-service offers free listing and matches your liquidity, allowing your community decentralized trading in a fast, cheap, and trustworthy manner at no additional cost.

Project teams can benefit from liquidity provision and community growth, while network users can enjoy increased token availability and efficient trading.

Builders can scale their token utility with single-sided liquidity provision (no ETH/stable needed), potential additional revenue streams, and reach new users in the process. Moreover, Polkaswap- as-a-service offers revenue opportunities for builders’ treasuries.

Additionally, a multi-EVM and Substrate bridge, in development, will enable seamless cross-chain interoperability. With the SORA Card offering, users will have a neobank-style experience within the Polkaswap DEX and other apps, adding more utility to a project’s token.

_Without further ado, let’s dive into its inner workings._

How Does Polkaswap-as-a-service Work?
-------------------------------------

Polkaswap and Project XYZ have agreed to collaborate and jointly contribute $400K, with funds locked for 12 months in the [CERES liquidity locker](https://dapps.cerestoken.io/liquidity_locker).

### Revenue Model

The revenue model for projects participating in the Polkaswap-as-a-service program consists of liquidity provision fees and strategic bonus rewards.

Additionally, teams can incentivize liquidity provision and farming of their pool with their own token through the [Demeter Farming Platform](https://farming.deotoken.io/farms) (more about [Demeter Farming-as-a-service](/sora-xor/ceres-releases-demeter-farming-platform-on-thesora-network-7028aced3bba)).

On a wider scale, however, the greatest reward for participating will be that users will have wider access to your token, in case it was only available within a CEX or awarded to users for a parachain auction (crowdloan), or ICO.

For additional incentive, you can earn PSWAP for your treasury as part of the rewards available on Polkaswap for providing liquidity. The total provided liquidity will be distributed between the project and the SORA community, with half going to the project, while the rewards earned by the SORA community will be used to buy back and burn XOR or put to other productive use.

### Bottom Line

Polkaswap-as-a-service will allow your community to benefit from a platform designed for the future of interoperability as well as fast and affordable transactions in a beautiful and easy to use interface.

Being listed on Polkaswap will allow access to the Ethereum, Polkadot, and Kusama networks! Bridges to other main EVM networks like BNB Chain, Klaytn and Avalanche, as well as Bitcoin are being worked on.

The current offer is only available to teams that have ERC20 tokens, and it will soon be extended to all Substrate builders once SORA’s Substrate <> Substrate bridge is up and running.

If you’re interested in learning more about Polkaswap or want to get your token listed on our platform, we’d love to hear from you! Simply shoot us an email at [team@polkabiome.com](mailto:team@polkabiome.com).

### About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet

[SORA](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new monetary system for humanity with democratic oversight by all participants aimed at creating a supranational multiverse economic order with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org/) network implements an advanced way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/), a noncustodial liquidity aggregating, cross-chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT).

[Fearless Wallet](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet and browser extension designed for the decentralized future of the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystem, with native support for [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089) and [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) platforms, and Chromium-based browsers. A premium user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet has integrated [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
