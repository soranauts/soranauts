SORA Dev Update #16
-------------------

🚀 Android 2.1 SORA mainnet [**beta application**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.sora) was released. Look for “Join the beta” section on the [app page](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.sora) in Google Play and tap on “Join” in order to get the beta build (after joining, it takes a few minutes for the beta to become available). Improvements include:

* Better wallet usability
* Displaying of addresses, blocks, and transaction IDs

🎈 Preparing the next SORA network upgrade

* Slow block production problems fixed
* Fees for rewards claiming updated
* Fast native WASM runtime

💧 Working on the Polkaswap mobile app module

🙏 Collaborations:

* Working with the [**SubQuery**](https://subquery.network/) team. Statistics calculation will be easier!
* Working with the [**SubScan**](https://www.subscan.io/) team. One more block explorer for the SORA substrate network!

📕 The 1st Annual SORA Economic Forum took place on June 22nd! Amazing talks by Richard Werner, Terrill Bouricius, Tim Swanson, and more! The videos will be uploaded to the [SORA youtube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCb2i3VaGrggBhfo2ytg8xCA)

Fearless Wallet Dev Update #22
------------------------------

🎉 **The updated version of** [**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io) is now available on the [**AppStore**](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089) **and** [**Google Play**](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) with **Staking and** **Crowdloan** improvements!

🙌 **Crowdloan** improvements:

* View your contributions on the Crowdloan tab
* Added “Completed” category for finished crowdloans
* Added sorting for the crowdloans list

🥞 **Staking** improvements:

* Added support for Polkadot and Kusama new runtimes with **updated staking limits** — new limits are 40 DOT min and 22,500 max nominators for Polkadot, and 0.1 KSM / 20,000 nominators for Kusama
* Added an **alert** for setting up **nomination** in case your staking account has been sent to (netflix and) chill — e.g., if you were staking less than 40 DOT / 0.1 KSM

🎁 **The demo for Sprint 0706** was conducted on the 25th of June. Recent dev updates and roadmap refinements were presented. Recording and slides are available here: [Link](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1tBVfx3rLMO-NF7PURry_lBLOImK4EUrz?usp=sharing)

🤝 **Collaborations**:

🌟 **Beacon** and Fearless Wallet teams have started to test and refine the solution for mobile signers in the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems in the context of a Kusama bounty

🌟 **Ryabina** and Fearless Wallet teams are continuing to develop a decentralized solution for push notifications in the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems

🌟 **SubQuery** and Fearless Wallet teams are continuing to refine common APIs for improving the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystem state for new apps and developers

⛳ **The roadmap is being refined**, next action is to compose a proposal to the Kusama Treasury to add **support of custom networks** and **multi-assets** in Fearless Wallet. The team is working on benchmarking different solutions to investigate technical constraints.

About SORA, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------

[**SORA**](/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system aimed at creating a supranational, world economic system with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org) network implements a new way of parachain architecture on [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) network, with the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/), a non custodial liquidity aggregating, cross chain AMM DEX designed uniquely for the Polkadot ecosystem with boundless liquidity through its one-of-a-kind **Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT)**.

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Kusama and Polkadot ecosystem, with support for iOS and Android platforms. An awesome user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate Polkaswap for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
