Welcome to Polkaswap Ecosystem Updates #82! In this latest edition, we dive into the most recent advancements driving innovation within the Polkaswap ecosystem. This month’s highlights include the rollout of **Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT)** for optimized trading and various user experience improvements, including streamlined wallet connectivity and enhanced privacy features in the Telegram Mini App.

As Polkaswap continues to evolve, new features like **Vested Transfers**, **Multi-Signature Transactions**, and the **HASHI Bridge with TON** are developing, making Polkaswap an even more robust and decentralized trading platform. Keep reading to explore how these updates are shaping the future of decentralized finance!

### Key Highlights for October and November;

### Release 1.44.0:

**🔥 Wallet Activity Improvements**

* **Fiat Price at Time of Transaction**

Now, you can view the fiat price at the time of your transaction — not the current rate. This brings added clarity and precision to your activity history.

* **Token Icons**

Token icons were added to transaction details, making it easier to identify used assets at one glance.

* **Improved search behavior**

When you search for a transaction that doesn’t exist, the list will now be empty rather than showing your entire history of transactions.

### Ongoing Developments

Polkaswap is actively working on several projects that will further expand its capabilities and improve user experience:

### Vested Transfers Support — 100% Complete

Vested Transfers allow assets to be locked and released gradually over time according to a predefined schedule. This feature is handy for distributing tokens in a controlled manner, such as vesting plans for team members or participants in decentralized governance. With Vested Transfers, token holders can ensure that assets are not immediately available but become accessible incrementally, promoting long-term commitment and reducing the risks.

### Asset Owner Page — 97% Complete

This page will streamline the token issuance process, enabling users to issue tokens with just a few clicks. Future updates will allow the creation of **Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)**, representing digital identities and unique traits.

### Point System Improvements — 50% Complete

Enhancements to the Point System are underway, incorporating more on-chain data. New criteria for tasks, achievements, and point rewards will increase the system’s overall impact.

### Multi-Signature Transaction Support — 40% Complete

Multi-signature transactions add an extra layer of security by requiring multiple authorized parties to sign off on a transaction before it can be executed. This feature is essential for collaborative environments like DAOs or organizations managing shared funds, where consensus among several members is needed for important decisions. Multi-signature support on Polkaswap enables safer asset management by reducing the risk of a single point of failure or unauthorized access.

### HASHI Bridge with TON — 10% Complete

Following the listing of Polkaswap’s Telegram Mini App on **ton.app**, we’ve begun development on the **HASHI Bridge** with the TON network. This will enable native TON tokens to be used on Polkaswap, enhancing cross-chain interoperability.

### Pool Page Redesign — 7% Complete

Work has started redesigning the Pool Page to improve statistics visibility and simplify liquidity management. More features will follow in upcoming updates.

The latest Polkaswap updates showcase steady growth and innovation. In the past month, Polkaswap has introduced powerful features like Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT), improving both user experience and trading efficiency. With continued advancements such as Vested Transfers, Multi-Signature transactions, and expanding cross-chain support via the HASHI Bridge with TON, Polkaswap is further establishing itself as a secure, user-friendly, decentralized trading ecosystem.

### About Polkaswap

Polkaswap is a next-generation, cross-chain liquidity aggregator and hybrid AMM and order book DEX on the Polkadot (and Kusama) network(s), parachains, and blockchains connected via bridges. Through the development of bridge technologies, Polkaswap enables Ethereum-based tokens to be traded. This is done seamlessly, at high speed and low fees, while exchanging assets in a non-custodial manner on the SORA network.

Polkaswap utilizes liquidity aggregation technology to trade against multiple sources, promising to solve critical problems AMM DEXs face, such as impermanent loss and pair fragmentation. As an open-source project, it allows the community to add more liquidity sources, enhancing its potential. Currently, Polkaswap offers cross-chain asset exchange with major Polkadot and EVM chains, with TON coming soon.
