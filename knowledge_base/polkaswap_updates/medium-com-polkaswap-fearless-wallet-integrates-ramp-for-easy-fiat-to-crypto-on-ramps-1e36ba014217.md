SORAMITSU is pleased to introduce [Fearless Wallet](https://fearlesswallet.io)’s integration with [Ramp](https://ramp.network), to simplify the self-custodial purchase of crypto using fiat currencies. This integration makes Fearless Wallet the first Polkadot and Kusama wallet specializing in DeFi to integrate fiat purchase of crypto.
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Earlier in 2020, we released Fearless Wallet, the first wallet designed for use on the Kusama and Polkadot networks. Even though Fearless Wallet is very slick and easy to use, not everyone has cryptocurrencies. For [n00bs](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/n00bs), the process of buying cryptocurrency is littered with barriers: multiple platforms must often be used in order to exchange from fiat currencies (e.g., EUR, USD) to specific tokens (e.g., XOR). Because Fearless Wallet focuses on creating outstanding user experiences in Kusama and Polkadot DeFi, it was a goal from early on to realize a simple way to enable in-app purchases of crypto, that is also fully compliant with regulations.

In cooperation with [Ramp](https://ramp.network), we upgraded Fearless Wallet to ensure that anyone (in supported countries) can buy cryptocurrencies seamlessly. Fearless Wallet’s interface greatly reduces the complexity of key operations, making it possible to buy crypto using fiat in a few clicks. This new feature pairs with Fearless Wallet’s built-in Kusama and Polkadot functionality, promising to increase access to these groundbreaking networks and the vibrant ecosystem of communities and tokens they will host.

Why Integrate With Ramp?
------------------------

Integrating [Ramp](https://ramp.network/) with Fearless Wallet has enabled buyers to exchange fiat money for tokens directly inside the wallet, without having to send the tokens through an external exchange. It combines the security of a decentralized, self-custodial exchange and the convenience of a centralized exchange, to the benefit of users and the crypto community alike. One of Ramp’s biggest advantages is the ability to transact instantly, which makes it both faster and cheaper than traditional payment methods (e.g., cards or manual transfers). An advantage of real-time payment in the fast-moving world of crypto is that the user is guaranteed the price listed at the time the transaction is made.

The Fearless Wallet app is available for both [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) and [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089) devices, and supports the following payment methods:

* **Visa or MasterCard —** Ramp enables users to carry out payments by credit card in a fast and easy way, ensuring accessibility to a broad user base worldwide. Its know-your-customer system is tiered, which means that for [purchases above certain limits](https://support.ramp.network/en/article/what-are-the-kyc-limits-1vbm6z/), secure verification by bank API, ID and selfie, and/or other means is required.
* **SEPA —** Ramp supports SEPA money transfers, allowing very inexpensive payments for users who have the time.
* **Apple Pay —** For iOS users, payments can be made and accepted with Apple pay.

The fees, limitations and country payment acceptances can be found [here](https://support.ramp.network/en/).

Improvements in Fearless Wallet 1.1 and Future Releases
-------------------------------------------------------

Fearless Wallet 1.1, which will be the next release, includes extensions and improvements. The first significant improvement is the UI, which keeps the operation of the wallet intuitive and avoids the need for complex instructions. The Actions menu, which contains the Send, Receive, and Buy buttons, has been improved for better clarity and convenience. The Send feature was also upgraded with additional checks. These are only a few of the features that distinguish Fearless Wallet from its competitors. Many more features will be introduced in 2021 — but we would like to give you a preview here.

> _SORAMITSU, Fearless Wallet’s designer, will implement the_ **_Polkaswap DEX._** _Users will be able to make instant trades without leaving the Fearless Wallet. Fiat on-ramps via Ramp integration will allow users to buy crypto and then trade it on Polkaswap._

The DEX interface has been designed with ease of operation as a primary goal. Users will be able to **buy, sell, and trade cryptocurrencies** within the wallet itself. In the process of the upgrade, the token buying feature itself will also be improved by displaying pending purchase transactions in the transfer history on the wallet screen.

Another groundbreaking Fearless Wallet implementation is the **staking function** for Kusama and Polkadot. Users will be able to nominate validators and stake tokens to provide liquidity, thereby generating passive income within the wallet.

And that’s not all. Fearless Wallet will also incorporate Kusama and Polkadot **governance functions**, transforming it into an interface for deliberation and participation.

The takeaway: Fearless Wallet will be the first true **all-in-one** DeFi application for Web 3.0!

About SORA NEO Network, Polkaswap, and Fearless Wallet
------------------------------------------------------

[SORA NEO (New Economic Order)](https://medium.com/@sora.xor/sora-the-new-economic-order-3ec3f0327e5a) is a new economic system based upon a supranational, decentralized central bank (DCB) with built-in tools for decentralized finance (DeFi). The [SORA](https://sora.org) network implements a new kind of parachain architecture on the [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) networks, providing the capability to [bridge](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/en/learn-bridges) external blockchains (like Ethereum) to the Polkadot ecosystem.

One of the DeFi applications that will run on the SORA network is [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/), a non-custodial liquidity aggregator cross-chain AMM DEX designed specifically for the Polkadot ecosystem. Polkaswap combines the security and convenience of a DEX with a one-of-a-kind **Aggregate Liquidity Technology (ALT)** capable of providing boundless liquidity.

[Fearless Wallet](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Kusama and Polkadot networks, with support for the iOS and Android platforms. It offers an awesome user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. The Fearless wallet will integrate with Polkaswap for easy, decentralized asset trading.
