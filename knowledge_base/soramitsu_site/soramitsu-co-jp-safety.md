**Soramitsu Child Safety Standards Policy**

* * *

**Introduction** 
Soramitsu is deeply committed to providing a safe, secure, and respectful environment for all users of [our applications available on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=Soramitsu&amp;hl=en_US&pli=1). This Child Safety Standards Policy applies to all our blockchain-based wallet applications listed below: 
 

* [Fearless Wallet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless)
* [SORA Wallet: Polkaswap](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.sora)
* [Byacco Wallet](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.byacco)
* [Soralution Wallet (Testnet)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.sora.communitytesting)
* [Soramitsu CBDC Wallet (Demo)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.cbdc)

 
**Our Commitment** 
Soramitsu maintains a strict zero-tolerance stance against any form of child sexual abuse or exploitation (CSAE). Our applications do not support or facilitate social interactions, dating, or content sharing functionalities, significantly reducing the risk of misuse related to CSAE. Nevertheless, we actively uphold and enforce stringent safety standards to comply fully with [Google's Child Safety requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14747720). 
 
**Reporting Mechanisms** 
If you encounter any inappropriate or suspicious activity related to child safety within any of our applications, please immediately report it to us by emailing: 
[legal@soramitsu.co.jp](mailto:legal@soramitsu.co.jp) or fill in the online form at [https://soramitsu.co.jp/safety#report](https://soramitsu.co.jp/safety#report) 
We guarantee a prompt and confidential response to all reports. 
 
**Compliance with Laws** 
We strictly adhere to all applicable laws and regulations regarding child safety, including those addressing child sexual abuse and exploitation. Soramitsu cooperates fully with law enforcement authorities if illegal activity is identified or suspected. 
 
**Policy Updates** 
We regularly review and update this Child Safety Standards Policy to reflect our ongoing commitment to safety and compliance with changing regulations and best practices. Users are encouraged to periodically review this page for the latest information.

For questions, concerns, or further information regarding our Child Safety Standards Policy, please contact:
