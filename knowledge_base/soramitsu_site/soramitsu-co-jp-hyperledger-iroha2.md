# Soramitsu Expands Use of Hyperledger Iroha 2, an Innovative Open-Source Blockchain Framework

## → Soramitsu is proud to announce its contribution to Hyperledger Iroha 2, a next-generation open-source blockchain framework that delivers robust, fast, and efficient digital asset and identity management solutions.

![](./images/9cc625c8b454f6e07d58c1ca1a2c6fcc1b3a657b16950b11c09843cfbce711d3.jpg)

Now a project of the [LF Decentralized Trust](https://www.lfdecentralizedtrust.org/), Iroha 2 offers an advanced suite of features that streamline blockchain adoption for developers and enterprises. As maintainers of Iroha, [Soramitsu](https://soramitsu.co.jp/) plays a leading role in developing the open-source platform.

Advancements in Hyperledger Iroha 2

Building on the successes and lessons learned from its predecessor, Iroha 2 represents a significant leap forward with a complete architectural overhaul and enhanced functionality.

**Key innovations in Iroha 2 include:**

**Granular Access Control & Security** 

* A role-based permission model with fine-tuned access controls, multi-signature transactions, and the new Byzantine fault-tolerant Sumeragi consensus algorithm ensures security and reliability.

**Developer-Friendly Environment** 

* Support for Rust, Python, Java/Kotlin, and JavaScript SDKs, alongside a command-line interface, simplifies blockchain interaction and smart contract development.

**Enhanced Smart Contract Capabilities** 

* WASM-based smart contracts and Iroha Special Instructions streamline contract execution and operations.

**Enterprise-Ready & Customizable** 

* A permissioned blockchain framework designed for trusted environments supporting customizable fungible and non-fungible assets.

**Scalability & Flexibility** 

* Extensible infrastructure enables seamless integration with existing systems, making it ideal for various industry use cases.

Real-World Applications of Hyperledger Iroha 2

Hyperledger Iroha 2 is already being implemented in innovative projects worldwide, reinforcing its reputation as a next-generation blockchain framework.

**Notable projects include:**

**Bokolo Cash (Solomon Islands)** 

* [A blockchain-based digital currency solution](https://soramitsu.co.jp/bokolo-cash) tailored to the Solomon Islands' underbanked population, ensuring secure and scalable financial transactions.

**Palau Invest (Republic of Palau)** 

* [A blockchain-based, retail savings bond system](https://soramitsu.co.jp/palauinvest) enabling citizens to invest in national infrastructure and earn, built on **Hyperledger Iroha 2 and the SORA v3 Hub Chain network**.

![](./images/912fee98b3c9ff4c480c123a9107b46e14d1695f75921641f072e15719d677cf.jpg)

**Digital Kina (Papua New Guinea)** 

* [The Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) proof of concept](https://soramitsu.co.jp/bpng-poc) was successfully started in December 2024, paving the way for financial inclusion and digital payment infrastructure in the country. It uses **Hyperledger Iroha 2 and the SORA v3 Hub Chain network**.

SORA v3: The Future of Decentralized Finance

Soramitsu is integrating **Hyperledger Iroha 2** into the **SORA v3** ecosystem, creating a hub that connects permissioned and public blockchain networks.

**SORA v3’s [testnet](https://medium.com/sora-xor/the-fujiwara-testnet-the-next-step-on-the-way-to-sora-v3-c11e49ab496c)**, **Fujiwara**, is now live, demonstrating the potential of **Hyperledger Iroha 2** as a foundation for financial services tailored for **Central Banks**, **MLDBs**, **NGOs**, and **traditional financial institutions**.

Based on Iroha 2, the **SORA v3 Hub Chain** will drive financial innovation globally, providing an affordable, secure, and scalable platform for tokenizing assets and delivering next-generation digital financial services.

![](./images/807562d4d51a1547f07e8fb060b29512dda5ba23fca804fb8c5136373cf1517c.svg)

**About Soramitsu**

[soramitsu.co.jp](https://soramitsu.co.jp)

Soramitsu is an award-winning global financial technology company with expertise in developing blockchain-based solutions for digital asset and identity management. Our mission is to use blockchain to promote innovation and solve pressing societal challenges.

![](./images/1e519313b833e3b2315d6d39de1e73b4c876b778ac9c099221f53086df07e769.png)

Utilising blockchain, Soramitsu has developed a digital currency infrastructure backbone for the [National Bank of Cambodia](https://soramitsu.co.jp/bakong-press-release), CBDC Proof-of-Concepts with the [Bank of the Lao PDR](https://soramitsu.co.jp/dlak-cbdc), the [Central Bank of Solomon Islands](https://soramitsu.co.jp/bokolo-cash) and the [Bank of Papua New Guinea](https://soramitsu.co.jp/bpng-poc), a blockchain-based digital savings bond system [Palau Invest](https://soramitsu.co.jp/palauinvest) in collaboration with the Government of Palau, a closed-loop [payment system for the University of Aizu](https://soramitsu.co.jp/byacco) in Japan, an identity verification system prototype for Bank Central Asia in Indonesia, shortlisted in the [Monetary Authority of Singapore CBDC Challenge](https://soramitsu.co.jp/global-central-bank-digital-currency-challenge/), participated in Asia-Pacific's first proof-of-concept test of a cross-border multi-currency security settlement system using distributed ledger technology with the Asian Development Bank, and in collaboration with the Risk Assurance Group and Orillion Solutions developed the technology core of the [Fraud Intelligence Blockchain](https://fraudintelligencelimited.com/). 
 
Soramitsu is a contributor to many open source projects, such as Klaytn (now [Kaia](https://kaia.io/)), South Korea's leading Layer-1 blockchain; [Zenswap](https://zenswap.io/), a cross-chain liquidity platform being developed by Soramitsu as a joint venture with Analog; the [SORA](https://sora.org/) crypto-economic system; the [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) DEX; and the DeFi wallet, [Fearless Wallet](https://fearlesswallet.io/). Soramitsu also collaborated with Filecoin to develop FUHON, a C++ implementation of their protocol; and with the Web3 Foundation to develop [KAGOME](https://github.com/soramitsu/kagome), the C++ [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) Host implementation. In addition, Soramitsu is the developer of and major contributor to the open-source blockchain platform [Hyperledger Iroha](https://iroha.tech/), a project of Hyperledger Foundation, now part of the [LF Decentralized Trust](https://lfdecentralizedtrust.org/), tailored for enterprise and public-sector use. As founding contributors to the Hyperledger Project, Soramitsu is building the digital infrastructure of the future. 
 
Based on these experiences, Soramitsu continues to deploy cutting-edge technology on a global scale in order to expedite financial inclusion and health, mitigate economic inefficiencies, and contribute to the fulfilment of the Sustainable Development Goals.
