# Polkadot Blockchain Training with Immunefi

## → Soramitsu celebrates an education partnership with Immunefi, providing theory and practical sessions covering the Polkadot blockchain basics, security, best practices, and practical sessions sharing the knowledge and experience from building on the ecosystem.

![](./images/167362b207a0ef79a9e6656e713861e1b073773c85055dfe1eeabe7de01f0692.png)

We are thrilled to announce a partnership with Immunefi consisting of knowledge sharing and training on Polkadot blockchain fundamentals and security, a comprehensive program designed to share a robust understanding of Polkadot's unique blockchain technology.

This achievement marks a significant milestone in our ongoing commitment to fostering innovation, collaboration, and knowledge sharing in the blockchain community.

Our strategic partnership with Immunefi made this course possible, and it delivered an insightful and practical curriculum.

Together, we have empowered participants to explore the intricacies of Polkadot's multi-chain framework, parachains, cross-chain interoperability, and especially security, among other key concepts.

Our collaborative efforts have underscored the importance of working together to drive forward the boundaries of what blockchain technology can achieve while helping Polkadot ecosystem builders provide the highest security standards for their users.

Highlights of the course include: 

* **In-depth Modules**: Covering the core principles of Polkadot, including its consensus mechanisms, governance, and scalability.
* **Hands-on Projects**: Allowing participants to apply their knowledge in real-world scenarios, fostering practical understanding and skills.

We extend our heartfelt gratitude to Immunefi for their trust. We look forward to seeing the innovative solutions you will undoubtedly create as you apply your newfound knowledge of Polkadot blockchain technology.
