## 45Accounts Overview
An address is active when it holds a set minimum amount on the Polkadot and
Kusama networks, this quantity is called the existential deposit (ED). In Polkadot
the ED is currently 1 DOT, while on the Kusama network, it is set at 0.0000333333
KSM.
If wallet funds are lower than the existential deposit amount, the account will be
deactivated (reaped) and any remaining funds are destroyed. In order to reactivate
it, you must send an amount larger than the existential deposit. However, this will
not restore the destroyed funds.
Further useful information:
•If you contribute to crowdloans, make sure that your balance doesn't drop
below the existential deposit or that you contribute your entire balance.
•If you are staking, do not direct staking rewards to an inactive account. If
rewards are less than 1 DOT (0.0000333333 KSM), you will lose them.
•If you're sending a transaction that will reduce your balance below the
existential deposit, you will be notified with the following message:
Existential Deposit11/2/25, 9:53 AM Fearless Wallet

## 88Crowdloan Overview
•Fearless Wallet supports all crowdloans on Kusama and Polkadot
•Participating in crowdloans is easy and intuitive using the Fearless Wallet mobile
app
Crowdloans are a great way for projects to raise funds to participate in parachain
slot auctions, and for passionate users to support the projects they follow in
achieving those slots. These projects can participate in one or more auctions for a
parachain lease, and users can collaborate throughout the duration of the auctions
(you can take a look at the Kusama website for more information on how
Crowdloans and Auctions work).
First and foremost, all Kusama and Polkadot crowdloans are supported on
Fearless Wallet. With that said, since users will be loaning their funds (this is not
really a loan, since funds are returned in full after auctions if the slot is not
provided, or after the lease is over if it is), projects offer rewards in the form of their
token in proportion of the amount provided by the user. This makes crowdloans an
attractive (and perhaps lucrative) way to support projects. Each project will have
different rewards (Fearless Wallet does not determine the reward payout or
amount, this is done by the projects. Fearless Wallet cannot answer inquiries
regarding reward payout dates or amounts), this should be taken into
consideration when allocating tokens to each project. There are plenty of projects
to choose from here is an overview of the current projects.
