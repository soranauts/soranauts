[Fearless Wallet](http://fearlesswallet.io) development status updates are released every two weeks, with development updates from across the community.

Greetings, Fearless community! It’s that time again when we pull back the curtains and grant you an exclusive glimpse into the ongoing evolution of Fearless Wallet. As pioneers in the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, we’ve always been driven by the pursuit of perfection and the relentless urge to push boundaries.

If you missed the previous ones: [July 28](/fearlesswallet/fearless-wallet-ecosystem-update-65-july-28-2023-2c822629cb9d) / [July 4](/fearlesswallet/fearless-wallet-ecosystem-update-65-july-4-2023-7406f5e7be83) / [June 16](/fearlesswallet/fearless-wallet-ecosystem-update-64-june-16-2023-d862760fc3da).

Today, this passion is reflected in every line of code, every pixel of design, and every interaction on our platform. As the decentralized future unfolds before our eyes, our commitment to providing a seamless, secure, and intuitive experience remains unwavering.

Our recent endeavours in the fearless mobile and desktop platforms and our collaborations have positioned us at the vanguard of innovation, and there’s plenty to share! Whether you’re a long-time supporter or a newcomer to our ecosystem, the following updates shed light on how we’re sculpting a cutting-edge experience for you.

A special mention goes out to the SORA community, who have an ongoing Polkadot parachain crowdloan. You can easily contribute to their efforts from the palm of your hand using the Fearless Wallet app. This is a special crowdloan, as **each DOT will be rewarded with 0.1337 g of synthetic gold (0.004716 XSTXAU)**!

Let’s delve deeper into the updates, partnerships, and visions shaping the next chapter of the Fearless Wallet journey.

Always have your [Fearless Wallet mobile app](https://fearlesswallet.io/), whether for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) or [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089), and the [Fearless browser extension](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/fearless-wallet/nhlnehondigmgckngjomcpcefcdplmgc) up to date to enjoy the latest improvements.

Now, without further ado, let’s dive into the Fearless Wallet’s exciting developments from the last weeks!

📱 Mobile 📱
------------

Fearless Wallet v2.4.1 for iOS and Android is here!

Updates contain the exciting feature to ‘Connect with Google’ and more improvements! Securely store and import your secret key pairs from Google storage. Say goodbye to the worry of losing your private keys.

Performance improvements to enhance your overall experience.

### What’s on the Horizon:

✅ SORA Card Phase #1. Finishing and brushing up on the integration

✅ EVM network support. Researching new networks and testing the solution

✅ UX and performance continue improvements for your convenience as usual

🖥 Desktop 🖥️
--------------

Fearless Wallet Chrome Extension, v1.2.2 and v1.2.3, with mobile platforms compatibility issues, have been released recently. It was necessary to support ‘Connect with Google’ cross-platform flows.

### What’s on the Horizon:

\- SORA Card Phase #1 testing

\- EVM networks support: testing and cleaning up the solution

\- Wallet Connect v2 integration to add compatibility with dApps based on EVM.

\- SORA relay chain staking

* Layout and UI have been finished recently
* The team has switched to API development and updates

🤝Collaborations and future initiatives 🤝
------------------------------------------

Collaborate with [**Ajuna Network**](https://ajuna.io/ajun/): Fearless wallet integration, community call excitement, future plans brewing, and upcoming drop.

💪 Exploring [Algorand’s network](https://algorand.com/) potential, exploring angles for collaboration

💪 Embrace [**Unique network**](https://unique.network/) opportunities, expand horizons together in Polkadot

💪 Nurture [**Genie Metaverse**](https://www.gemie.io/) partnership, aiming for future collaborations

Asia’s calling: Preparing for the next thrilling event!

**We’ve had a blast during** [**WebX Tokyo**](https://webx-asia.com/) **and looking to explore more!**

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
