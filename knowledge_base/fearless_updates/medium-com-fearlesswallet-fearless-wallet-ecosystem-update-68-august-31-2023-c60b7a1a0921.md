Welcome back to another chapter in the Fearless Wallet story. Every two weeks, we release these updates from our community of developers to keep our loyal users in the loop.

Greetings, Fearless community! Our bi-weekly ritual returns as we reveal the behind-the-scenes happenings of the Fearless Wallet. Leading the charge in the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, our goal remains the same: chasing perfection and continuously challenging the status quo.

Charting new territories has become second nature in our quest for excellence. Fearless Wallet continually expands its horizons as the flag bearers of the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, integrating innovative features and functionalities. This time, we have implemented **Connect with Google**, which will help open the doors to users who are not yet comfortable taking the step into the great unknown that is Web3.

By providing an interface that is familiar and somewhat “simpler” than remembering private keys, we can reach a new group of people just getting accustomed to Web3 platforms. They do not need to learn a new way to interact, which many media outlets have reported is one of the main obstacles to crypto adoption.

We will continue to optimize and improve the experience of using Fearless Wallet for DeFi. Upcoming releases such as EVM integration and SORA Card will make Fearless Wallet the only app you need to control all your Web3 and TradFi holdings.

Without further ado, let’s explore the recent advancements of Fearless Wallet!

📱 Mobile 📱
------------

Fearless Wallet v2.4.1 for iOS and Android was released on August 18th and 23rd.

The release contained exciting improvements, including ‘Connect with Google’!

You can easily and securely store and import your private keys from Google Drive, eliminating the worry of losing your valuable data.

We have also implemented performance improvements to enhance your overall experience.

### On the Horizon:

* **SORA Card Phase #1:** Wrapping up and refining the integration.
* **EVM Networks Support:** Delving into new networks, evaluating solutions, and integrating features like NFT ERC-721 support and Wallet Connect.
* **Continuous Enhancements:** Ongoing UX and performance upgrades for a smoother user experience.

🖥 Desktop 🖥️
--------------

The desktop version of Fearless Wallet isn’t resting on its laurels but is constantly improving!

* **SORA Card Phase #1:** Recap, rigorous testing, and preparing for v1.3.1.
* **EVM Networks Support:** Final testing and optimization for a seamless solution.
* **Wallet Connect v2 Integration:** Broadening our reach to connect with dApps based on EVM.
* **SORA Relay Chain Staking:** Awaiting integration.
* **API Development:** Rounding up and transitioning to user-case executions.

🤝Collaborations and future initiatives 🤝

Our collaborations serve as bridges to a more expansive and inclusive financial future.

* Exploring ongoing activities and forthcoming autumnal plans with [**Gemie Metaverse**](https://www.gemie.io/).
* We are collaborating with [**Ajuna Network**](https://ajuna.io/ajun/) to bring you a dynamic Twitter Space and an exclusive giveaway.
* Brewing secret strategies with the [**Pendulum team**](https://pendulumchain.org/).
* Ready your guesses for our following event location and stand a chance to win exclusive merchandise!
* Stay tuned! More invigorating partnerships are just around the corner.

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
