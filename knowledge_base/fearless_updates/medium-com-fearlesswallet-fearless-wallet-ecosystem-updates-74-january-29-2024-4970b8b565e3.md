As the new year unfolds, we’re already in full swing, working tirelessly to enhance and expand our services.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Our mission to build a wallet for a truly multi-chain future doesn’t slow down, and we’re excited to share the strides we’ve made so far in 2024.

In this update, we’ll glimpse the latest advancements and exciting developments for Fearless Wallet. So, let’s get right to it and discover what’s coming up!

### 🖥 Desktop 🖥️

The latest version of the Fearless Wallet browser extension, v2.3.1, is here.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

What’s new?

* **New EVM Chains:** Our browser extension now supports six new EVM-compatible chains. Explore, transact, and grow now on even more networks.
* **Seamless WalletConnect Upgrade:** Shift from the Beacon SDK to the intuitive WalletConnect SDK. Your mobile wallet effortlessly syncs with our browser extension, enabling you to manage transactions and dApp interactions easily.
* **Unified Mobile-Browser Experience:** Enhanced cross-device functionality allows you to switch between your mobile wallet and browser extension smoothly, maintaining access and control over your assets wherever you are.
* **UI/UX Improvements:** With aesthetic and performance enhancements, your fearless journey is more intuitive and responsive than ever.

### 🍎 iOS

Our iOS team released build v3.2.2 on Monday, December 19th. This version brought significant improvements, including:

* Enhancements for HASHI Substrate bridge
* XCM cross-chain transfer fixes

We’re already gearing up for our next update, which is scheduled for January 22nd. This upcoming build promises exciting new features like:

* A revamped NFT UI/UX for a smoother user experience
* Research into REEF Chain support
* Integration of new EVM chains
* Chainlink price feeds for assets like ETH, DOT, MATIC, and BNB.
* Continual UI/UX and performance upgrades

### 🤖 Android

On the Android front, we rolled out builds v3.1.1 & v3.1.2 on December 12th, packed with:

* Updates to the HASHI Substrate bridge
* Polkaswap enhancements with a new tap bar button
* SORA staking updates, including VAL rewards, aggregated rewards, and pending payouts.
* Pendulum ORML asset support for USDT.
* Balance fetching improvements and a Bokolo Cash update.
* Various fixes and UI/UX improvements.

Another update, v3.2.1, was released on January 18th, featuring:

* WalletConnect integration
* Network management and multi-chain UI/UX enhancements

Work is now focused on:

* Support for NFT ERC 721/1155
* Adding new EVM chains
* REEF chain support

### 🤝Collaborations and Future Initiatives 🤝

Look forward to these partnerships and upcoming projects:

* Teaming up with [**ZetaChain**](https://www.zetachain.com/) for co-marketing activities and planning.
* A co-marketing campaign with the [**Gemie Metaverse**](https://www.gemie.io/)
* Conducting fresh research in the Metaverse industry.
* Launching a new campaign with the [**Tezoro**](https://tezoro.io/) project.

Stay fearless as we continue to innovate and drive the future of Fearless Wallet!

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
