_Bringing a Unified Multi-chain Experience to the Masses_

We’re thrilled to announce a significant and fearless upgrade. Fearless Wallet, renowned for its commitment to the Kusama and Polkadot ecosystems, has broadened its horizons. We’ve extended our multi-chain capabilities to EVM networks, starting with the titans: Ethereum, Polygon (formerly Matic), and BNB Chain.

**Multi-chain Mastery in Your Pocket**
--------------------------------------

Fearless Wallet, already celebrated for its user-friendly design tailored to Substrate-based ecosystems, takes the user journey to new heights with this expansion. Introducing EVM network capabilities signifies a transformative moment for the wallet, amplifying its utility and solidifying its place as a top-tier, holistic cryptocurrency wallet solution.

### **What’s New?**

\- **Multi-chain capabilities for EVM networks** like Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, and Polygon. Easily view balances, make transfers, and explore the possibilities of multiple chains.

\- We’ve also added **improved onboarding flow**, making it easier than ever to get started with Fearless Wallet.

\- Additionally, enjoy an **improved network management** experience and benefit from the multi-chain state for the asset details screen.

### **Benefits**

**Multi-chain Accessibility:** Seamlessly switch between 50+ Substrate networks and Ethereum, Polygon, and BNB Chain, all from within the Fearless Wallet interface.

**One-stop Crypto Solution:** Manage, transact, stake, and engage with a myriad of networks without the hassle of jumping between different wallets or platforms. \*_Staking is available for a select list of networks only._

**Cross-platform Experience:** Whether on your smartphone or desktop, Fearless Wallet has got you covered. Available as a mobile app for iOS and Android and as a browser extension, a unified experience across devices is ensured.

**Continued Emphasis on Security**: Fearless Wallet’s commitment to user security remains unwavering. With local storage of private keys, users can feel confident that their assets are protected.

### **Get Started with Fearless Wallet**

**Mobile:**

iOS and Android users can download the app via this [link](https://qrcodes.pro/03tA5y).

**Browser extension:**

Those leveraging the wallet’s features on desktop browsers can download the extension [here](https://qrcodes.pro/dr0Fwi).

### **Stay fearless!**

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralised future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralised swaps of assets.
