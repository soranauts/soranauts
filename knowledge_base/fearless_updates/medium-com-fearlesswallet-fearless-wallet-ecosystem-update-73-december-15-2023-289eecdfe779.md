A deep dive into Fearless Wallet’s latest innovations, new features, partnerships, and more.

Welcome to the 73rd edition of the Fearless Wallet ecosystem updates! We’re excited to share the latest advancements of our ongoing work to revolutionize the decentralized finance landscape. In this update, we cover the ongoing SORA Polkadot parachain crowdloan, which offers enticing rewards for participants, as well as the substantial updates to Fearless Wallet on iOS and Android, and the enhancements to both mobile and desktop experiences. We also delve into our ever-expanding collaborations within the metaverse and other sectors.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Stay tuned as we unveil insights and developments that are shaping the future of Fearless Wallet!

The SORA Polkadot Parachain Crowdloan is ongoing | Contribute via Fearless Wallet
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fearless Wallet supports the SORA Polkadot parachain crowdloan, running from the 23rd of September 2023 to the 10th of January 2024 and beyond. Similar to the previous batch of crowd loans, **contributor rewards will be 0.1337gr of synthetic Gold (Kensetsu Gold) for every DOT contributed**. _More information on the Kensetsu project is available in the SORA Ecosystem Updates_.

### 📱Mobile📱

### 🍎 iOS

The latest v3.2.1 release includes:

🌐 The UI/UX is upgraded for better network management and versatile multi-chain state info.

🌉 The HASHI Substrate bridge is now available (you can already bridge $KSM from the Kusama — SORA network and vice versa)!

🏆 SORA history is expanded with VAL rewards on the SORA staking page!

🔗 Integrated USDT tokens via Pendulum ORML assets are supported

✨ Plus, enjoy the polished Polkaswap swaps tool through a brand-new button on the tap bar.

Currently, the team is working on several issues:

✅ NFT UI/UX update to provide users with a smooth and convenient flow.

✅ REEF Chain support research.

### 🤖 Android

The latest release, v 3.1.1 includes:

* HASHI Substrate bridge
* Polkaswap update with the button on the tab bar
* SORA staking updates: history update including VAL rewards + aggregated rewards + pending payouts
* Pendulum ORML asset support: USDT
* Balance fetching improvements + Bokolo Cash update
* Fixes and UI/UX improvements

Next steps include:

✅ NFT ERC 721 & 1155 support

✅ Wallet Connect integration

✅ Network management and asset details for multi-chain UI/UX update

### 🖥 Desktop 🖥️

### The build v 2.2.1 was released on December, the 7th containing the following updates:

💪 SORA relay chain native staking

💪 Several UI/UX improvements and fixes

### Next steps:

💪 Migration from Beacon SDK to Wallet Connect SDK for Wallets injected from Mobile clients

### Collaborations and future initiatives

🤝 Planning tasks for implementation with the [**Step app**](https://step.app/).

🤝 Research for the [**Tezoro**](https://tezoro.io/) collaboration and preparation of an educational plan for users.

🤝 Meetings with [**MetaBuzz**](https://metabuzz.medium.com/), [**Paima Studio Japan**](https://paimastudios.com/), and other stakeholders.

🤝 Maximizing brand visibility at the [**Pendulum**](https://pendulumchain.org/) Berlin event and fostering cross-team collaboration.

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
