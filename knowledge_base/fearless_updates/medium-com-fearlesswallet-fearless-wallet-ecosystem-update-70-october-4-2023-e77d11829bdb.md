In a world rapidly shifting towards decentralization, staying updated is paramount. Enter Fearless Wallet, a torchbearer in mobile wallets designed for the decentralized age.

Fueled by passion, innovation, and relentless commitment, our community of developers constantly evolves the platform to cater to the needs of our growing user base.

This fortnightly chapter brings forth many exhilarating updates spanning mobile and desktop domains. For those who’ve been eagerly waiting, and even those just tuning in, brace yourself for a journey through the latest enhancements redefining the landscape of decentralized mobile wallets. Welcome back to another chapter in the Fearless Wallet story.

### 📱 Mobile 📱

### Fearless Wallet v3.0.2 with EVM support, including Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, and Polygon, is live!

As part of this build,

✅ Users can view their balances of multiple assets and easily transfer funds between these chains.

✅ For iOS users, we have an additional treat! NFT assets are now available on Fearless Wallet. Easily explore and manage your NFT collection.

✅ This release also includes performance and UX improvements to ensure a smoother and more reliable experience

and

✅ Special flow for wallet import for the Pendulum event in Rio.

### 🖥 Desktop 🖥️

### Fearless Wallet browser extension v2.0.1 with EVM support, including Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, and Polygon, is now live!

💪 EVM networks supported: Ethereum, BNB Smart Chain, and Polygon are available in the App!

### Other developments:

💪 The integration of Wallet Connect v2 to add compatibility with dApps based on EVM is now ready for testing.

💪 SORA relay chain staking: user cases implementation. ETA is the second part of October.

### 🤝Collaborations and future initiatives 🤝

🤝 Attended the [**Dcentral event**](https://dcentralcon.com/) in Tokyo last week

🤝 Successfully built new connections and relationships with various teams: [**Immutable**](https://www.immutable.com/), [**Oasis**](https://oasisprotocol.org/), [**Pacific Meta**](https://pacific-meta.co.jp/), and [**Symbiosis**](https://symbiosis.finance/).

🤝 Expending our vision to Asia: released the builds with Japanese and Chinese localisations

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
