Welcome to Fearless Wallet Ecosystem Updates #83! This month’s highlights include the release of iOS version 4.0.1 on December 9, introducing full support for the TON blockchain. Users can now seamlessly manage wallets, track balances, make transfers, and see transaction histories. Ongoing projects like cross-chain swaps, SORA Card delivery, and expanded TON ecosystem integration across platforms are also making great strides.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Collaborations with Nomis, CUBE3.ai, and OKX are in progress, reflecting our commitment to innovation and collaboration in Web3.

Stay tuned as Fearless Wallet continues to deliver features and lead the way in DeFi.

### 🍎 iOS Updates

Version 4.0.1 was released on Monday, December 9th, and is rolling out to users with auto-updates active, and is available to download for all who manually update the App.

We’re thrilled to announce TON blockchain support! Here’s what you can do now:

* **TON Wallet Management:** Easily create, import, and manage your TON wallets. Track TON and JETTON balances, review transaction histories, and enjoy seamless transfers.

The build is available for manual download and is still being gradually rolled out.

**In progress:**

* Integrating Cross-chain swaps for several EVM chains
* SORA Card delivery

### 🤖 Android Updates

**In progress:**

* TON Ecosystem support
* Cross-chain swaps for plenty of EVM chain integration
* SORA Card delivery

### 🖥 Desktop Updates

In progress:

* TON Ecosystem support
* Addressing technical debt

### 🚀 Collaborations and Future Initiatives

* Discussing new opportunities and efforts with the [**Nomis**](https://nomis.cc/) team
* Expanding Fearless Wallet security facilities with [CUBE3.ai](http://cube3.ai/)
* OKX team catchups for the upcoming DEX aggregator release

Fearless Wallet’s innovation and dedication to the future remain unstoppable!

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet and browser extension tailored for the expansive horizons of the Polkadot and Ethereum ecosystems. Superior user interface, 80+ networks, DEX integration, and access to Web3 applications. Users own their tokens and interact directly with their favorite blockchain networks.
