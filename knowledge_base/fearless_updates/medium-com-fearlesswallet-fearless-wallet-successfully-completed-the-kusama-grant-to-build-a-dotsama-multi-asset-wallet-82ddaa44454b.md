### We are excited to announce the completion of a [Kusama grant](https://kusama.polkassembly.io/post/819) to build a wallet with multi-asset support for all [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) parachains.

[Fearless Wallet](https://fearlesswallet.io/) has successfully achieved all milestones of the grant we received from the Kusama council to create a _DOTsama_ multi-asset wallet, which supports all parachains in the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems.

### The Polkadot and Kusama Parachain Network Wallet

Fearless Wallet seeks to enable users to manage their Parachain tokens simply and intuitively from a single, easy-to-use mobile interface. Fearless Wallet is a Web3 native, non-custodial DeFi wallet, and it is the most user-friendly and intuitive wallet available.

As the Polkadot and Kusama space continues to expand (currently there are around 27+ networks and counting), Fearless Wallet will continue to support more tokens and networks as they are onboarded in the ecosystem.

Since its launch in 2020, Fearless Wallet has reached over **130,000 organic users worldwide** with currently over **1 million daily requests** (based on [OnFinality](https://onfinality.io/) nodes) to Kusama, Polkadot and all of their Parachains, and continues to grow with many more exciting features to come.

### DeFi and NFT Fearlessly!

The successful release of [Fearless Wallet 2.0](/fearlesswallet/fearless-wallet-2-0-multi-asset-parachain-support-release-eb6e2e8b9d31) included various improvements and implementations such as optimizing the user experience of [Staking](/fearlesswallet/stake-polkadot-and-kusama-fearlessly-fdde4381d73f), crowdloans, and other native Polkadot and Kusama functionalities, and most importantly, the release of multi-asset support. The continued progress with Fearless Wallet will constantly provide our users with new and exciting DeFi features and a great yet intuitive UI/UX. Two of the most important ones are the implementation of Non-Fungible assets and compatibility with decentralized exchanges.

[**RMRK**](https://www.rmrk.app/) **NFTs** are one of the most exciting ongoing developments for Fearless Wallet, and with the implementation of the [**Polkaswap**](https://polkaswap.io/#/swap) decentralized exchange, users will be able to **buy, sell, and trade cryptocurrencies** within the wallet itself.
