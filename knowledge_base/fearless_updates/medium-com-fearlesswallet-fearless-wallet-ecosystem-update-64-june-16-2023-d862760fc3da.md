[Fearless Wallet](http://fearlesswallet.io) development status updates are released every two weeks, with development updates from across the community.

Moving forward, Ecosystem updates from [SORA](https://sora.org), [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io), and [Fearless Wallet](https://fearlesswallet.io) will be presented separately, allowing each project to have a space to highlight their specific roadmaps and progress.

In the dynamic Dotsama ecosystem, of which Fearless Wallet is a part, every week is teeming with progress, innovation, and collaborative efforts.

Notably, the much-awaited [**Polkadot Decoded**](https://polkadot.network/ecosystem/events/decoded-2023/) event is only a fortnight away, and we anticipate a vibrant showcase of blockchain projects and speakers. Moreover, the [**Polkadot Hackathon Global Series**](https://www.polkadotglobalseries.com/?utm_source=ahtwitter&utm_medium=socials&utm_campaign=launch) launched by [**AngelHack**](https://www.angelhack.com/) has injected new energy into our development landscape. We eagerly await the innovative ideas this platform will attract to our ecosystem. The network is always expanding, with new teams gaining parachain leases within the [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) and [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) blockchains, and we continue to work toward adding compatibility to all these networks, so you can manage all your accounts in one place.

As part of Dotsama, Fearless Wallet serves as _the_ DeFi wallet mobile wallet and browser extension designed for the decentralized future of the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems and provides an exceptional user experience for users to interact with parachain and standalone substrate networks and their services. We are _fearlessly_ working toward adding all the features that will allow you to partake in DeFi and all the innovations that Web3 has to offer, so stay tuned for upcoming developments and let us know in the [Telegram channel](https://t.me/fearlesswallet) what features you would like to have added.

Always make sure to have your [Fearless Wallet mobile app](https://fearlesswallet.io/), whether for [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=jp.co.soramitsu.fearless) or [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/fearless-wallet/id1537251089), and the [Fearless browser extension](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/fearless-wallet/nhlnehondigmgckngjomcpcefcdplmgc) up to date to enjoy the latest improvements.

Now, without further ado, let’s dive into the Fearless Wallet’s exciting developments from the last weeks!

Fearless Wallet v 2.2.5 is released for iOS and Android
-------------------------------------------------------

We’re excited to announce that Fearless Wallet v2.2.5 has been recently released for both iOS and Android platforms. This update brings numerous enhancements, including improvements in UX/UI, as well as updates to SORA staking.

In alignment with the evolving Dotsama ecosystem, we’ve also incorporated features supporting XCM cross-chain transfers. In the background, we’ve been prepping for the next release (v2.2.6), rigorously testing and polishing the build and libraries before the public rollout.

Additionally, we’ve made significant strides in application improvements and reducing our tech debt, ensuring our app remains robust and reliable.

Collaborations and future initiatives

About Fearless Wallet
---------------------

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
