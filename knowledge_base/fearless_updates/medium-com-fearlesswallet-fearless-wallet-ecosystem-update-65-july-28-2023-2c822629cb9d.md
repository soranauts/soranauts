Greetings, Fearless community! The summer heat is sizzling, and so are we at Fearless Wallet. As always, we’ve been hard at work, committed to bringing you cutting-edge developments and striving to elevate your mobile wallet experience. Let’s dive into our recent progress!

📱 Mobile 📱
------------

**Fearless Wallet v2.3.1 has been live since July 7th and 14th** **on the App Store and Google Play.**

Press enter or click to view image in full size

The latest update is packed with exciting new features and enhancements:

* Experience the power of XCM cross-chain transfers, delivering seamless transactions across the most popular Polkadot and Kusama parachains.
* We’ve added new assets for several chains, expanding your possibilities.
* With performance and UI/UX improvements, managing your assets has never been smoother.

Get ready to unlock a world of opportunities and take your crypto journey to new heights. Update now and fearlessly seize the future!

### What’s on the Horizon

* We are preparing to integrate the Google Authorization and Backup feature. It is currently being tested and will be released soon.
* Adapting to the fresh Kusama and Polkadot Runtime upgrades, including the Controller account sunset.
* Our dedication to enhancing the UX and boosting performance continues unabated.

**Rolling out EVM network support. We’ve already etched out basic user scenarios for Ethereum mainnet and Goerli test networks, including:**

* Fetching ETH and ERC-20 asset balances.
* Streamlined transfers.
* Precise gas fee computation.
* Transaction history retrieval via Etherscan.

🖥 Desktop 🖥️
--------------

**Fearless Wallet’s Chrome Extension v1.2.1 is available on the** [**Chrome Web Store**](https://chrome.google.com/webstore/detail/fearless-wallet/nhlnehondigmgckngjomcpcefcdplmgc)**. Here’s a sneak peek into its offerings:**

* Turbo boosting performance and structural refinements.
* XCM cross-chain transfers.
* Upgrades to the visual and user experience.

### What’s on the Horizon

Fleshing out EVM networks support for Ethereum mainnet and Goerli test networks, with tests slated for the upcoming week.

* Harnessing the potential of SORA relay chain staking.
* Drafting comprehensive technical user scenarios.
* Crafting intuitive UI and designs for basic staking flow.

🤝 Forging Bonds and Charting the Road Ahead 🤝
-----------------------------------------------

Fearless Wallet was present in Japan, participating in one of the most sought-after Web3 events in the Land of the Rising Sun — [**VS2023 / IVS Crypto 2023 KYOTO**](https://www.youtube.com/@ivs3731)**.**

We had an opportunity to discuss possible future collaborations with the following projects:

[**🔥 MOOI Network**](https://mooinetwork.io/)

[**🔥 Ginco company**](https://www.ginco.co.jp/)

[**🔥 Gemie Metaverse**](https://www.gemie.io/)

[**🔥 Ternoa Network**](https://www.ternoa.network/)

Fearless Wallet was also in attendance at another of Asia’s leading Web3 conferences, [**WebX**](https://webx-asia.com/)**,** and working on expanding deeper into the Asian market.

Join us, share our enthusiasm and #StayFearless as we leap into the future! 🌟

Not only has July been a month of development but also education. Here are two pieces that provide a deep dive into Cross-Consensus Messaging (XCM) and a tutorial on how to use it fearlessly.

### [Understanding XCM on Polkadot: Enabling Cross-Chain Use-cases](/fearlesswallet/understanding-xcm-on-polkadot-and-how-it-enables-cross-chain-use-cases-e1902889d28)

In the ever-evolving realm of decentralized finance (DeFi), staying innovative is pivotal. With the introduction of Cross-Consensus Messaging (XCM) on Polkadot, a new horizon of unparalleled interoperability and cross-chain collaboration has dawned. Join us as we demystify XCM technology, shedding light on its intrinsic value and transformative potential within the Polkadot ecosystem. Understand its foundation and the vast array of use cases it brings to the table!

### [Introduction to Cross-Chain XCM Transfers in Fearless Wallet](/fearlesswallet/introduction-to-xcm-transfers-in-fearless-wallet-99e3b0d9e907)

The power of seamless token movements across the Polkadot ecosystem is here. Whether you’re a seasoned blockchain enthusiast or just beginning your crypto journey, Fearless Wallet simplifies the process of XCM transfers. It offers a clean and intuitive interface suitable for all users. Available on both iOS and Android platforms, our cross-chain XCM feature is designed for effortless token transfer experiences, such as transitioning from Moonbeam to Acala and beyond. Take a step-by-step journey on how to make the most of it with our tutorial.

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
