Welcome to Fearless Wallet Ecosystem Updates #82! This update (combining October and November) brings a host of exciting advancements, including secret ecosystem developments, expanded cross-chain swap integration, and ongoing enhancements for iOS and Android users.

Additionally, the desktop team is making significant strides with key-ring storage improvements and preparing the app for the Telegram Mini App Store.

Collaborations with partners like Viction Chain and Tap-to-Earn games are helping to attract more users, while future campaigns with 5ire chain and bitsCrunch promise even more growth. Stay tuned for these and other upcoming innovations as Fearless Wallet continues to lead the way in Web3 finance!

### 🍎 iOS and 🤖 Android ongoing work:

✅ New ecosystem delivery. No spoilers; you’ll have to wait and see…

✅ Cross-chain swap integration for plenty of EVM chains.

✅ SORA Card delivery

### 🖥 Desktop updates

💍 New key-ring storage development to easily support various types of key pairs. Released!

### 🚀 Current efforts:

👨‍🍳 Rebuilding the App to make it suitable for the Telegram Mini App Store. In the final stage.

🚀 New ecosystem delivery.

⚙️ Addressing technical debt.

### 🚀 Collaborations and Future Initiatives

🤝 [SmartCon 2024](https://smartcon.chain.link/) Participation (online)

🤝 Exploring grant programs on the[Atleta](https://atleta.network/) and [Injective](https://injective.com/ru) chains

🤝 Identifying local Asian Event Opportunities in 2025

**Fearless Wallet’s innovation and dedication to the future remain unstoppable!**

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet and browser extension tailored for the expansive horizons of the Polkadot and Ethereum ecosystems. Superior user interface, 80+ networks, DEX integration, and access to Web3 applications. Users own their tokens and interact directly with their favorite blockchain networks.
