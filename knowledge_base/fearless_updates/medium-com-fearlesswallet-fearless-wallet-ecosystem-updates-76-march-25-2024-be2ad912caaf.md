Hey everyone, welcome to our latest Ecosystem Updates! We’ve been busy over the last month, rolling out new features and forming partnerships to make your DeFi experience smoother and more secure across iOS, Android, and browser extension platforms.

In this update, we’re excited to share the improvements we’ve made, from fixing little bugs that make a big difference to adding new functionalities that you’ve been asking for. We’re making it easier to manage your digital assets, interact with NFTs, and get involved with staking — plus, we’re always looking for ways to make the Fearless Wallet not just a tool, but a community hub for DeFi enthusiasts.

We’re also putting a lot of effort into working with other projects and platforms to bring you more features and opportunities. From voting for Fearless Wallet on Magic Square to sending us your feedback on our browser extension (with a chance to win some USDT!), your involvement directly shapes what we do next.

As always, we’re grateful for your support and feedback. It’s what keeps us moving forward and helps us make Fearless Wallet the best it can be. Let’s keep the conversation going — drop us a note, vote, or share your thoughts on what you’d like to see next. Together, we’re making the DeFi space more accessible, secure, and fearless for everyone.

### 🍎 iOS

**The latest release v3.5.1, now features:**

XCM transfer amount validation. It prevents users from trapping their assets on the Polkadot relay chain while transferring 100% of their balance.

✅ Flexible and convenient “Onboarding” flow

✅ Updated screen for locked assets: comprehensive locked token details

✅ Some NFT UI and UX fixes

⚙ Tech debt: 
☝️ APY calculation syncs up between platforms for SORA & REEF staking

⚙ REEF staking updates:

✅ Full validator list, including validators waiting in queue

✅ Extrinsic history (staking actions)

✅ Updated APY calculation

☝️ Extra: Turkish and Indonesian Localizations

### 🤖 Android

**The latest release v3.4.1, now features:**

XCM transfer amount validation. It prevents users from trapping their assets on the Polkadot relay chain while transferring 100% of their balance

✅ Flexible and convenient “Onboarding” flow

✅ Finally, NFT ERC 721/1155 support for Ethereum and Polygon networks

✅ Claiming crowdloan rewards

✅ EVM connection optimisation

⚙ Tech debt: ☝️ APY calculation syncs up between platforms for SORA & REEF staking

⚙ REEF chain feedback fixes:

✅ Full validator list, including validators waiting in queue.

✅ Extrinsic history (staking actions)

✅ Updated APY calculation

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet and browser extension tailored for the expansive horizons of the Polkadot and Ethereum ecosystems. Superior user interface, 80+ networks, DEX integration, and access to Web3 applications. Users own their tokens and interact directly with their favorite blockchain network
