Discover the latest Fearless Wallet updates: new network support, enhanced bridges, and improved user experience across iOS, Android, and desktop platforms.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Welcome to the latest Fearless Wallet Ecosystem Updates! Fearless Wallet has released significant updates across iOS, Android, and desktop platforms. The latest iOS and Android versions introduce new bridges and integration features, while the desktop updates enhance user experience and functionality. These updates highlight our commitment to continuous improvement and innovation. Here are more details:

### New Networks

### Klaytn Mainnet Cypress

Fearless Wallet now supports the [Klaytn](https://klaytn.foundation/) Mainnet Cypress network and over 80 other networks, delivering a truly multi-chain experience!

You can view, send, and receive tokens on this network with ease. Supported assets include USDC, OUSDT, KLAY, and DAI.

### ZChains

Press enter or click to view image in full size

We’re excited to announce support for [ZChains](https://www.zchains.com/), a new Layer 1 blockchain running on PoS. ZChains is EVM-compatible, allowing communication with other networks and seamless transfers of ERC-20 and ERC-721 tokens.

### Athleta (Coming soon)

Press enter or click to view image in full size

We’re pleased to announce that soon, you’ll be able to manage assets on [Atleta](https://atleta.network/) — an EVM-compatible L1 blockchain built for the sports industry — directly within Fearless Wallet 🌐

This collaboration brings seamless access to Atleta’s ecosystem. More to come. Stay tuned.

### 🍎 iOS Updates

Press enter or click to view image in full size

**Version 3.7.2 is now live, featuring:**

✅ Polkaswap liquidity pool integration.

✅ New bridges and XCM routes:

⛓ SORA Mainnet ↔ Liberland

⛓ SORA Mainnet ↔ Ayala

⛓ SORA Mainnet ↔ Astar

⚙️ Addressing technical debt

**Ongoing work includes:**

✅ Nomis multi-chain score integration.

✅ Transfer flow refactoring allows for more scalability across blockchain ecosystems.

### 🤖 Android Updates

Press enter or click to view image in full size

**Version 3.6.1 is now live, featuring:**

✅ New bridges and XCM routes:

⛓ SORA Mainnet ↔ Liberland (ready for release)

⛓ SORA Mainnet ↔ Acala (ready for release)

⛓ SORA Mainnet ↔ Astar (ready for release)

⚙️ Fixes for balance fetching and asset management flows.

**Ongoing work includes:**

✅ Polkaswap liquidity pool integration → finishing flows.

✅ Nomis multi-chain score integration.

### 🖥 Desktop Updates

**Two new versions are now available.**

Press enter or click to view image in full size

**v 2.5.2 includes,**

💱 Polkaswap Liquidity Pools Support: Seamlessly manage your SORA liquidity with full Polkaswap integration.

🔛 Ethereum JSON Backup Compatibility: Easily backup and switch Ethereum wallets using JSON files, compatible with Metamask.

🖼 Performance & UI/UX Enhancements: Enjoy a faster, sleeker app with improved user interface and performance.

**v 2.6.1 includes,**

🔗 Seamless dApp Integration: EIP protocol integrations enable direct connections to dApps without intermediaries such as Wallet Connect.

🛠 Enhanced User Experience: Revamped Settings section for improved UI/UX. Minor performance boosts and UI/UX tweaks for a smoother browsing experience.

**Ongoing developments:**

* New keyring storage development to support various types of key pairs with ease
* Firefox release deployment is ongoing
* Addressing technical debt

### 🚀 Collaborations and future initiatives

🤝 Negotiation and integration of the [**5fire project**](https://www.5ire.org/home), including planning for future activities

🤝 Attendance at various crypto events in Tokyo, Japan

🤝 Including the [**Banshee NFT project**](https://bansheenft.xyz/) in meetings and planning for future integration.

🤝 Preparation for [**EDCON Tokyo**](https://www.edcon.io/) and associated side events.

🤝 Preparation and planning for integrating the [**Zchains project**](https://www.zchains.com/), including co-marketing activities.

**Fearless Wallet’s innovation and drive for the future never stop!**

### Bottom Line

Looking ahead, we have a _fearless_ focus on integrating Nomis multi-chain scores, and refining transfer flows for greater scalability. Desktop efforts include developing new key-ring storage and tackling Firefox release deployment issues.

Additionally, Fearless Wallet actively engages in collaborations and future initiatives, such as integrating the 5fire and Banshee NFT projects and preparing for EDCON Tokyo. With these developments, Fearless Wallet continues to push the boundaries of innovation and drive its mission forward. Stay Fearless!

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet and browser extension tailored for the expansive horizons of the Polkadot, SORA, and Ethereum ecosystems. Superior user interface, 80+ networks, DEX integration, and access to Web3 applications. Users own their tokens and interact directly with their favorite blockchain network.
