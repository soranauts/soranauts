As 2024 draws to a close, it’s time to reflect on the strides Fearless Wallet has made throughout the year. From significant feature rollouts to innovative partnerships, the Fearless Wallet ecosystem continued to expand and set new benchmarks for Web3 accessibility and multi-chain management.

Here are the highlights that made 2024 _fearless_.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

### Expanding Interoperability and Multi-Chain Support

This year, Fearless Wallet solidified its reputation as the go-to platform for multi-chain interactions. The platform expanded support for Substrate networks such as Avail DA, CAGA, ZChains, 5ire, Atleta Olympia, and Kaia Kairos Mainnet and Testnet, alongside EVM-based networks including Polygon ZKEVM, Avalanche C-chain, Viction, inEVM (Injective), Arbitrum One, OP Mainnet, Zetachain Testnet, X Layer Mainnet, Reef, and Manta.

As one of its latest additions, **Fearless Wallet fully supports the TON blockchain**. Users can manage wallets, track balances, send transfers, and view transaction histories — further expanding the app’s multi-chain capabilities.

The HASHI Substrate bridge, now available on iOS, Android, and browser extension, enables users to bridge assets between Substrate and the SORA network. The enhanced bridge functionality provides seamless connectivity between SORA Mainnet and several networks — Kusama, Polkadot, Liberland, Acala, and Astar. These improvements expand users’ ability to interact across diverse blockchain networks.

### Elevating User Experience

### Mobile

Fearless Wallet’s mobile apps on iOS and Android underwent major upgrades, including:

* **Improved asset management**: A streamlined asset management interface made handling assets more intuitive and efficient.
* **Enhanced NFT UI**: Integration of NFT standards (ERC-721/1155) on Ethereum and Polygon networks lets users easily manage digital collectables.
* **Performance boosts**: Optimized app loading, streamlined staking processes, and enhanced chain connection management delivered a faster, smoother user experience.
* **Languages**: Added Turkish 🇹🇷 and Indonesian 🇮🇩 language support.

### Desktop

Major updates to the desktop app included;

* **Ethereum JSON backup compatibility**: This feature enables users to back up and restore Ethereum wallets seamlessly.
* **Enhanced UI/UX**: Streamlined interface design and performance improvements made desktop interactions faster and more intuitive.
* **dApp connectivity**: Integration of EIP protocols enables direct connections to decentralized applications, removing the need for intermediaries.
* **Master password system**: A streamlined password migration system introduced master passwords that enhance security and provide flexible backup options through mnemonic phrases and JSON format.

_The communication of the Fearless browser extension with Fearless Mobile clients has also been enchanced. Your mobile wallet syncs smoothly with the browser extension, providing seamless transaction management and dApp interactions._

### Integrations

### Polkaswap Liquidity Pools

Press enter or click to view image in full size

The Fearless Wallet browser extension fully integrates with Polkaswap liquidity pools, letting users seamlessly manage their liquidity on the SORA Mainnet. This integration simplifies DeFi operations and makes interacting with decentralized liquidity platforms easier.

### Chainlink Price Feed

Real-time Chainlink price feeds were integrated for major assets, including $ETH, $BNB, $MATIC, and $DOT. This addition provides users with accurate, up-to-date market data to make informed trading decisions across multiple platforms.

### Nomis Multi-Chain Score

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Fearless Wallet integrated the Nomis multi-chain score system, enabling users to monitor blockchain activities across Ethereum, Polygon, and BNB Smart Chain networks. This feature helps users detect low-activity addresses and protect themselves against threats, scams, and Sybil attacks.

### Claim Crowdloan Rewards

Fearless Wallet improved its mobile crowdloan reward claiming system, allowing users to access their rewards directly in the app.

### Reef Staking Integration

Fearless Wallet has integrated comprehensive support for the Reef chain, offering users a complete suite of staking features. Users can now check balances, transfer, and stake assets directly through the wallet interface. This addition strengthens Fearless Wallet’s multi-chain capabilities while making interaction with the Reef ecosystem simple and seamless.

### OKLink Integration

Integration with OKLink’s data provider added advanced analytics and transaction history tracking, enhancing users' transparency and usability across multiple networks.

### Dwellir Integration

Press enter or click to view image in full size

In collaboration with Dwellir, Fearless Wallet gained access to over 40 high-speed nodes, improving blockchain infrastructure reliability and efficiency. This partnership underscores Fearless Wallet’s commitment to providing a seamless and secure user experience.

### Key Collaborations and Initiatives

### Partnership Campaigns

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Fearless Wallet strengthened its presence through impactful partnerships and collaborative campaigns, including initiatives with theGemie Metaverse, ZChains, Analog, and others. These campaigns celebrated fearless partnerships and offered exciting rewards, engaging users across the ecosystem.

### AMAs

Throughout the year, Fearless Wallet hosted a series of insightful AMAs with key partners such as Reef, OKLink, and the CAGA network. These sessions fostered community interaction and shared knowledge about staking integrations and ecosystem developments.

### Global Blockchain Events Participation

Fearless Wallet actively participated in prominent blockchain events worldwide, including the WOW Summit in Hong Kong, the Teamz Summit in Tokyo, and IVS Crypto 2024 in Kyoto. These events showcased Fearless Wallet’s capabilities and vision, solidifying its reputation.

### Looking Ahead to 2025

Fearless Wallet’s achievements in 2024 demonstrate its unwavering (and fearless) dedication to innovation and user-focused design. Looking ahead to 2025, we plan to implement more integrations, streamline transfer processes, and broaden support for emerging blockchain networks. Through these improvements, Fearless Wallet continues its mission to make decentralized finance accessible, intuitive, and secure for all users.

Thank you for being part of this journey. Stay Fearless!

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet and browser extension tailored for the expansive horizons of the Polkadot and Ethereum ecosystems. Superior user interface, 80+ networks, DEX integration, and access to Web3 applications. Users own their tokens and interact directly with their favorite blockchain networks.
