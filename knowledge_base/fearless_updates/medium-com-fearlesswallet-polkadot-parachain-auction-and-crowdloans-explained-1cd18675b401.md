Blockchains: Parachains and Relay Chains
----------------------------------------

Background: Polkadot (DOT) and Kusama (KSM) are so-called “Relay Chains” which are designed to host blockchains called “Parachains”.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

* **Relay Chain**: The Relay Chain is the central chain where transactions are finalized. (Polkadot and Kusama)
* **Parachains:** Blockchains that are pegged to the relay chain

Parachains are blockchains that operate on top of the Polkadot and Kusama networks. Different Parachains can be developed to match their individual design and specifications, e.g: decentralized finance (DeFi), identity management, and gaming. This is significant, as no single blockchain is optimal for all use case scenarios.

The [Polkadot](https://polkadot.network/) and [Kusama](https://kusama.network/) relay chain supports up to **100 parachains**. The parachain slot auction process decides which parachain should be connected to the relay chain.

How Do Polkadot Parachain Auctions Work?
----------------------------------------

A [parachain auction](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/learn-auction) offers an opportunity for [Substrate](https://substrate.io/)\-based (framework for building blockchains on Polkadot or Kusama) blockchains to connect to the Polkadot or Kusama relay chains. This is achieved via an open and permissionless auction while benefiting from the security of the relay chain (Polkadot and Kusama). However, Parachain slots are scarce by design in order to encourage competition and to optimize the relay chain’s resources. On Polkadot, the parachain leasing period is a max. of 96 weeks, while on Kusama, the parachain leasing period is max. 48 weeks.

So-called “_candle auctions_” are the auction mechanism for parachain auctions. This particular auction-style traces back to the 17th century, when the bidding was allowed until a candle burned out. The highest bid up until that point was considered the winner. Candle auctions are implemented to avoid “auction sniping”, where an entity would come in at the end of the auction and outbid all other contenders. Thus. candle auctions have the major advantage of not knowing exactly when the auction will end, and therefore, participants are more likely to place early bids that correspond to their estimated value of the auctioned item. This has led to more accurate pricing in parachain slot auctions. For more detailed information you can read the [research article on candlelight auctions by the Web3 Foundation.](https://polkadot.network/blog/research-update-the-case-for-candle-auctions/)

Crowdloans Are Used to Win a Parachain Auction
----------------------------------------------

Crowdloans allow the community to provide a project with DOT or KSM to participate in a parachain auction in order to win a parachain slot. The Сrowdloan process allows the community to contribute to the project by locking their own DOT or KSM until the lease period is over. In this process, the projects that participate in parachain auctions reward DOT or KSM contributors with their native blockchain token. The contributed DOT (for Polkadot auction) or KSM (for Kusama auction) are automatically returned after the parachain leasing period is over. And if the project does not win the parachain auction, then the DOT or KSM tokens are returned in a few days. (current batch).

For more detailed information on crowdloans please [visit the Polkadot Wiki](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/learn-crowdloans)

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

What is a Crowdloan?
--------------------

Crowdloans allow the community to provide DOT or KSM to a Crowdloan Campaign (project) to participate in a parachain auction in order to win a parachain slot. The Сrowdloan process allows the community to contribute to a project by locking their own DOT or KSM until the lease period is over. In this process, the projects reward the DOT or KSM contributors with their native blockchain token.

Detailed information is [available here](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/learn-crowdloans)

How can I participate in Crowdloans?
------------------------------------

On the Fearless Wallet App, you can find a button labelled “**Crowdloans**” on the bottom of the screen. Click on it, then provide your contribution to participate in the selected Crowdloan Campaign. If you use Fearless Wallet, this ensures that your DOT or KSM tokens are returned to your account automatically after the leasing period.

What is a Parachain Auction?
----------------------------

Parachain auctions are candlelight auctions that are held on the Polkadot or Kusama network in a permissionless way to determine which blockchain is going to be connected as a parachain.

Detailed information is [available here](https://wiki.polkadot.network/docs/learn-auction)

What is the minimum amount of DOT or KSM required to participate in Crowdloans?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The minimum amount for Polkadot crowdloan contribution is 5 DOT

The minimum amount for Kusama crowdloan contribution is 0.1 KSM

When will I get my DOT or KSM back?
-----------------------------------

If a Crowdloan Campaign wins the parachain auction, you will receive your DOT or KSM tokens after the leasing period (**24 months on Polkadot and 48 weeks on Kusama**), automatically in the account associated with the Fearless Wallet app you used to contribute.

If a project does not win a parachain slot, you will get your DOT or KSM back in the account associated with your Fearless Wallet app in a few days after the auctions are over.

For more information on parachain auctions, check out the [Fearless Wallet Wiki section on Crowdloans](https://wiki.fearlesswallet.io/crowdloans/crowdloan-overview).
