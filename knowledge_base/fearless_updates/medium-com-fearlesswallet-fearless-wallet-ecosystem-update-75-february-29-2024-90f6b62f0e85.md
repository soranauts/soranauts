Latest on Fearless Wallet development with Reef Chain, Chainlink price feeds, and enhanced wallet functionalities.

Welcome back, Fearless fam!

This update will bring you up to speed with exciting advancements across various platforms, including iOS, Android, and Desktop, ensuring that users remain at the forefront of the decentralized finance (DeFi) revolution with us! In this issue, we delve into the latest features and improvements like REEF Chain integration, real-time price feeds powered by Chainlink, and enhanced crowdloan reward-claiming functionalities.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Join us as we explore the highlights and future endeavors that set new benchmarks in the DeFi space.

### 🍎 iOS

Press enter or click to view image in full size

### Version 3.4.1 Was Successfully Released on Wednesday, February 21, 2024.

REEF Chain Integration: Enjoy comprehensive support for the REEF chain — check balances, make transfers, and engage with a full suite of staking features. Fearlessly dive into new opportunities.

Real-Time Price Feeds: Powered by Chainlink, get up-to-the-minute price information for ETH, BNB, MATIC, and DOT. Make smarter choices with reliable data at your fingertips.

Crowdloan Reward Claiming: Claim your rewards from crowdloan contributions directly within the app. Simplify your journey towards growth within the DeFi space.

**Under development:**

✅ Staking updates and improvements, including SORA staking APY evaluation

✅ UI and UX updates

✅ Architectural updates and fine-tuning stability

### 🤖 Android

### Version 3.3.1 Was Released on Wednesday, February 14th.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

Dive deeper into the DeFi world with the latest update, supporting the REEF chain. Manage your assets effortlessly with balances, transfers, and a comprehensive staking suite. 💪

Crucial bugs have been squashed to enhance stability and ensure your experience is smoother than ever.

Stay ahead with real-time asset tracking: Chainlink price feeds are now available for your favorite assets — ETH, BNB, MATIC, and DOT. Know your portfolio’s worth at a glance and make informed decisions.

**In progress:**

✅ NFT ERC 721/1155 support

✅ EVM and Substrate chains’ connection stability improvements

✅ Crowdloan reward claiming

✅ Performance improvements

### 🖥 Desktop

### The release 2.4.1 is in progress;

* HASHI Substrate bridge
* NFT ERC 721/1155 support

The following steps are:

💪 EIP protocol integrations

💪 Indexing project development for bridge transaction tracking
