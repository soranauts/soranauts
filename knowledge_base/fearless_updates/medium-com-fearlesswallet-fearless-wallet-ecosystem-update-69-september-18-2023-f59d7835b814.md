Welcome back to another chapter in the Fearless Wallet story. Every two weeks, we release these updates from our community of developers to keep our loyal users in the loop.

Press enter or click to view image in full size

The Fearless Wallet team is back with some exciting updates! From mobile to desktop, we’ve been hard at work making the app better for our community.

On the mobile front, we’re rolling out support for EVM networks and adding new features for the Pendulum event in Rio. Our design team is crafting a more intuitive user interface while the desktop version is prepping for its v2.0.1 release with added EVM support!

We’ve teamed up with Pendulum and are gearing up for a major NFT event in Tokyo. A lot is happening, so let’s jump in and unpack all the latest news!

Without further ado, let’s explore the recent advancements of Fearless Wallet!

📱 Mobile 📱
------------

Let’s kick things off with a peek into our mobile developments. From enhancing support for EVM networks to refining the user experience, our team is laser-focused on providing the best for our users. Here’s a breakdown of what we’ve been up to:

✅ EVM networks support. This feature is currently undergoing QA, testing the build, and looking for bugs. The release for iOS will contain ERC-721 NFT-standard support.

✅ A Special flow to import your wallet is being implemented for the Pendulum event in Rio. We have finished the main development stage and handed over builds to the Pendulum team for testing.

✅ UX and performance continue improvements for your convenience.

✅ SORA Card Phase #1. The development is temporarily on hold as we await the next SDK update from our partners.

🖥 Desktop 🖥️
--------------

Swinging over to the desktop side of things, we’ve been just as busy ensuring a seamless and powerful user experience. Whether it’s enhancing compatibility with dApps or preparing for the much-anticipated next releases, here’s a snapshot of the recent strides made on the desktop front:

💪 EVM networks support: testing and cleaning up the solution, preparing the next release, v2.0.1.

💪 Wallet Connect v2 integration to add compatibility with EVM-based dApps.

💪 SORA relay chain staking: user cases implementation.

💪 SORA Card Phase #1 is temporarily on hold.

🤝 Collaborations and future initiatives 🤝
-------------------------------------------

Our collaborations serve as bridges to a more expansive and inclusive financial future.

🤝 Successfully rolled out a strategic partnership with [**Pendulum**](https://pendulumchain.org/), with exciting developments on the horizon. Eyes on Brazil Pessoal!

🤝 Engaged with over 60 potential partners and project teams during [**Korean Blockchain Week**](https://koreablockchainweek.com/).

🤝 Participated in VIP events in Seoul, establishing critical relationships and connections.

🤝 Preparing for an NFT event in Tokyo, marking an expansion into the Japanese market.

🤝 Achieved successful closure of the 2nd Season with [**Awesome Ajuna Avatars**](https://aaa.ajuna.io/), reinforcing our commitment to quality and community engagement.

🤝 Almost completed Fearless Chinese localization.

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
