Welcome to Fearless Wallet Ecosystem Updates #84!

This month’s edition features exciting developments, including the release of iOS v4.0.3, forthcoming cross-chain swaps, enhanced TON support, and essential collaborations propelling Fearless Wallet forward.

Stay tuned for these and other forthcoming innovations as Fearless Wallet remains at the forefront of Web3 finance!

### iOS Updates

### Done:

Version 4.0.3 was released on 30 January but experienced delays during Apple’s review process. The update includes:

* **TON Connect Integration:** Effortlessly connect with TON dApps and discover a realm of decentralised opportunities.
* **Integrated dApp Browser:** A portal to the TON ecosystem, enabling users to explore and engage with dApps directly within the application.

We’re actively working with Apple’s review team to expedite the update.

### In Progress:

**Cross-chain swaps** across multiple EVM chains are on the verge of release. The OKX integration is nearing completion, with the team fine-tuning edge cases and addressing bugs.

**Integration of Coinbase on-ramp** for assets such as DOT, ETH, and others. This complete feature will be included in the OKX update (v4.1.1).

[**SORA Card**](https://soracard.com) **delivery** is progressing as planned.

### Android Updates

**TON Ecosystem support** is coming with version 4.0.1, scheduled for release on February 28.

**Coinbase on-ramp integration** for assets, including DOT and ETH, will be included in the next update (v4.0.2), set for release next week.

**SORA Card delivery** is a priority, having passed testing and nearing final integration testing before release.

### Desktop Updates

### Latest Release:

**TON ecosystem support v3.0.3 was released, featuring:**

* **Complete TON Integration:** Seamlessly manage TON assets.
* **TON Wallet v4 Support:** Improved security and functionality with the latest wallet technology.
* **TON & Jetton Management:** View balances and transfer TON and Jettons effortlessly.
* **Transaction History:** Easily track all your TON transactions in one place.

### Collaborations and Future Initiatives

* [**CAGA Chain**](https://www.cagacrypto.com/): The request to add CAGA Mainnet has been processed, and the network is now available on Fearless Wallet.
* [**Dwellir**](https://www.dwellir.com/): The team provided updates on their new dashboard and supported chains.
* [**StealthEx**](https://stealthex.io/): A collaboration discussion is scheduled for March, after the current development scope is completed.

Fearless Wallet’s innovation and commitment to the future remain unstoppable!

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a mobile wallet and browser extension designed for the Polkadot and Ethereum ecosystems. With a superior user interface, support for 80+ networks, DEX integration, and Web3 access, Fearless Wallet enables users to securely manage their assets and interact directly with blockchain networks.
