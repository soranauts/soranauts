Welcome to the Fearless Wallet ecosystem updates. We’re thrilled to share the latest developments and exciting features that continue to shape our journey in the dynamic world of decentralized finance.

Diving into some highlights, we’re actively supporting the SORA Polkadot parachain crowdloan with enticing rewards for community contributors. Our latest mobile and desktop updates have brought significant enhancements, and we’re constantly exploring new collaborations with metaverse projects and beyond.

Stay tuned for more insights and developments defining the future of Fearless Wallet!

The SORA Polkadot Parachain Crowdloan is ongoing | Contribute via Fearless Wallet
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Fearless Wallet supports the SORA Polkadot parachain crowdloan, running from the 23rd of September 2023 to the 10th of January 2024. Similar to the previous batch of crowd loans, **contributor rewards will be 0.1337gr of synthetic Gold (Kensetsu Gold) for every DOT contributed**. _More information on the Kensetsu project is available in the SORA Ecosystem Updates_.

### 📱Mobile📱

### 🍎 iOS

The latest version of Fearless Wallet v3.0.6 contains:

✅ WalletConnect integration.

✅ Several UI/UX improvements and fixes.

Currently, the team is working on multiple initiatives:

✅ NFT ERC 1155 transfers and general support on Ethereum and Polygon chains.

✅ SORA Staking updates: improve reward histories and calculations with the pending payouts feature.

✅ HASHI Substrate bridge integration.

✅ REEF Chain support research.

### 🤖 Android

Currently, the team is committed to releasing:

✅ NFT ERC 721 & 1155 support.

✅ Wallet Connect integration.

✅ SORA Staking updates: improve reward histories and calculations together with the pending payouts feature.

✅ HASHI Substrate bridge integration.

### 🖥 Desktop 🖥️

### Fearless Wallet Browser v2.1.1 and now includes:

💪 Wallet Connect integration.

💪 Several UI/UX improvements and fixes.

### **What’s next?**

💪 Migration from Beacon SDK to Wallet Connect SDK for Wallets injected from the Mobile clients.

💪 SORA relay chain staking: testing and brushing app the solution before the next release.

It is essential to clarify what Fearless Wallet XOR staking can and cannot do. **Find that out** [**here**](/fearlesswallet/fearless-xor-staking-the-advanced-guide-05932341fd8f)**.**

### Fearless Wallet Polkadot Parachain Crowdloan Unlock Update

Press enter or click to view image in full size

The end of the DOT lock-up period for Polkadot Parachain crowdloans symbolises the tremendous growth and innovation we’ve witnessed over the past two years. Users can now effortlessly access their DOT tokens, which **will be automatically returned to their wallets**, and those with v**ested parachain tokens from crowdloan participation can claim them now from the projects they supported**. We congratulate the parachain teams on reclaiming their tokens, and enhancing their community and treasury for future endeavors. [Read more here](/fearlesswallet/fearless-wallet-dot-slot-auction-update-8415a78a3c3f).

### About Fearless Wallet

[**Fearless Wallet**](https://fearlesswallet.io/) is a bespoke mobile wallet designed for the decentralized future on the Polkadot and Kusama ecosystems, with native support for iOS and Android platforms. It provides a thrilling user experience, fast performance, and secure storage for your accounts. Fearless Wallet will integrate [Polkaswap](https://polkaswap.io/) for easy, decentralized swaps of assets.
