## What is Tonswap?

Tonswap is a next-generation decentralized exchange (DEX) and launchpad built on The Open Network (TON) blockchain. It combines cutting-edge concentrated liquidity tech with a user-friendly design to make DeFi feel as easy as chatting with friends.

As a CLMM-based DEX, Tonswap lets liquidity providers focus their funds for optimized liquidity, meaning traders enjoy minimal slippage and better prices. It’s non-custodial and secure – you always control your assets – and deeply integrated with Telegram for a mobile-first experience.

## Why TON?

The TON blockchain offers unique advantages that make it the perfect foundation for Tonswap’s ecosystem:

![globe icon](/icons/globe.svg)

### Massive User Base

TON was initially conceived by Telegram, and it now powers Telegram’s Web3 features. With native Telegram integration, TON can tap into a user base of 1 B+ potential users – making decentralized trading more accessible than ever.

![networks icon](/icons/networks.svg)

### Speed & Scalability

TON’s sharded Layer-1 infrastructure achieves sub-second transaction finality and can handle millions of transactions, ensuring your trades execute lightning-fast with no waiting.

![wallet icon](/icons/wallet.svg)

### Ultra-Low Fees

On TON, transaction costs are virtually zero . Even high-frequency traders and micro-swaps won’t be eaten up by fees, ensuring cost-effective trading for all.

![telegram icon](/icons/telegram.svg)

### Telegram Native

Thanks to TON’s deep Telegram integration (TON is now the exclusive blockchain for Telegram’s mini-apps ), users can access Tonswap directly through Telegram with a familiar interface. Managing a crypto trade can be as simple as sending a sticker in your chat.

![ton-icon icon](/icons/ton-icon.svg)

### Growing Ecosystem

TON’s ecosystem is expanding rapidly, with a vibrant community and excellent developer support. From built-in Telegram wallets to a host of TON dApps, building on TON means Tonswap users benefit from a thriving DeFi environment and interoperability within the Telegram super-app.

## Built to Launch

![star icon](/icons/star.svg)

### All-in-One Launch Hub

The Tonswap Launchpad provides a one-stop solution for emerging projects to launch new tokens and kickstart their communities. Projects can easily initialize a smart contract, create a token, and immediately list on the DEX for trading. No deep technical expertise required – we simplify the heavy lifting so founders can focus on innovation.

![lightning icon](/icons/lightning.svg)

### Instant Market Access

Every launchpad project benefits from instant DEX liquidity. By launching through Tonswap, projects automatically get a liquid market for their token from day one, allowing early supporters to trade immediately. Additionally, being featured on our platform provides projects with exposure to TON’s large user base and Telegram community.

![github icon](/icons/github.svg)

### No Gatekeepers, Just Code

Tonswap’s launchpad is open and community-driven. No centralized gatekeepers decide which project can launch; instead, community interest drives success. All launches run on smart contracts for transparency and fairness. Supporters can trust token distribution and liquidity locking (if any) follow on-chain rules, levelling the field for big and small players alike.

![telegram icon](/icons/telegram.svg)

### Community at Your Fingertips

Uniquely, Tonswap integrates launchpad projects with Telegram. Projects can engage users directly through Telegram bots and channels during their launch. Community members get updates, participate in discussions, and even receive launch alerts right within Telegram, ensuring high engagement and launch experience that standard launchpads can’t match.
