## What Is MeowFi?[🔗](#what-is-meowfi)

Meet **Meowkoto**, a samurai cat crypto trader who’s clawing his way up from humble beginnings to great success. **MeowFi** is a Telegram mini app game where your taps help this crypto-cat build a global trading empire. In plain terms, _it’s a simple clicker game_, but you don’t need any crypto knowledge, hats or special abilities to enjoy it. It’s lighthearted and **just for fun** (TSP are **probably nothing**), offering the simple satisfaction of watching Meowkoto rise to a wealthy shogun as you play. Meowkoto is so fluffy and determined that [the Observers have even covered his story](https://www.observers.com/meowfi-a-cat-themed-tapper-with-bigger-ambitions/)!

## Quick Start (Telegram)[🔗](#quick-start-telegram)

MeowFi runs inside Telegram, so starting the game is a snap:

1. **Open the bot** — Tap the link [@tonswap\_org\_bot](https://t.me/tonswap_org_bot) to launch the MeowFi bot in Telegram.
2. **Launch the game** — Press “Open” in the bot chat to load the MeowFi mini app (Web App) interface.
3. **Finish onboarding** — Complete the quick tutorial to meet Meowkoto and learn the basics.
4. **Start tapping!** — Tap on Meowkoto (the samurai cat) to begin earning TSP points. A limited energy bar prevents endless tapping (it refills over time). Each tap triggers an animation for feedback.
5. **Upgrade income** — Spend your TSP on upgrades that boost your passive income (TSP per hour). Upgrades let you keep earning even when you’re not tapping.
6. **Claim a daily reward** — Hit the daily check-in button to grab a free TSP bonus (you can gamble it double-or-nothing if you’re feeling lucky that day).
7. **Referral perks** — Invite friends via your link and earn a paw of their earnings. You get 5% from direct referrals, a bit less from each level beyond (down to 1% five levels deep). Plus, your friends get a 5% bonus based on what you earn, so everybody benefits.
8. **Watch out for the idle tax: Stay active** — if you go idle for over 12 hours, a small ~1% idle tax will chip away at your points. So keep tapping to stay on top of the global leaderboard!

Tap to get as many TSP as you can! There might be a nice reward soon…

## What Makes MeowFi Different[🔗](#what-makes-meowfi-different)

* **Built into Telegram:** MeowFi runs right in Telegram, so there’s no install or signup needed.
* **Tactile & responsive:** Taps are instant and satisfying, with subtle haptic feedback and fun bubble animations. The server-verified tap system and energy limits ensure the game stays cheat-free without lag.
* **Social play:** Unlike most idle games, MeowFi is surprisingly social. The five-level referral system makes progression a team effort — your friends help you earn more (and you help them), alongside the competition of leaderboards.
* **Fair way to earn TSP:** No paywalls here. You _can_ support the game with small donations (e.g. Stars or TON) for a TSP boost, but smart tapping and strategy win, not just deep pockets.

## Tips for Day One[🔗](#tips-for-day-one)

Keep these pointers in mind as you start:

* **Tap on Meowkoto:** Only taps on the cat count. If your TSP isn’t increasing, make sure you’re tapping the cat’s image (not the background).
* **Mind your energy:** Watch the energy bar (lightning icon). When it’s empty, take a cat nap until it refills. No energy = no points from tapping.
* **Daily first:** Grab your daily reward early each day. If you try the double-or-nothing gamble and lose, you’ll know to focus on tapping until tomorrow.
* **Log in twice a day:** Check in every 12 hours to avoid the ~1% idle tax on your points. Consistency pays!
* **Invite friends:** Even one friend joining via your link gives you bonus income. Plus, it’s more fun to have a buddy in the game.

## FAQ[🔗](#faq)

**\- How do I access MeowFi?**

Open the MeowFi bot in Telegram and tap “Open” to launch the game. MeowFi only runs inside the Telegram app, not in a regular browser.

**\- Does playing cost anything?**

No. MeowFi is free to play. Optional donations via **Telegram Stars** (and soon TON) can give you some extra in-game TSP, but TSP points have no real-world value.

**\- How do referrals work?**

Share your invite link (from the **Invite** menu) with friends. You’ll earn 5% of all TSP your direct referrals earn, 4% from second-level referrals, and so on (down to 1% at the fifth level). Each friend you invite also gets a bonus equal to 5% of _your_ earnings, so everyone benefits.

**\- What data does MeowFi collect, and can I delete my account?**

Just your Telegram ID and name (for login). It doesn’t gather any other personal data. If you ever want to wipe your data, use **Delete Account** in the game’s menu to erase your account from the server.

**\- I have an issue or suggestion — where can I get help?**

If you need help, you can message the bot or ask in the [TONSWAP Telegram group](https://t.me/tonswap_org). The community (and devs) are usually quick to respond. If a donation or payment isn’t working, make sure you’re playing inside Telegram (not a web browser) and that your connection is stable, then try again.
