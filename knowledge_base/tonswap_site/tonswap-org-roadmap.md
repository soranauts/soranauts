# Roadmap Preview

We’re moving fast to bring Tonswap from vision to reality. Here’s a sneak peek at our development roadmap:

## Q3 2025 – Testnet Beta (Status: In progress)

* Public Testnet launch of Tonswap’s DEX with concentrated liquidity pools.
* Early adopters and developers will be invited to try out swapping and liquidity providing in a sandbox environment.
* We’ll run a bug bounty to ensure the smart contracts are rock-solid before mainnet.

## Q4 2025 – Mainnet & Telegram Mini-App

* Official Mainnet launch of Tonswap DEX on the TON blockchain, open to all users.
* Liquidity mining and LP incentives will kick off to bootstrap liquidity.
* Importantly, this phase also introduces the Tonswap Telegram Mini-App, which enables a complete trading experience directly within Telegram. DeFi will truly go mobile, open and social.

## Q1 2026 – Launchpad Goes Live

* Debut of the Tonswap Launchpad with the first cohort of TON ecosystem projects.
* Users will be able to discover and participate in new token launches directly via our platform.
* This period will also focus on expanding our community features and educational content to support new users coming via Telegram.

### 2026 and Beyond — Expanded DeFi Suite

Following its launch, we plan to introduce advanced features to solidify Tonswap as a one-stop DeFi hub. These include exploring perpetual swaps (decentralized futures trading) and a cross-chain yield aggregator to maximize returns across multiple ecosystems.

We’ll also roll out mobile-first staking vaults and governance features, empowering the community to guide Tonswap’s future. Our ultimate goal is to innovate continuously, bringing CEX-like performance together with DeFi’s openness — all on TON.
