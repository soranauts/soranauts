# Decentralization in Cryptoeconomic Systems: Exploring Dimensions and Relationships via Fuzzy Cognitive Mapping

## Abstract

Decentralization is a fundamental yet poorly defined concept in cryptoeconomics and blockchain research. This paper addresses these gaps by offering a comprehensive analysis of decentralization in cryptoeconomic systems (CESs) from technical, political, and economic perspectives. To achieve this, we first establish a theoretical foundation by examining decentralization across various disciplines, providing a more nuanced definition relevant to CESs. Next, we identify key decentralization factors through a qualitative content analysis (QCA). Finally, we employ Fuzzy Cognitive Mapping (FCM) to analyze the interdependencies among these factors, uncovering complex interactions that shape decentralization dynamics. Our findings contribute to the theoretical discourse on decentralization by demonstrating its intricate and interconnected nature. Moreover, the application of FCM introduces a novel methodological approach that enhances our ability to model and predict decentralization trends in cryptoeconomic ecosystems. As decentralized systems continue to evolve, understanding these relationships is crucial for designing more resilient and adaptive blockchain networks. This study provides a foundation for future empirical research, encouraging a multidimensional approach to decentralization in cryptoeconomics.
