# Enhancing Transparency and Compliance in Blockchain-Based Pension Systems: The Role of Blockchain-Enabled Audit Trails

## Abstract

Blockchain technology offers a transformative solution for enhancing transparency, security, and efficiency in public administration, particularly within pension systems. However, ensuring that these systems comply with regulatory frameworks and provide trustworthy, auditable records remains a significant challenge. This paper explores the integration of blockchain with Business Process Management (BPM) to create secure and transparent audit trails within pension systems. We propose a theoretical framework for implementing blockchain-based audit trails, which ensures data integrity, compliance, and security by leveraging blockchain?s immutable ledger and smart contract automation. The article synthesizes existing research on blockchain-enabled audit systems in various sectors, identifies gaps, and builds on these findings to outline practical applications in the pension sector. We also introduce a comparative analysis of blockchain audit trails versus traditional auditing methods, highlighting key advantages in transparency, fraud reduction, and regulatory compliance. Finally, we discuss the future of blockchain in pension systems, providing recommendations for further research and development to refine audit mechanisms and enhance system security.
