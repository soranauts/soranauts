# Blockchain for Empowering Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs): Examples from Industry

## Abstract

Central banks around the world have been looking at using blockchain technology to power the next generation of the global financial system. Whereas legacy payment systems rely on sending messages between financial institutions, with complex clearing and settlement phases to actually deliver payments, central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) promise to deliver money instantly, by directly updating the central bank?s ledger. By using blockchain technology with self-custodied private keys, something akin to a digital bearer asset can be realized. Using the Hyperledger Iroha open source blockchain technology, we worked with the National Bank of Cambodia to implement a digital payment system built around a central bank digital currency and stablecoin in Cambodia. We then extended this system for the Central Bank of Solomon Islands to bridge to the SORA permissionless blockchain platform via a permissioned bridge. We then extended our platform into a hybrid permissioned/permissionless platform to realize the Hyperledger Iroha 2-powered SORA v3 Hub Chain to create a digital Kina for the Bank of Papua New Guinea. Taken together, these systems show concrete, real-world examples of how blockchain technology can empower digital asset platforms for central banks and can lay the foundation for the next iteration of financial information systems that power the global economy.
