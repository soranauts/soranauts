# Quadratic Voting and Reputation Tokens in Physical DAOs: An Agent-Based Model

## Abstract

This paper investigates governance challenges in physical Decentralised Autonomous Organisations (DAOs), which are decentralised entities that manage both digital treasuries and physical assets. Using No1s1, a self-owned physical DAO, as a case study, we develop an agent-based model to simulate governance dynamics. The model integrates two mechanisms: quadratic voting, which reduces wealth-based voting dominance while capturing preference intensity; and reputation tokens, which track individual contributions over time to establish persistent governance reputation. The study contributes to the design of fairer and more accountable governance systems for physical DAOs and lays the groundwork for their integration into broader, interconnected governance networks, where reputation and governance health signals may serve as key indicators of reliability and trustworthiness.
