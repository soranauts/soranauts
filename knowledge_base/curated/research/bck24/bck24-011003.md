# FoodPrint: A Blockchain-Enabled Farm-to-Fork Traceability Platform for Local Food Supply Chains

## Abstract

Consumer need for information on food origin and quality, an increase in food scandals, tightening government regulations and trade requirements are key drivers for the increasing food supply chain (FSC) transparency and traceability requirements. Blockchain technology has the potential to enhance transparency and traceability in food supply chains (FSCs), owing to its ability to securely store data in a decentralised manner, whilst providing a single source of truth. However, majority of FSC systems are centralised and opaque. To remedy this and demonstrate blockchain-enabled transparency and traceability, we design and implement FoodPrint, a blockchain-enabled farm-to-fork traceability platform for use in local food supply chains. FoodPrint is used to capture data on the harvest, transportation and storage of produce; and provide producers with blockchain-based production reports for their history of production. Producers and local cooperatives access the FoodPrint platform via a WhatsApp chatbot and a web interface respectively. Consumers scan supplier-produce specific QR codes to view the provenance of weekly produce on the web interface. FoodPrint provides tamper-evident traceability of produce from local producers, provides them with blockchain-based production history reports to bolster their credibility and potentially unlock access to markets and financing, and ensures they are digitally included.
