# Developing Human Resources Capable of Using Blockchain Technology

## Abstract

There is a significant shortage of human resources capable of utilizing blockchain technology. It has been believed that acquiring a high level of mathematics and programming skills automatically makes one a superior person capable of utilizing blockchain technology, but the business world has already recognized that this is not necessarily true. Developing a training program for human resources capable of utilizing blockchain technology is an urgent issue in the blockchain community; however, there is no such training program yet. In this article, we introduce a successful case study of a similar training program that can serve as a reference. It will be helpful for designing a training program for human resources capable of utilizing blockchain technology.
