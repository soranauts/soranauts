# Stochastic Modeling of UTXOs in Bitcoin Graphs

## Abstract

This study focuses on the dynamics of unspent transaction outputs (UTXOs) in the Bitcoin graph, and presents a novel stochastic model employing a coagulation-fragmentation process with immigration. We enhance the model?s fidelity by integrating the notion of age, a crucial factor, particularly in UTXO simulation.We perform some simulations incorporating these key elements, offering valuable insights into the behavior of UTXOs within the Bitcoin network.
