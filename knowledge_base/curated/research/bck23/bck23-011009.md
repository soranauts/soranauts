# Investigating the Evolution of Bitcoin Markets: An Analysis From the Perspective of the USD/EUR Foreign Exchange Spot Market

## Abstract

An alternative approach to examine the progression of bitcoin markets involves assessing currency values, particularly those of the U.S. dollar and euro. This study investigates the dynamics of the deviation rate between the USD/EUR bitcoin exchange rate and the USD/EUR FX spot, revealing the identification of three potential endogenous breaks through the Threshold Autoregressive model and Bai & Perron tests. Employing ARIMA models to potential partitions, we suggest delineating the bitcoin market evolution into three distinct regimes.
