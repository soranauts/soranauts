# The Role of Money

## Abstract

Today, the monetary system itself is undergoing a major transformation in line with rapid advances in information technology. On the one hand, the rapid deterioration of the current monetary system cannot be overlooked. However, since the Second World War, until the advent of crypto currency, economics has rapidly lost interest in the role of money, especially to discuss the essential property of banking system. This paper starts with the Chicago Plan on the full reserve ratio system proposed by mainstream economics in the first half of the last century, followed by the views of Frederick Soddy, who endorsed the same plan but with different content. His view of economic systems and wealth is rooted in the conservation laws of matter and energy. This allowed him to recognise the difference between virtual assets based on bank loans and real wealth. This observation will suggest the chaos generated by the banks devoting to create the asymmetric revenue generation. Finally, we refer to the distributive ledger system as well as the contribution from the evolution of quantum computer.
