# Empowering Patients in the U.S. Health System by Using Blockchain Technology

## Abstract

This paper describes the challenges of managing patient identity in a fragmented health data system in the United States. These data are currently stored in organizational databases because of which patients have limited access and control over their personal health information. Blockchain technology has inherent features as a distributed ledger technology, to help create verifiable identity credentials that can be stored and managed by patients on their personal devices and shared externally in a secure and efficient way. We describe a blockchain-based identity management system that allows patients? control over their verifiable credentials and how the system helps in reducing the administrative burden on organizations and patients to show proof of identity at each encounter in the health system.
