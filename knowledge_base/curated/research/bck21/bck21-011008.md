# Structural Reduction of Hypergraphs Based on Topology

## Abstract

Hypergraphs are a generalized version of graphs and their edges can have multiple ends. They appear in various situations, which include chemical reaction networks as an example. We discuss a method of the structural reduction of hypergraphs, by which the degrees of freedom are eliminated and a smaller hypergraphs are obtained. In application to chemical reaction networks, we find a topological condition on a subnetwork, removal of which preserves the steady-state property of the original reaction network.
