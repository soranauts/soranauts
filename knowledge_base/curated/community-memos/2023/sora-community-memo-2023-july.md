2SORA MEMO
This document accompanies the invoice sent to SORA 
for the services provided by SORAMITSU. 
July 2023 was an incredible month of accomplishments, the 
SORA Integrated Plan continues to be the roadmap for the 
SORA Network development pipeline, and the progress rate 
has steadily advanced. 
This memo will provide a perspective on the activities for 
each track, a description of the development activities 
specific to this month, and the total amount of hours 
presented in invoice number 205040 served by 
SORAMITSU Helvetia to the SORA Community. 

3SORA MEMO
Work in July is spread across the XST asset platform, the Substrate bridge, 
Orderbook implementation and the EVM bridge. The XST asset platform is 
undergoing final fixes and testing ahead of its launch, with fallback mechanisms 
implemented and continuous support with the Band protocol for accurate pricing 
and frontrunning prevention. 
The Substrate bridge is undergoing an audit, for which additional teams were 
onboarded to support. The front end team is currently developing a UI for 
the bridge, while a limit on withdrawals is being implemented as an additional 
security measure.
The multi-EVM bridge has ongoing work on its fee mechanism, while the team is 
also developing the smart contracts required for multiple chains. 
Market limit order implementation for divisible and non divisible assets (both 
fungible and non fungible), as well as the implementation of a user interface has 
been the current focus of the Orderbook feature. 
Other activities include the preparation for the development of the Kensetsu 
platform, research into Ink! Smart contract support and further quality assurance 
improvements to the underlying SORA infrastructure as well as support for the 
1.11.0 and 1.11.1 runtime upgrades. 
Finally, technical documentation to support the aforementioned development and 
to incentivise dApp development on the SORA network is being integrated into the 
wiki, specifically on the Substrate and multi-EVM bridges and their dependencies, 
the XST platform, Orderbook and specialised governance. 
On the mobile side, three releases were worked on this month, the first, 3.1.0 
included support for SORA card signups and SORA onramp, the second, 3.2.0 
which included UX improvements and work on Google Authentication, and 
increased language support, and finally, 3.3.0, which included support for 
SORA liquidity pools and assets management, as well as UI/UX improvements 
and quality assurance.
