# Community Memo – SORA Community Memo 2025 July

January 2025
A Memo to the 
SORA Community
July 2025

SORA COMMUNITY MEMO / 2
This document accompanies the invoice 
sent to SORA for the services provided 
by SORAMITSU. 
July 2025 was an incredible month of accomplishments. 
The SORA Integrated Plan remains the roadmap for 
SORA network development, and progress has been 
steadily advancing. 
This memo will provide a perspective on the activities for 
each track, a description of the development activities 
specific to this month, and the total number of hours 
served to the SORA community, as presented in invoice 
number 900130.

SORA COMMUNITY MEMO / 3
This month’s work focuses on SORA v2 maintenance 
and SORA v3 development, as well as SORA Wallet work.
Blockchain
Activities carried out included regular synchronization with builders and 
ecosystem stakeholders to ensure project alignment and information flow. 
Routine maintenance of the blockchain codebase was performed, including 
bug fixing and technical improvements to enhance stability, performance, and 
long-term sustainability of the system.
The team has been actively brainstorming, strategizing, and planning for 
SORA v3, while simultaneously progressing with the Iroha 2 Hubchain. Phase 1 
of the Hubchain is currently being merged, and work on implementing Phase 2 
is already underway, ensuring steady momentum across both initiatives.
1.1 SORA Backend
• Builders:
• Regular synchronisation with builders and ecosystem stakeholders;
• Working with government actors to develop solutions to support the economic 
growth of developing countries:
a. Project Management, Design, and Development activities;
b. Continuous communication and synchronization with stakeholders;
c. Development of back-end.
• Others:
• Codebase routine maintenance and fixing;
• Project management activities (daily meetings, planning, retrospectives);
• Quality assurance and testing activities, as well as the development of 
automated tests;
• Data analysis of the network and ecosystem to drive data-driven decision-making;
• Meeting, brainstorming and planning with builders partners for implementing 
projects on SORA;
• Building, strategizing, and planning for SORA v3.
