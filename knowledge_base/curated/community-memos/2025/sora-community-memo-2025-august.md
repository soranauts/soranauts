SORA COMMUNITY MEMO / 3
This month’s work focuses on SORA v2 maintenance, 
specifically preparing a runtime update and developing 
SORA v3.
Blockchain
The team has been actively engaged in brainstorming, strategizing, and 
planning for SORA v3, laying the foundation for the next phase of the network’s 
evolution. This involves evaluating long-term goals, aligning technical direction, 
and ensuring that future developments address both community needs and 
ecosystem scalability.
Progress continues on the Iroha 2 Hubchain initiative, where Phase 1 (basic 
cross-chain transfers) is in the process of being merged. This milestone marks a 
crucial step in enhancing interoperability and advancing cross-chain capabilities. 
At the same time, Phase 2 (decentralised verification of XC transfers) is being 
implemented, which will introduce further improvements and expand the 
functionality of the Hubchain framework.
In parallel, the Iroha JS library is being updated to keep pace with ecosystem 
advancements. These updates aim to enhance the developer experience, improve 
compatibility, and ensure seamless integration with the evolving infrastructure.
Strong emphasis is also being placed on cross-functional team coordination 
and project development, where collaborative efforts between different domains 
are driving progress. This coordination ensures that technical, operational, and 
strategic goals remain aligned as multiple initiatives move forward simultaneously.
On the blockchain development side, work is underway on the SORA 1.7.0 update. 
This release focuses on key improvements such as redenomination support, which 
enhances token handling, and an eras bug fix that improves network stability. 
Additionally, adjustments to validator rewards fees are being introduced to better 
balance incentives and sustainability. Beyond these targeted enhancements, the 
team continues to provide maintenance and support, ensuring the network’s 
robustness and reliability.
