# Community Memo – SORA Community Memo 2025 April

January 2025
A Memo to the 
SORA Community
April 2025

SORA COMMUNITY MEMO / 2
This document accompanies the invoice 
sent to SORA for the services provided 
by SORAMITSU. 
April 2025 was an incredible month of accomplishments. 
The SORA Integrated Plan remains the roadmap for 
SORA network development, and progress has been 
steadily advancing. 
This memo will provide a perspective on the activities 
for each track, a description of the development activities 
specific to this month, and the total number of hours 
served to the SORA community, as presented in invoice 
number 900124.

SORA COMMUNITY MEMO / 3
This month’s work focuses on the TON bridge and 
SORA v3 development. 
Bridges
The team continued with quality assurance and testing activities across multiple 
bridge technologies, including HASHI, TON, and Substrate. 
These efforts are focused on enhancing interoperability, ensuring security, and 
validating system performance to support robust cross-chain operations within 
the SORA ecosystem.
Builders
Regular synchronization is maintained with builders and ecosystem stakeholders 
to align on priorities, share progress, and coordinate ongoing initiatives. 
A significant focus remains on collaborating with government actors to 
co-develop solutions that foster economic growth in developing countries. 
Comprehensive project management, design, and development activities 
are progressing steadily, supported by consistent communication and 
stakeholder engagement. 
Key milestones include the development of a new website and web application, 
the creation of a reliable back-end and supporting database infrastructure, 
and the continued build-out of a dedicated mobile application to expand 
user accessibility.
1 SORA (Substrate) - 1,039 hrs 

SORA COMMUNITY MEMO / 4
Other
Routine maintenance and fixes have been applied to the codebase to 
ensure operational stability and improve performance. Core infrastructure 
components, including the pricing server, price tools, and network nodes, 
are actively maintained. 
Additionally, the team has prioritized the continuous documentation of SORA’s 
implementation and vision within the wiki, ensuring transparency and clarity 
for contributors and users alike. Data analysis of network activity and ecosystem 
dynamics is ongoing, providing critical insights that inform strategic, data-driven 
decision-making. 
Active collaboration with partner builders includes regular meetings, brainstorming 
sessions, and joint planning efforts to implement impactful projects on the 
SORA platform. Strategic planning has also commenced for SORA v3, with cross-
functional coordination driving the next phase of ecosystem development forward.
