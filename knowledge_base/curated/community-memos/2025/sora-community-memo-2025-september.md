accomplishments. The SORA Integrated Plan remains 
the roadmap for SORA network development, and

SORA COMMUNITY MEMO / 3
This month’s work focuses on SORA v2 maintenance, 
specifically preparing a runtime update and developing 
SORA v3.
Blockchain
The team has been actively engaged in brainstorming, strategizing, and 
planning for SORA v3, laying the foundation for the next phase of the network’s 
evolution. This involves evaluating long-term goals, aligning technical direction, 
and ensuring that future developments address both community needs and 
ecosystem scalability.
The development of SORA v3 (also known as SORA Nexus) and the Iroha 3 
repository is ongoing. This marks a significant step forward in the evolution of the 
SORA ecosystem, establishing a stronger foundation for scalability, performance, 
and interoperability within the network.
The Iroha 3 whitepaper is in progress and will offer detailed insight into the next-
generation architecture and its improvements over Iroha 2. It will outline the 
conceptual framework, new consensus mechanisms, and enhanced modular design 
that will power upcoming blockchain infrastructures. Similarly, the SORA v3 (Nexus) 
whitepaper is in progress; it will provide a comprehensive overview of the network's 
vision and technical direction for its next phase of development.
In parallel, work on the Hubchain Phase 2 is ongoing. The progress made during 
this phase also generates conceptual features and implementation strategies that 
can be reused in future development, particularly in the transition to SORA Nexus 
and Iroha 3.
Looking ahead, the development team is preparing to conduct refinement and 
planning sessions to assess the feasibility of migrating from Iroha 2 to Iroha 3. 
These sessions will focus on mapping dependencies, aligning development 
priorities, and evaluating whether a smooth, structured transition between protocol 
versions is possible. If feasible, this migration could mark the beginning of a new 
era for SORA’s infrastructure — one designed for enhanced stability, extensibility, 
and innovation.

SORA COMMUNITY MEMO / 4
On the blockchain development side, the release 4.7.0 was rolled out. The primary 
change centred on expanding denomination functionality, enabling additional 
adjustments and preparing the network for broader scalability. 
Alongside this, important refactoring work was completed, including setting an 
on-chain election fallback mechanism to enhance governance resilience and 
reducing the staking payout fees to one-tenth of their previous value, improving 
reward distribution efficiency. 
Finally, work on updating the SORA Kusama parachain to address the recent 
Parity migration from the Relay chain to the Asset Hub is in progress. Users who 
are having issues bridging from SORA Kusama to Kusama will see this situation 
resolved when the update is released.
